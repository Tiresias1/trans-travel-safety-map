## Score placement rules of thumb (for subsequent countries)
- 0.90+: European/Americas best-practice + low incidents (CA/NZ/UY/PT/IE candidates)
- 0.80–0.89: strong framework with one notable stressor (friction, incident rise, gap)
- 0.60–0.79: broadly safe, no/weak legal recognition, moderate social conservatism or
  policing gaps (much of LatAm outside worst zones; TWN, PHL candidates)
- 0.40–0.59: mixed/protectionless with real harassment or violence levels; or good
  law + very high violence (regional variance heavy)
- 0.20–0.39: criminalising or morality-law exposure without systematic brutality;
  or protective law on paper amid heavy social/police hostility
- 0.05–0.19: state repression frameworks; criminalised identity/expression; torture
  credible (most Gulf states, IRN-with-executions lower, north Nigeria etc.)
- 0.00–0.04: active killing campaigns / death penalty in practice / regime hunting
  (AFG floor; likely parts of SOM, ISIS-type zones if scoreable)

- Foreign-visitor document friction (X markers, changed markers) is scoreable when
  it produces real entry/detention risk — but weigh it below domestic-law severity.
- Resident-only rights (adoption, employment, transition healthcare infrastructure)
  count only as hostility signals, per PLAN §5.2.
- Countries not anchored here: cite ≥1 anchor by name in the note; place relative
  to two anchors where possible ("between THA and GBR").
