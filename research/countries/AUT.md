# Austria (AUT) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.79** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/security:** Schengen (no internal border for EU travellers); Vienna
  airport for non-EU. No documented trans-specific border-police humiliation (contrast
  France/CDG-Giovanna) and no documented airport-scanner outing pattern. Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** LGR is court-based (no self-ID); Constitutional Court
  ruling (18 Dec 2025, E 1297/2025) affirmed gender identity under the right to private
  life and ordered a registry entry struck for a trans person — a positive. No
  trans-traveler hotel/SIM/banking outing cases documented. [vfgh.gv.at, outrightinternational]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no documented women-only-carriage
  trans incidents; hospital-ward placement not specifically documented. Low axis-C.
- **D Public/social reaction:** FPÖ officials publicly mock gender-diverse people —
  Manfred Haimbuchner (FPÖ leader, Upper Austria) at a 5,000-person party event (May
  2025) mocked gender-diverse people and implied they don't belong in politics (axis-K
  signal raising social hostility). [ilga-europe.org 2025 review]
- **E Violence & predation (real):** March 2025 — authorities uncovered a neo-Nazi
  network using fake dating profiles to lure, ambush, beat, humiliate and film victims
  (≥17 lured, 12 men + 3 women arrested by July; falsely accused victims of pedophilia,
  filmed the attacks). Primarily targeted gay/bi MEN, but the organised dating-app
  ambush pattern exposes trans app users too — axis-E risk for queer travellers using
  apps in Austria. [dw.com, ilga-europe.org 2026 review]
- **F Police:** No specific trans street-police targeting documented; police generally
  professional. General accountability gap (no individual ID badges per Amnesty) —
  not trans-specific. [amnesty.org]
- **G Law:** No cross-dressing statute; no propaganda law; same-sex legal since 1971
  (marriage equality 2017, via Constitutional Court). FPÖ manifesto: constitutional
  "two sexes" definition that would repeal non-binary protections — a threat (axis K,
  not enacted).
- **H Detention/prison (weakness):** Parliamentary answer (May 2025, Justice Minister
  Anna Sporrer): 3 trans people in Austrian detention. Ludwig Boltzmann national report:
  "no specific legal provisions protecting LGBTIQ detainees; general equal-treatment
  principles apply but implementation limited; LGBTIQ housed with general population, no
  standardized guidelines, individual facilities ad-hoc." Real axis-H gap, but minor in
  scale (3 prisoners) and less severe-documented than France's men's-prison/gynecological-
  exam cases or Germany's Liebich-type flip; no reform trajectory noted. [parlament.gv.at,
  gmr.lbg.ac.at]
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful; no documented trans-traveler
  customs/insurance cases.
- **J Family/diaspora:** Not documented for Austria.
- **K State & media climate (negative trajectory):** FPÖ won the 2024 election and is
  in government; manifesto's "two sexes" constitutional pledge; officials publicly mock
  trans/gender-diverse people; "Stop the Right" filed against FPÖ candidates for anti-
  LGBTQ violent fantasies (Graz, June 2026). Several FPÖ leaders + Kickl have targeted
  trans people. This raises the probability/severity of axes D, E, F for a visibly trans
  visitor. [thepinknews.com, stopptdierechten.at, ilga-europe.org]
- **Hate-crime data:** 2025 — 1,571 prejudice-violence victims; SO the 4th-most-common
  target (179 SO-motivated, incl. 161 homophobic); GGG.at: homophobic crimes rose but
  *transfeindliche incidents declined* in 2025. [thelocal.at, ggg.at]

## Visitor-focused summary
Austria is a reduced-risk destination with a notably more conservative climate than its
Western neighbours and a live far-right government hostility signal. The governing FPÖ
mocks gender-diverse people publicly and has pledged a constitutional "two sexes"
definition (not enacted). The most concrete traveler-relevant risk is organised dating-
app predation: a neo-Nazi network was uncovered in 2025 luring and violently ambushing
queer men through fake profiles (15 arrested) — caution warranted on dating apps. There
is no bathroom restriction, no cross-dressing statute, no documented border-police
humiliation, and detention protections are weak-but-minor in scale (3 trans prisoners,
housed with the general population, no specific guidelines).

## Score rationale
0.79 — Reduced Risk, below FRA (0.82)/DEU (0.84), above GBR (0.74) and THA (0.77).
- Below FRA/DEU: the FPÖ is **in government** (axis K — a stronger hostility signal than
  Germany's out-of-government AfD or France's out-of-government RN), with a credible
  constitutional two-sex rollback threat; plus the organised dating-app ambush network
  (axis E). Austria also has no self-ID (resident-facing, weak signal, but adds to the
  conservative-climate picture).
- Above GBR (0.74): no *in-force* rollback — FPÖ's two-sex pledge is a threat, not
  enacted, unlike the UK's biological-sex facilities code; no border-police humiliation;
  trans-specific hate incidents declined in 2025 (UK's climate worsened).
- Above THA (0.77): stronger EU rule of law, lower general crime, no documented trans-
  specific violence spike (Thailand had the Pattaya murder + no hate-crime law).
