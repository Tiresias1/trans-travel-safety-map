# Brunei (BRN) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.07** · Anchor: BOTTOM-SCALE (explicit trans criminalisation)

## Key findings (with sources)
- Syariah Penal Code Order 2013 (SPC), final phase in force 3 Apr 2019: death by
  stoning for "liwat" (anal sex between men) and zina; whipping/amputation for other
  offences. Sultan announced moratorium on the death penalty under SPC (May 2019,
  reaffirmed by law minister) after global outcry — but moratorium ≠ repeal; whipping,
  imprisonment remain. [bbc.com, apnews.com (x2), nytimes.com (403; from summary),
  guardian.com (x2), reuters.com (from summary)]
- **Section 198 SPC: explicit offence of "a man posing as a woman or vice versa"** —
  one of only a handful of codified cross-dressing criminalisations worldwide (with
  Oman, Malaysia's state Sharia codes). Government Q&A (hosted by Norwegian govt)
  confirms: **"The SPC applies to all persons in Brunei Darussalam, including
  tourists"** — travel agents briefed. Section 197 "indecent behaviour" (fine up to
  BND2,000 / 6 months) captures clothing that "tarnishes the image of Islam", etc.
  [regjeringen.no SPC Q&A PDF (403; full text quoted in search result),
  humandignitytrust.org briefing (PDF fail; from summary), hrw.org lgbt_laws map]
- Secular Penal Code s377: same-sex intimacy up to 10 years (pre-2019 law retained).
  [commonslibrary.parliament.uk (403; from summary), ssrn.com]
- Practice: no public enforcement against foreign tourists documented; absolute
  monarchy with heavy surveillance, censorship, no public LGBT organising; LGBT
  Bruneians in fear, community members fleeing (France 24; Time; Zoella Zayce case).
  [france24.com, time.com, adnasia.org (PDF fail; from summary)]
- US State Dept human rights report: police intimidation of LGBTI persons documented.
  [state.gov (PDF fail; from summary)]

## Visitor-focused summary
Brunei's Sharia code contains the world's most explicit visitor-relevant trans
provision: Section 198 criminalises "posing as" another gender, and the government has
confirmed in writing that the code applies to tourists, not only Muslims or residents.
Same-sex intimacy carries a stoning sentence (under moratorium) and whipping is an
active punishment. No prosecution of a foreign tourist under s198 has been reported,
and day-to-day enforcement is quiet rather than predatory — but the legal exposure for
a discovered trans visitor is categorical, with no recognition, no protections, and a
surveillance-heavy state where visibility itself is dangerous.
