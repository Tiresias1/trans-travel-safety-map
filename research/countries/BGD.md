# Bangladesh (BGD) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.24** · Band: High Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** US State Dept Level 3 "Reconsider Travel." 5 hijra/trans women
  arrested at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, Dhaka (Apr 2024 — axis-A/F overlap, arrests
  AT the international airport for "begging/extortion"). [76crimes.com, tripbase.com]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** Hijra legally recognised as third gender (2013/2014 — can change
  gender markers to third gender). BUT: recognition is for the specific hijra socio-cultural
  category, not general trans identity ("not all transgender or intersex people identify as
  Hijra" — OHCHR). No comprehensive LGR law. No anti-discrimination law. [outrightinternational.org,
  ohchr.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** Not specifically documented.
- **D/E Violence (SEVERE — escalating Islamist violence since Aug 2024 political crisis):**
  - **Trans activist Shila brutally murdered** (Feb 2025 — police suspect extremist
    involvement; "gruesome murder"; Shila was "known for her relentless fight for transgender
    and third-gender rights"). A directly trans-specific killing of an activist.
  - JMBF 2024 report: **260 anti-LGBTQI+ incidents** (murder, arson, blackmail); "attacks on
    queer Bangladeshis have skyrocketed."
  - Mob attack on trans safe house: "dozens of men stormed the gate" — Babuni survived:
    "I escaped only with my life" (Global Press Journal: "No Longer Safe: Extremism Upends
    Trans Lives in Bangladesh").
  - Post-Hasina political crisis (Aug 2024): Islamist fundamentalist groups "intensified
    violent and inhumane acts"; LGBTQ community "under unprecedented threat"; "forced into
    hiding" (Washington Blade Aug 2024/2025). "Interim government failed to protect" (76crimes
    Apr 2026).
  - Conversion therapy reportedly "widespread practice" (EUA).
  [76crimes.com ×3, globalpressjournal.com, washingtonblade.com ×2, europa.eu, frontiersin.org]
- **F Police (documented extortion):** "Dozens of hijras detained in arbitrary arrests across
  Bangladesh following refusal to pay extortion money to police" (76crimes Oct 2025). s377 used
  as "pretext to harass people they thought were LGBTIQ" (UK CPIN). 5 hijra arrested at Dhaka
  airport (Apr 2024). [76crimes.com, gov.uk CPIN]
- **G Law:** Penal Code s377: "intercourse against the order of nature" — **life
  imprisonment** (colonial-era, 1860; "rarely enforced" but used for harassment/extortion).
  Hijra third-gender recognition (2013/2014). No anti-discrimination law; no same-sex marriage.
  [outrightinternational.org, gov.uk CPIN]
- **H Detention/prison:** Arrests documented (5 hijra at airport; dozens in arbitrary arrests);
  no prison-placement case beyond arrests.
- **I Health/medication:** No documented HRT customs issues, but the anti-LGBTQ climate +
  police extortion make medication carry-in hazardous.
- **K State & media climate (Islamist, escalating, political crisis):** Aug 2024 political
  crisis (Hasina ousted; interim Yunus government): Islamist fundamentalist groups intensified
  attacks; "unprecedented threat"; "forced into hiding"; "No Longer Safe"; "skyrocketed";
  260 incidents. BUT: hijra third-gender recognition (positive); civil society active (JMBF,
  Inclusive Bangladesh, Pathchola/GATE); academic discourse on trans-inclusive Bangladesh.

## Score rationale
0.24 — High Risk, marginally below PAK (0.26)/IDN (0.25), above NGA (0.19), below KEN (0.38).
- Below PAK (0.26): Bangladesh has escalating post-2024-political-crisis Islamist violence
  ("unprecedented threat"; "forced into hiding"; "skyrocketed"; 260 incidents incl. murder,
  arson, blackmail; Shila murder; mob attack on safe house) that exceeds Pakistan's profile
  (3 trans killed in Karachi + 267 violence cases in KP, but Pakistan's violence is
  more chronic than the acute post-crisis escalation in Bangladesh). Bangladesh has s377
  life-imprisonment (Pakistan's Zina is unclear, no known cases). Bangladesh's airport arrests
  (5 hijra at Dhaka international airport, axis-A/F) are visitor-adjacent.
- Below IDN (0.25): Bangladesh's 260 incidents + Shila murder + "forced into hiding" + mob
  attacks on safe houses + police extortion (dozens arrested for refusing to pay) are more
  numerous/systematic than Indonesia's documented cases (Rodrigo + vigilante attacks + 75-
  person raid). BUT Indonesia has the new national penal code criminalising same-sex (Jan 2026)
  + Aceh Sharia caning (applies to tourists) + presidential "security threat" + Rodrigo's
  trans-traveler death — more directly visitor-targeting. Bangladesh's escalating violence is
  more against local communities.
- Above NGA (0.19): Bangladesh has hijra third-gender recognition (2013/2014, a genuine legal
  positive Nigeria lacks); s377 "rarely enforced" (Nigeria's SSMPA is actively enforced +
  Sharia death penalty); no UK travel advisory (Nigeria has one); no trans-specific tourist
  killing (Nigeria has Abuja Area Mama). BUT Bangladesh's 260 incidents + Shila murder +
  "forced into hiding" + police extortion are very severe.
