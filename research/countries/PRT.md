## Visitor-focused summary
A trans visitor in August 2026 still benefits from the 2018 self-ID framework, legally
protected bathroom access, a conversion-therapy ban, and hate-crime law covering gender
identity — with EU-member rule of law and very low violent crime. Two caveats pull the
score below the Iberian peer Spain: (1) a live, legislative repeal of self-ID that
passed first reading in March 2026 (reverting to a medical-gatekept 2011 regime) —
not yet final law, facing a constitutional challenge, but the most advanced anti-trans
legislative process in Western Europe; and (2) hate-crime reports up ~38% in two
successive years, with acknowledged under-reporting. For a visitor these translate to
a real political-hostility signal and elevated (still low-level) harassment risk, not
criminal exposure or physical danger.
