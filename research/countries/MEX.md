## Visitor-focused summary
Mexico combines strong formal rights in much of the country (state-level self-ID in
22 states, transfemicide laws in CDMX/Nayarit, marriage equality) with one of the
world's worst records of violence against trans people — concentrated among local
trans women (often sex workers/activists) and migrants in transit, frequently with
organised-crime involvement and near-total impunity. For a visitor staying in tourist
and central urban areas, the trans-specific practical risk is mostly harassment;
general crime is the dominant hazard. Tourist zones are heavily protected, and Mexico
City is among the region's most progressive capitals.
