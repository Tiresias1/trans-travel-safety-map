# Slovenia (SVN) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.78** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/documents:** Schengen (EES); no trans-specific border friction; foreign
  X markers accepted. Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (improving, depathologised):** LGR moving to a depathologised
  model — F&M Global Barometer records **no physiological-alteration requirement and no
  psychiatric-diagnosis requirement** for LGR; Advocate of the Principle of Equality (2022)
  recommended transparent, fast, accessible, self-determination-based, depathologised
  procedures. **Ministry of Interior (Oct 2022) abolished local jurisdiction** in the
  gender-change procedure — now any administrative unit; "transgender persons no longer
  exposed to involuntary disclosure in the local environment" (a real axis-B outing-via-
  bureaucracy reduction). Not yet full administrative self-ID per ILGA recommendation.
  Resident-facing mostly; Schengen home-documents recognised for visitors.
  [fandmglobalbarometers.org, gov.si, outrightinternational.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no cross-dressing statute. Low axis-C.
- **D Public/social reaction:** SDM (Slovenian Democratic Youth) "Two Sexes, One Truth"
  campaign opposing LGR reform, equating gender theory with "psychological abuse" /
  "indoctrination of children" — far-right rhetoric but a youth wing, not the government.
  ENACT (Mirovni institut): underreporting of anti-LGBTQ hate crimes, lack of trust in
  police. US State Dept 2023: credible reports of violence/threats targeting LGBTIQ
  persons (but constitution prohibits torture, no impunity in security forces). No
  documented mob-violence-against-trans, trans-tourist-rape, or dating-app-ambush (unlike
  Greece/Italy/Czechia). [ilga-europe.org 2024, mirovni-institut.si, state.gov]
- **E Violence & predation:** Lower-severity than CEE peers; no organised dating-app-ambush
  network documented.
- **F Police:** No documented trans-specific police-abuse ECtHR case (contrast Greece
  Koutra / Italy Milan). ENACT flags underreporting/trust gap; need for police training.
  OSAC/State Dept: civilian control of security forces effective, impunity not a problem.
  Better axis-F than Greece/Italy.
- **G Law (strong, improving):** No criminalisation; comprehensive PADA (2016)
  anti-discrimination covering SO/GI/GEx in public and private life; independent Advocate
  of the Principle of Equality; marriage equality (2022, constitutional court ruling —
  first post-Yugoslav state); Constitutional Court protective (forced LGR reform +
  marriage equality). F&M: freedom from arbitrary arrest; legal recognition of gender
  identity. [outrightinternational.org, fandmglobalbarometers.org]
- **H Detention/prison (low-confidence — thin evidence):** No Ludwig Boltzmann / CPT /
  ECtHR trans-detainee case surfaced; no documented foreign-trans-detention case; State
  Dept reports no impunity in security forces. Assume EU-typical biology/registry default
  but **flag: axis-H evidence is thin — partly source scarcity (small country), not
  confirmed safety.**
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (improving, moderate):** No far-right in government (contrast
  Austria FPÖ / Hungary Orbán-then-Tisza); progressive legal trajectory (constitutional
  court rulings, PADA, marriage equality, depathologised LGR); "one of the most progressive
  in CEE" (Outright). SDM rhetoric is the main negative.

## Visitor-focused summary
Slovenia is among Central/Eastern Europe's most progressive destinations for trans
visitors, with a cleaner documented profile than its neighbours: depathologised legal
gender recognition (no surgery/diagnosis requirement), a local-jurisdiction reform that
reduces involuntary outing via bureaucracy, comprehensive anti-discrimination law, and
marriage equality — all behind a protective Constitutional Court, with no far-right in
government. No documented severe traveler-axis incidents surfaced (no mob violence, trans-
tourist rape, police-abuse ECtHR case, or foreign-trans-detention case — unlike Greece,
Italy, Czechia, Poland). The main caveats are SDM "Two Sexes, One Truth" far-right
rhetoric, an underreporting/trust gap in hate-crime reporting, and thinner detention-
placement evidence (partly source scarcity for a small country).
