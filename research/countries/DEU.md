## Traveler-outing axis findings (§5.2)
1. **Border & documents (axis 1):** No documented trans-specific border-police
   humiliation case (contrast France/CDG-Giovanna). Bundespolizei professional; within
   Schengen no border for most EU travellers. No X-marker entry issues for visitors.
   Low axis-1 risk.
2. **Public presence & outing (axis 2):** Far-right street activity is the standout
   concern — Amadeu Antonio Foundation: ~110 of 245 Christopher Street Day (Pride)
   events in 2025 disrupted or attacked, 53 linked to organised far-right networks.
   A visitor attending Pride or visibly trans in eastern Länder faces elevated
   harassment risk; baseline tourist areas lower. Rising right-wing hostility.
   [ilga-europe.org Annual Review 2026]
3. **Bathrooms & facilities (axis 3):** No legal restriction. Venue-level friction
   only (German Sauna-Bund guideline restricting women's sauna by "primary sex
   characteristics" — controversial, non-binding; Berlin practice relaxed). Low
   axis-3 risk.
4. **Police interaction (axis 4):** No systematic trans-targeting of visitors by
   street police reported.
5. **Arrest & detention / prison placement (axis 5) — real weakness, now credited:**
   Länder prison law anchors placement on the civil-registry gender entry; under SBGG
   §6(1) the *self-chosen* entry is grundsätzlich maßgeblich, BUT Länder laws allow
   *Ausnahmen* placing people contrary to self-declared gender. KFN (criminology
   institute): placement by gender identity "only in exceptional cases" historically.
   Reform trajectory (better than France): Berlin §11 StVollzG (2021) allows
   self-disclosed-gender placement even without registry change; Rheinland-Pfalz
   §17 LJVollzG (in force 1 Mar 2026); Bavaria, Saxony, Hamburg, Bremen reformed —
   majority of Länder updated. Inconsistent in practice: the **Liebich** case
   (neo-Nazi who self-ID'd to female) assigned to Chemnitz women's prison (Aug 2025),
   then transferred to **men's** prison (Jul 2026) after controversy — shows the
   exceptions are invoked, grey-zone. BVerfG (1996): state must respect individual's
   gender self-determination. Prison staff union: men's prisons "inadequately prepared"
   for trans people; understaffing. [verfassungsblog.de, dw.com (×2), kfn.de, berlin.de,
   bbc.com, france24.com]
6. **Medication/HRT (axis 6):** Carrying personal HRT within Schengen/EU is
   uneventful; SBGG + healthcare generally support continuity. Low axis-6 risk.

## Visitor-focused summary
Low-risk, with two real traveler-axis caveats. (1) Far-right street violence is the
most documented concern: roughly 110 of 245 Pride events were disrupted in 2025, over
half by organised far-right networks — a visitor at Pride, or visibly trans in eastern
Länder, faces elevated harassment. (2) If arrested, prison placement follows the
self-chosen civil-registry entry under recent Länder reforms (better than France, which
still places by legal gender/ID with gynecological-exam "verification"), but
exceptions allow placement contrary to self-declared gender and are invoked
inconsistently (the Liebich women's→men's flip). Bathrooms are unrestricted; no
border-police humiliation documented; HRT carry-in is uneventful.
