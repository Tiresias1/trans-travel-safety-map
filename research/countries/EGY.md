## Visitor-focused summary
Egypt is high-risk for trans visitors — with a directly documented study on the treatment of
queer FOREIGNERS by Egyptian authorities ("Your Kind Is Not Welcome Here," Cairo 52, 12
incidents 2018-2022). Same-sex conduct is not explicitly criminalised, but authorities use
"debauchery" and cybercrime laws (6 months + fine for "violation of social and family values")
to arrest and prosecute — and Cairo 52 reports that courts have moved from de facto to explicit
criminalisation under the cybercrime law. Police hunt LGBT people on dating apps (BBC Grindr
entrapment) and subject arrestees to coerced anal examinations (Bedayaa). Gender-affirming
healthcare is PROHIBITED. The Ministry of Interior is closing a loophole that allowed limited
legal gender recognition. Egypt's UPR (2025): rejected ALL recommendations on SO/GI. The
authorities' goal is described as "not only to criminalize LGBTQ+ identities but also to erase
them from society and public life."

0.22 — High Risk, below KEN (0.38)/TUR (0.47), above UGA (0.17)/NGA (0.19), ~tied with BGD
(0.24)/IDN (0.25).

  police-entrapment-via-dating-apps (Turkey has presidential "Year of Family" + profiling +
  draft law but no foreigner-targeting study + no anal exams + no healthcare prohibition).
- Above UGA (0.17): Egypt has **no death penalty** (Uganda's AHA has death penalty for
  "aggravated"); no "promotion" criminalised (Uganda criminalises advocacy); no 1,000+-
  violations/mobs-scale (Uganda's violence is more societal+mobs). Egypt's violence is more
  POLICE/institutional (entrapment, anal exams) while Uganda's is more SOCIETAL+STATE (mobs,

- Above NGA (0.19): Egypt has **no death penalty** (Nigeria has Sharia death penalty in 12
  states); no documented trans-specific killing (Nigeria has Abuja Area Mama). BUT Egypt has
  "Your Kind Is Not Welcome Here" foreigner-targeting + healthcare-prohibited + coerced-anal-
  exams + police-entrapment + LGR-being-closed — systematic institutional targeting Nigeria
  lacks at the foreigner-specific level. Egypt's 6-months/fine (morality/cybercrime) is lower-
  penalty than Nigeria's SSMPA 14-years + Sharia-death, but the **practical risk** (police
  entrapment + foreigner-targeting + anal exams + healthcare prohibition) is severe.
- The probability×severity frame: Egypt = high-probability of police targeting (dating-app
  entrapment + "Your Kind Is Not Welcome Here" + morality-law enforcement) + moderate-severity
  (6 months + fine + anal exams + healthcare-prohibited — bad but not death/life-imprisonment/
  murder). This places it in the High Risk band, below Kenya (lower-probability despite 14-year
  criminalisation, because Kenya's court rulings + "thousands visit" reduce practical risk).
