# Iran (IRN) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.09** · Band: Do Not Travel

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** Intelligence-service surveillance; security forces "harassed,
  arrested, and detained individuals they suspected or perceived as being LGBTQI+" (US State
  Dept). No trans-specific border-friction case documented, but theocratic system monitors
  visitors. [iranprimer.usip.org]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (UNIQUE — LGR contingent on surgery):** Transsexuality legally
  recognised as a medical condition (Khomeini fatwa); LGR + state-funded gender-affirming
  surgery available. BUT recognition is **contingent upon undergoing sex reassignment
  surgery** (HRANA 2023); the judiciary (LMO/pezeshk-ghanoni; Judge Abbas Ghaderi notorious
  for harsh/invasive requirements) controls the process. Post-op trans people can get
  matching documents; pre-op/non-binary cannot. **Forced transitions documented** ("40
  years of forcing gay people to choose between transitioning and death," LGBTQ Nation).
  [outrightinternational.org, businessinsider.com, lgbtqnation.com, ecoi.net]
- **C Gendered spaces:** Tehran City Council (Mar 2024) **told transgender people to
  avoid "busy" areas** of the capital — official spatial segregation. Compulsory veiling
  (hijab) for women creates complex complications for trans women (pre-op = male compulsory
  rules + female clothing restrictions). [rferl.org, tanide.org]
- **D/E Violence & persecution (systematic, state-led):** 6Rang survey: 25 LGBTIQ+ fled
  "systematic persecution." State + non-state violence. Hate speech weaponized as state
  repression (6Rang Feb 2025). "Reparative" therapies to "cure" homosexuality. Grossly
  substandard sex reassignment surgeries without consent (6Rang CRPD). US State Dept:
  security forces targeted perceived LGBTQI+. [6rang.org ×2, amnesty.org,
  upr-info.org]
- **F Police/intelligence (SEVERE — documented rape in custody):** HRANA: **Ghazal
  Marzban, a trans woman incarcerated in Evin Prison, was RAPED at a Ministry of
  Intelligence detention facility** (arrested May 2023; threatened into silence). A
  directly documented case of sexual violence against a trans person in state custody.
  [en-hrana.org]
- **G Law (death penalty for same-sex + forced transition for trans):** Islamic Penal
  Code: same-sex conduct criminalised — punishments from flogging to **death penalty**. No
  anti-discrimination laws; no recognition of LGBT+ couples. Transsexuality recognised
  only if medicalised (surgery); non-op/non-binary face immorality/indecency restrictions
  (imprisonment, fines, lashes). [humandignitytrust.org, gov.uk CPIN, outrightinternational.org]
- **H Detention/prison (severe):** Evin Prison; Ministry of Intelligence detention
  facilities (Ghazal Marzban rape). Forced transition surgeries without consent (6Rang CRPD).
  [en-hrana.org, 6rang.org]
- **I Health/medication (UNIQUE POSITIVE — state-funded but coercive):** State FUNDS
  gender-affirming surgery; trans medical tourism "booming" (LGBTQ Nation Oct 2025).
  BUT: the system is coercive (forced transitions, grossly substandard surgeries without
  consent, judiciary control); no legal protections for non-op trans or non-binary.
  [lgbtqnation.com, businessinsider.com, 6rang.org]
- **K State & media climate (theocratic, systematic persecution):** Islamic Republic;
  death penalty for same-sex; hate speech weaponized; censorship bans LGBTIQ media;
  Tehran City Council segregation order; "systematic persecution" (6Rang). BUT:
  transsexuality legally recognised as medical condition (unique in the region) — a
  bifurcated system that tolerates POST-OP trans people while criminalising everything else.

## Visitor-focused summary
Iran is one of the world's most severe environments — but with a unique bifurcation.
Same-sex conduct carries the **death penalty**, and a trans woman was **raped in Ministry
of Intelligence custody** (Ghazal Marzban, Evin Prison, 2023). The Tehran City Council
ordered trans people to avoid "busy" areas. Trans people who have NOT had surgery face
immorality/indecency provisions (imprisonment, fines, lashes) and documented **forced
transition** pressure. BUT: transsexuality IS legally recognised (Khomeini fatwa) and
surgery is state-funded — making Iran one of the few countries in the region with ANY
legal recognition of trans identity, and a trans medical-tourism hub. A post-op trans
visitor with matching documents is in a structurally different (and better) position
than a pre-op/non-op visitor, a non-binary person, or anyone perceived as same-sex-
active — who face the death penalty, state-custody rape, and forced-transition coercion.

## Score rationale
0.09 — Do Not Travel, below RUS (0.12), above SAU (0.05), below MYS (0.28).
- Below RUS (0.12): Iran has the **death penalty** for same-sex (Russia has no death
  penalty for trans/same-sex); documented **state-custody rape of a trans woman**
  (Russia has persecution but no documented state-custody-rape); **forced transitions**
  (Russia has transition banned, not forced); Tehran City Council **official segregation
  order**. These are more severe than Russia's transition-ban + extremist-designation.
- Above SAU (0.05): Iran has **legal recognition of post-op transsexuality + state-funded
  surgery + trans medical tourism hub** — a genuine positive axis-B/I element that Saudi
  completely lacks (Saudi: no recognition, no surgery funding, morality policing, custodial
  death). The bifurcated system means post-op trans visitors are in a structurally better
  position in Iran than in Saudi Arabia.
- The score reflects the extreme severity (death penalty + state-custody rape + forced
  transitions + segregation) offset by the unique legal-recognition-of-post-op-trans
  element. For a post-op trans visitor with matching documents, Iran is marginally safer
  than Saudi; for everyone else, it is among the world's most dangerous environments.
