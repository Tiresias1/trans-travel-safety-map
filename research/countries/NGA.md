## Visitor-focused summary
Nigeria is among the most severe environments for trans visitors — with a death penalty
under Sharia in 12 northern states, national criminalisation of same-sex conduct (SSMPA,
14 years, applies to tourists), a trans-specific killing with impunity (Abuja Area Mama
found beaten on a highway; no arrest six months later), documented police brutality against
people "looking gay" ("weaponised" law), a UK Foreign Office travel advisory warning of arrest,
violence, and death-penalty risks, and dating-app "kito" entrapment gangs. A proposed cross-
dressing ban (not yet passed) would criminalise gender expression. Trans identity is NOT
explicitly criminalized nationally (the SSMPA targets same-sex conduct), and the killing and
violence concentrate against local communities rather than tourists. But the "weaponised"
application of the SSMPA, the Sharia death penalty, the trans-specific killing with impunity,
and the UK advisory make this a Do Not Travel environment for trans visitors.

0.19 — Do Not Travel, below MYS (0.28), above SAU (0.05)/IRN (0.09), ~tied with RUS (0.12)
marginally above.
- Below MYS (0.28): Nigeria has the **death penalty** under Sharia (Malaysia has no death
  penalty for same-sex); national SSMPA (14 years, applies to all incl. tourists; Malaysia's
  s377A/B is 20 years but Sharia applies to Muslims only); **trans-specific killing with
  impunity** (Abuja Area Mama; Malaysia has documented trans killings but with some
  enforcement); UK travel advisory (Malaysia doesn't have a UK trans advisory); proposed
  cross-dressing ban (Malaysia already criminalises cross-dressing via Sharia). BUT Nigeria
  doesn't criminalise trans IDENTITY nationally (SSMPA is same-sex conduct) and has no
  systematic tourist-border-targeting (unlike UAE).
- Above SAU (0.05)/IRN (0.09): trans identity not explicitly criminalized nationally (SSMPA
  is same-sex conduct; Saudi criminalises gender expression per se); no documented state-
  custody rape (unlike Iran's Ghazal Marzban); no systematic tourist-border-targeting (unlike
  UAE's X-ray/deportation); killing targets local communities, not tourists. But the death
  penalty (Sharia) + SSMPA (national) + trans killing + police brutality + UK advisory are
  extreme.
- ~marginally above RUS (0.12): Russia criminalises transition + extremist-designation +
  persecution-assessment-for-trans; Nigeria criminalises same-sex conduct (14 years) + Sharia
  death penalty but does NOT criminalise trans IDENTITY. Russia's framework is more
  systematically anti-TRANS (transition banned, extremist); Nigeria's is more anti-GAY (SSMPA)
  with anti-trans effects via "weaponised" application + Sharia. Trans identity per se is not
  criminal in Nigeria (unlike Russia's transition ban).
