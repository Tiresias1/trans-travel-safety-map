## Visitor-focused summary
Italy is reduced-risk with several real, documented traveler-axis risks and a
long-running government hostility climate. The most severe traveler-specific finding:
the **rape of two young trans tourists**, with convictions confirmed on appeal in Naples
in April 2026. There are organised dating-app entrapment gangs (Grindr-lure robberies),
a documented case of local-police beating a trans woman in Milan (officer convicted), and
prison placement of trans women in male facilities by biology (mitigated by a
specialized section at Rebibbia). The Meloni government has openly hostile rhetoric
toward LGBT people ("LGBT lobby," "gender ideology," surrogacy-abroad criminalization)
and Italy is rated among the least progressive Western European countries on LGBT rights.
Major tourist cities (Rome, Milan, Florence) have established queer scenes and are
generally safe day-to-day; bathrooms are unrestricted.
