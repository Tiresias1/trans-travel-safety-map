# Sweden (SWE) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.84** · Band: Low Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** Schengen/EU; no trans-specific border friction; foreign X accepted.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (improved July 2025, still medical-gatekept):** New Legal Gender
  Recognition Act (1 Jul 2025) simplifies the procedure and separates LGR from surgery
  requirements (welcome reform of the 1972 law — Sweden was the **last Nordic to reform**).
  BUT TGEU: "falls short of full self-determination model" — still requires medical
  examination + decision by the National Board of Health and Welfare. So medical-gatekept,
  not self-ID (weaker than Denmark's 2014 / Norway's 2016). [tgeu.org, loc.gov,
  equalitylaw.eu]
- **C Gendered spaces (documented friction):** **Medley (swimming-pool company) national
  guidelines restrict trans persons' changing-room access** — trans people can't choose
  their changing room; lawyers say it may violate discrimination law (SVT Nyheter). A
  documented axis-C venue-level friction Denmark/Norway lack. [svt.se]
- **D/E:** Brå reports ~6,700 LGBTQI hate-crime reports (Jan 2025 suspects analysis);
  government mapping digital threats against LGBTQI individuals (FOI institute). LGBTQI+
  safety-perception research in transit environments (KTH). No documented severe incidents
  (no tourist-rape, no mob-violence, no Oslo-Pride-equivalent). [bra.se, swedenherald.com,
  sakraplatser.abe.kth.se]
- **F Police:** No documented police-brutality case; institutional response (Brå statistics,
  hate-crime monitoring).
- **G Law:** Anti-discrimination (Discrimination Act 2009, covers GI/GEx); hate-crime law
  covers SO/GI; constitutional duty to combat discrimination; no criminalisation; no
  bathroom law.
- **H Detention/prison:** No documented Supreme Court/ECtHR trans-prisoner case surfaced
  (thin evidence). CPT general standards apply.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (progressive, stable):** Government strategy on LGBTIQ equality;
  no rollback movement; BUT anti-gender discourse present (Medley controversy, digital
  threats). Less acute than Norway's Oslo-shooting context or Denmark's Bjerre-op-ed.

## Score rationale
0.84 — below NOR (0.86)/DNK (0.87), ~tied with DEU (0.84).
- Below NOR/DNK: weaker LGR (medical-gatekept, only reformed Jul 2025; Denmark/Norway have
  self-ID since 2014/2016) + Medley swimming-pool axis-C friction (documented venue-level
  restriction, legally contested) + larger hate-crime reporting volume (~6,700 vs Norway's
  rising-but-lower). 
- ~tied with DEU (0.84): similar profile — medical-gatekept-but-improving LGR + some axis-C
  friction (Sweden's Medley / Germany's sauna-Bund) + hate-crime reporting + institutional
  response + no rollback. Sweden has no SBGG-equivalent self-ID (Germany does), but Sweden's
  reform is more recent (Jul 2025) and no repeal threat.

# Finland (FIN) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.85** · Band: Low Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** Schengen/EU; no trans-specific border friction; foreign X accepted.
  Finland issued US travel advisory for trans citizens (Mar 2025). [theguardian.com]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (2023 reform, medical-gatekept):** LGR reform (2023) removed the
  sterilisation requirement — Finland was the **last EU country to do so**. Still requires a
  medical statement (not self-ID; better than the old sterilisation law but medical-gatekept).
  [ilga-europe.org 2026, investigate-europe.eu]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no Medley-equivalent friction documented.
- **D/E:** Police University College: **record hate crimes in 2024 (1,808 suspected cases,
  +13% since 2023)** — majority racist but SO also identified among motives. [ilga-europe.org
  2026, polamk.fi]
- **F Police:** Police University College hate-crime reporting (institutional capacity); no
  documented police-brutality case.
- **G Law:** No criminalisation; anti-discrimination law (covers GI); hate-crime law (SO/GI);
  marriage equality (2017, via Parliament — after earlier rejection). No bathroom law.
- **H Detention/prison:** Thin evidence; no documented trans-prisoner case. CPT general
  standards apply.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (progressive, stable):** No rollback movement; government
  strategy; BUT record-high hate crimes (rising trajectory).

## Score rationale (Finland)
0.85 — between SWE (0.84) and NOR (0.86).
- Above SWE (0.84): earlier LGR reform (2023 vs Sweden's Jul 2025) + no Medley-equivalent
  axis-C friction + slightly cleaner profile.
- Below NOR (0.86): Finland's LGR still medical-gatekept (post-2023 reform, not self-ID like
  Norway's 2016) + record-high hate crimes (1,808, +13%) without Norway's Oslo-shooting-level
  acute violence but with a documented rising trajectory.
