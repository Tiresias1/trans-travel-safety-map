# Lithuania (LTU) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.66** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** EU + Schengen; no trans-specific border friction; foreign X accepted.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (LGR deadlock — the most restrictive in the Baltics):** Civil
  Code art. 2.27 grants unmarried adults the right to medically change gender, but **there
  is NO legal framework regulating the procedure** — a **17-year legal limbo**. Constitutional
  Court (16 Sep 2025) **REFUSED to address the gap** (declining jurisdiction / "legislative
  inaction"), perpetuating the injustice; trans people "left in a situation of distressing
  legal uncertainty" (ECtHR/CoE). Resident-facing; no tourist document-outing cases; but
  no effective LGR pathway at all. [lgl.lt ×3, lrt.lt, rm.coe.int]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom law. "Protection of Minors from the Negative Effects of
  Public Information" — propaganda-style law (banned dissemination of info about same-sex
  families to minors; Constitutional Court 2024 ruled that provision unconstitutional, but
  MP Gražulis tabled amendments calling sex-ed "sexualisation"/"sexual exploitation").
- **D Public/social reaction (elevated, conservative):** MP Gražulis called LGBT people
  "degenerated" and "spreading diseases" (immunity revoked, investigated). "Traditional
  family values" supporters disrupt peaceful protests. Seimas Ombudsperson: police
  FAILED to guarantee the LGBT community's right to peaceful assembly. Trans workplace
  harassment "systemic" (open ridicule, isolation, psychological abuse; "eight times openly
  told they don't accept people like me"). ILGA Rainbow Map 36th/49 (24% — NOT a score
  input, weak signal). [ilga-europe.org, lrt.lt, lgl.lt, lygybe.lt]
- **E Violence:** No documented mob-violence/tourist-rape/dating-app-ambush; ENACT:
  hate-crime victim-support gaps. Lower-severity than Serbia/Greece.
- **F Police:** Police failed to guarantee assembly rights (Seimas Ombudsperson); Prosecutor
  terminated investigation into conservative-group disruption of a peaceful protest (2025).
  No trans-specific police-brutality case (unlike Serbia).
- **G Law (stagnating):** No criminalisation; LGR legal in principle but no procedure
  (deadlock). Constitutional Court 2024 positive: anti-same-sex-family propaganda provision
  unconstitutional. LT Pride 2024 drew 15,000. EU/Schengen.
- **H Detention/prison:** Low-confidence (thin evidence); no documented trans-detainee case.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful; healthcare access barriers for
  trans people documented (resident-facing, lygybe.lt).
- **K State & media climate (negative trajectory):** Constitutional Court refusal (Sep 2025)
  perpetuates LGR deadlock — a negative inflection vs Estonia's/Hungary's reform trajectories.
  Conservative "traditional values" political bloc active.

## Score rationale
0.66 — below EST (0.77) and HUN (0.68), above SRB (0.64). Lithuania's LGR is in a 17-year
deadlock the Constitutional Court refused to resolve (Sept 2025 — a negative trajectory,
unlike Hungary's Tisza-improving or Estonia's progressive-action-plan); systemic trans
workplace harassment + Gražulis "degenerated" rhetoric + propaganda-style minors law +
police-assembly failure. Above Serbia (EU/Schengen, no criminalization, no police-brutality
case, no HRT disruptions, LT Pride 15k).
