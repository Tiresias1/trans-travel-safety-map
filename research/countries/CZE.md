## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/documents:** Schengen (no internal border for EU travellers); foreign
  X markers accepted; no trans-specific border friction; no foreign advisories. BUT axis
  A/H overlap — see the trans-foreigner extradition-detention case (axis H).
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** LGR — Constitutional Court (Pl. ÚS 52/23, 24 Apr 2024)
  struck the surgery/sterilisation requirement as a violation of bodily integrity/
  autonomy/dignity; Health Ministry guidelines (26 Jun 2025) implemented it: now no
  surgery, but still requires a transsexual diagnosis by specialist doctors + 6–12 months
  observation. Medical-gatekept (no self-ID), improving. Resident-facing mostly.
  [usoud.cz, reuters.com (401; from summary)]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no cross-dressing statute. Low axis-C.
- **D Public/social reaction:** ILGA 2024 review: 51% of LGBTIQ people targeted by
  insults/assault/threats in the past 5 years; high police-reported hate-crime rates; no
  hate-crime-bill progress that year. SPD party rhetoric ("deviants," "take it away from
  LGBT activists"). All About Czech: Prague/larger cities welcoming, harassment incidents
  occur, higher risk rural/late-night. No documented mob-violence-against-trans or
  trans-tourist-rape (unlike Greece/Italy). [ilga-europe.org 2024, allaboutczech.com]
- **E Violence & predation:** 51%-targeted figure (axis D); no documented organised
  dating-app-ambush network. GOV.UK: pickpocketing; beware fake plainclothes "police"
  asking to see currency/passport (general — but a document-friction scam axis-B risk).
- **F Police:** CPT (April 2024 visit): allegations of ill-treatment by police. The
  trans-foreigner extradition-detention case (axis H) shows police/court mishandling.
  UNHCR LGBTIQ-refugee toolkit + in-ius crime handbook for LGBTQIA+ victims exist
  (institutional support). [rm.coe.int, unhcr.org, in-ius.com]
- **G Law (improving):** No criminalisation; no cross-dressing statute. Hate-crime law
  amendments (2025) developed to cover disability/gender/SO/age/social group (GI via
  "gender"?), unifying to a discriminatory-selection model. Asylum law includes SOGI
  protection. No conversion-therapy ban; no bathroom law. [hatecrime.osce.org, sogica.org]
- **H Detention/prison (weakness, foreign-traveler-specific + Supreme Court–flagged):**
  - **Police mishandling detention of transgender FOREIGNERS for extradition** (Česká
    justice 2023): the Prague Municipal Court and Supreme Administrative Court clashed
    over an HIV-positive trans woman in transition detained for extradition; the higher
    court criticized the Municipal Court for "unnecessary sarcastic" remarks. A directly
    foreign-trans-traveler axis-A/H case. [ceska-justice.cz]
  - **Supreme Court (Nov 2025) demands prison-system rules for trans people** ("Czech
    prisons not prepared for trans people, lack clear rules"); calls on the justice-ministry
    concept to explicitly address trans people's needs (post-2024 Constitutional Court

  - ECtHR T.H. v. Czech Republic (non-binary applicant refused surgery) — LGR case law.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful; medical-gatekept LGR

- **K State & media climate (moderate, improving):** SPD far-right rhetoric ("deviants")

  prison reform (2025). Less extreme than Hungary; less restrictive-low-baseline than
  Poland.

## Visitor-focused summary
Czechia is reduced-risk with an improving trajectory and a major welcoming tourist hub
(Prague), offset by unprepared detention systems and elevated baseline social hostility.
Legal gender recognition no longer requires surgery (Constitutional Court 2024 + Health
Ministry 2025) but still needs a medical diagnosis and 6–12 months' observation —
improving, not self-ID. If detained, the system is unprepared: the Supreme Court (Nov
2025) demanded prison rules for trans people, and there is a documented case of police
mishandling the extradition-detention of an HIV-positive trans foreigner. About half of
LGBTIQ people report being targeted by insults/assault/threats in five years, and the
far-right SPD uses "deviant" rhetoric; Prague and larger cities are welcoming, but
harassment is more likely in rural areas or late at night. Bathrooms are unrestricted and
there is no criminalisation.
