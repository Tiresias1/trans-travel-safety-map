## Visitor-focused summary
Estonia is reduced-risk with a strong progressive trajectory but a social climate that
lags the law. It was the first Baltic and post-Soviet country to legalise same-sex marriage
(2024), has a 2025 national LGBT+ action plan with particular attention to trans people,
joined 16 EU states in urging Hungary to revise its anti-LGBT laws, and held Tallinn Pride
2025. There are no documented severe traveler-axis incidents (no mob violence, trans-tourist
rape, police-brutality, or foreign-trans-detention case). The notable gap: legal gender
recognition remains medical-gatekept (it requires a diagnosis and is "complicated,
arbitrary"; a reform bill stalled), and surveys show Estonians are markedly more avoidant
of public LGBTI visibility than the EU average.
