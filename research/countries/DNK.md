# Denmark (DNK) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.87** · Band: Low Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** Schengen/EU; no trans-specific border friction; foreign X accepted.
  Denmark issued its own US travel advisory for trans citizens (Mar 2025). Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (excellent):** **Self-ID since 2014** (first European country,
  no medical requirements); nonbinary markers available; conversion therapy banned (2023);
  anti-discrimination law covers SO/GI/GEx/sex characteristics since Jan 2022; marriage
  equality (2012, first registered partnerships 1989). Schengen home-documents recognised.
  [outrightinternational.org, wanderingwithpride.org, fandmglobalbarometers.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law. Copenhagen municipal inquiry (Mar
  2025) about trans persons' access to swimming-pool changing rooms — accommodation-
  focused, not restrictive. Low axis-C (like Iceland).
- **D/E Public/social reaction (low, but intensifying discourse):** Danish Institute for
  Human Rights: 2,000-3,000 LGBT+ persons experience hate-motivated violence per year
  (all LGBT+, not trans-specific); "few hate crimes investigated by police." Anti-trans
  discourse intensifying (LGR age-limit debate; Christian sites calling trans groups
  "gender lobbyists" claiming they "took children hostage"); former minister Bjerre op-ed
  presenting trans/intersex people as threatening gender equality (govt reorganised after).
  [humanrights.dk, ilga-europe.org 2024/2025]
- **F Police (under-investigation gap):** Danish Institute for Human Rights: "few hate
  crimes investigated; police not obliged at time of reporting to determine whether" it's
  a hate crime. Under-investigation gap (axis-F weakness); no brutality case documented.
  [humanrights.dk]
- **G Law (strong, stable):** No criminalisation; self-ID (2014); conversion-therapy ban
  (2023); hate-crime law covers SO/GI; anti-discrimination comprehensive (2022). No
  rollback movement. ILGA Rainbow Map #5 (85%) — NOT a score input.
- **H Detention/prison (Supreme Court ruling, axis-H):** **Danish Supreme Court
  (Højesteret, Sep 2024): a trans woman who legally changed gender to female (2015) but
  hasn't had surgery must serve in a MEN'S prison** — not a violation of the ECHR. Also
  **strip-searched by male prison staff** (nøgenvisitation). The 63-year-old rape-convicted
  trans woman's challenge rejected. Same axis-H issue as France/Germany/UK (men's-prison
  placement of trans women despite legal gender change). [berlingske.dk, tv2.dk, dr.dk,
  politiken.dk]
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (stable, moderate backlash):** "Decade of self-ID cools debate"
  (Context/TRF); no rollback movement; BUT anti-trans discourse intensifying (Bjerre op-ed,
  Christian sites, LGR-age-limit debate). Generally progressive consensus, unlike UK/Hungary.

## Score rationale
0.87 — Low Risk, ~tied with IRL (0.87), below BEL/NLD (0.88-0.89), above DEU (0.84).
- Above DEU (0.84): self-ID since 2014 (longer than Germany's 2024 SBGG) + conversion-therapy
  ban + nonbinary markers + no repeal threat (Germany has CDU/CSU review) + no record Pride
  disruptions (Germany: 110/245).
- ~tied with IRL (0.87): Denmark's longer self-ID + conversion ban + nonbinary ≈ Ireland's
  binary-only self-ID + 2024 hate-crime law + hate-speech shelved. Both ~0.87.
- Below BEL/NLD (0.88-0.89): the Supreme Court axis-H men's-prison ruling + strip-search-by-
  male-staff + under-investigation gap + intensifying anti-trans discourse keep Denmark below
  Belgium (which has GI in both hate-crime AND hate-speech criminal law + better enforcement).
