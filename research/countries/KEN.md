# Kenya (KEN) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.38** · Band: High Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** No trans-specific border friction documented; foreign documents
  recognised. BUT same-sex criminalisation applies to all including tourists (s162, 14 years).
  "Thousands of gay travelers visit annually through specialized tour operators" (Out of Office).
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (no LGR law BUT courts ruled constitutional right):** No LGR
  procedure exists — 94% of trans Kenyans refused by civil registries. BUT: **High Court
  (Nairobi, May 2026): affirmed constitutional right to legal gender recognition** for trans/
  gender-diverse Kenyans. **High Court (Eldoret, Aug 2025): S.C. v state — trans woman's
  arrest for "personation" at a hospital ruled unconstitutional; KES 1M (US$7,700) damages;
  government ordered to draft a Transgender Protection Rights Bill.** No anti-discrimination
  law for SO/GI. Intersex legally recognised as third gender. [gate.ngo, khrc.or.ke, inclo.net]
- **C Gendered spaces:** Not specifically documented.
- **D/E Violence (documented, significant):** Edwin Chiloba (gay rights activist) murdered —
  body found in metal box (Jan 2023, Eldoret). Perpetrator sentenced to **50 years** (Dec
  2024 — enforcement works). KHRC "Lives on the Line" report (Sep 2025): 102 evictions,
  43 unlawful arrests, 149 intimate-partner-violence incidents (Oct 2023-Sep 2024).
  "Organised groups who strip them naked out of curiosity" (The Star — mob violence).
  "Hell-hole for queer Kenyans." No documented trans-TOURIST-specific killing.
  [reuters.com, apnews.com, khrc.or.ke, the-star.co.ke]
- **F Police (documented systemic extortion):** CNN (Jun 2024): trans woman arrested in
  Mombasa, **forced to pay $50 bribe**; 25 trans/intersex people confirm police harassment is
  "common" — "the price they have to pay for their freedom and privacy." "BEHIND THE BADGE"
  report (2025): "systemic discrimination and violence" by law enforcement; "profound
  distrust" of police; "violations largely undocumented... perpetuating impunity." KHRC:
  43 unlawful arrests. [cnn.com, washingtonblade.com]
- **G Law (same-sex criminalised BUT courts protective):** Penal Code s162 (unnatural
  offences, **14 years**) + s165 (gross indecency between males) — criminalises same-sex
  conduct (colonial-era, not reviewed since independence 1963). Applies to all incl. tourists.
  BUT: Supreme Court (2023) upheld LGBTQ+ freedom of association; Court of Appeal (2018/2024)
  forced anal testing unconstitutional + compensation; High Court (2025/2026) trans-rights
  landmark rulings. No anti-discrimination law; no LGR implementation yet. [gov.uk CPIN,
  outrightinternational.org]
- **H Detention/prison:** S.C. arrest "personation" case (High Court ruled unconstitutional,
  Aug 2025 — positive axis-H response). No documented prison-placement case beyond the arrest.
- **I Health/medication:** Healthcare access limited (KHRC: evictions affecting healthcare
  access). No documented HRT customs issues.
- **K State & media climate (conservative, but courts + civil society active):** "Largely
  conservative society" (Intrepid); President Ruto called gay rights a "nonissue."
  BUT: courts have been protective (Supreme Court freedom of association; landmark trans-rights
  rulings 2025/2026); civil society active (KHRC, NGLHRC); "thousands of gay travelers visit
  annually" (Out of Office). Colonial-era criminalisation remains; no anti-discrimination law.

## Score rationale
0.38 — High Risk, below TUR (0.47), above NGA (0.19), below BRA (0.55).
- Below TUR (0.47): Kenya's same-sex criminalisation is **IN FORCE** (14 years, applies to
  tourists); Turkey's is only draft (11th Judicial Package, not passed). Kenya has documented
  **police extortion** targeting trans people ($50 bribes, "common"); "BEHIND THE BADGE"
  report on systemic police discrimination. KHRC "hell-hole" report (102 evictions, 43
  arrests, mob violence). These exceed Turkey's police-profiling + presidential hostility,
  which are more state-led than Kenya's colonial-criminalisation + police-extortion + mob-
  violence pattern. BUT Turkey's draft-criminalization law + presidential "Year of the Family"
  + health-profiling + passport-carry-ID-checks are more systematically state-orchestrated.
- Above NGA (0.19): no death penalty (Nigeria has Sharia death penalty in 12 states); no
  trans-specific tourist killing documented (Nigeria has Abuja Area Mama); **landmark court
  rulings** affirming trans rights (2025/2026) — Nigeria has no equivalent; "thousands of
  gay travelers visit annually" (Nigeria has a UK travel advisory); no UK travel advisory
  for Kenya specifically on trans risk. BUT Kenya has same-sex criminalisation (14 years,
  in force — Nigeria has SSMPA 14 years + death penalty); police extortion documented;
  KHRC "hell-hole."
- The same-sex criminalisation (14 years, in force, applies to tourists) + police extortion
  + "hell-hole" + mob violence + conservative society keep Kenya in the High Risk band.
  The landmark court rulings + "thousands visit" + civil society + no death penalty + no
  trans-specific tourist killing keep it from the very bottom.
