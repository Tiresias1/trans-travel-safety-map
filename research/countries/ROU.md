# Romania (ROU) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.67** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** EU member (Schengen land border checks lifted Jan 2025 for air/sea;
  full Schengen from Jan 2026). No trans-specific border friction. **ECJ landmark (Oct 2024,
  Case C-4/23 Mirin): Romania must recognize gender-identity changes made in other EU member
  states** without additional internal procedures (British-Romanian trans man Arian
  Mirzarafie-Ahi). **Definitive Romanian court ruling (Mar 2026): state must issue new birth
  certificate reflecting UK-recognized identity.** Positive cross-border recognition trajectory
  (for EU citizens with foreign-issued LGR). [reuters.com, acceptromania.ro, ilga-europe.org]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (LGR exists but restrictive; no self-ID):** Court-based LGR
  (no self-ID, medical-gatekept, inconsistent). ILGA-Europe: Romania has "consistent lack of
  implementation of CJEU/ECtHR judgements" on LGR. Anti-discrimination law (GO 137/2000)
  covers "sex" and "sexual orientation" but **NOT explicitly "gender identity."** No same-sex
  marriage or civil unions (Orthodox Church opposed 2017 referendum). Resident-facing; for
  visitors with EU-issued LGR, the ECJ ruling requires recognition.
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law documented.
- **D/E Violence (documented, rising rhetoric):** ILGA-Europe 2024: queer person verbally
  and physically attacked at a gas station in Bucharest; ACCEPT filed 8 criminal complaints.
  Gas-chamber leaflets + 4 hate crimes in 3 weeks (2022). ECtHR Accept v Romania (2021): state's
  failure to ensure LGBT event proceeded peacefully + failure to investigate homophobic
  counter-demonstrator motives. No documented trans-TOURIST-specific killing. ILGA-Europe
  2026: "anti-LGBTI rhetoric intensified" during 2025 presidential campaign.
  [ilga-europe.org 2024/2026, bailii.org]
- **F Police:** No documented police-brutality-against-trans case (unlike Serbia/India/
  Pakistan/Turkey). But ECtHR Accept v Romania (state failure to protect event from counter-
  demonstrators). ECRI: anti-discrimination body resources "insufficient."
- **G Law (no criminalisation but gaps):** No criminalisation of trans identity or same-sex
  (decriminalised 2001, art 200 repealed). BUT: no LGR self-ID (court-based, restrictive);
  no same-sex marriage/civil unions; **anti-discrimination law does NOT explicitly cover GI**
  (ECRI); **hate-crime law does NOT explicitly cover GI/SOGIESC** — "the provision on
  aggravating circumstances has never been applied in cases of SOGIESC-based hate crimes"
  (ECRI 2022 review). National Strategy on hate speech does NOT include anti-LGBTI hate crimes.
  [outrightinternational.org, rm.coe.int, ilga-europe.org Rule-of-Law submission]
- **H Detention/prison:** Thin evidence; no documented trans-prisoner case. Low-confidence.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen/EU HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (conservative, far-right rising, Orthodox):** ILGA-Europe 2026:
  "anti-LGBTI rhetoric intensified and was repeatedly instrumentalised for political
  mobilisation" during 2025 elections (Georgescu "against LGBTQ"; PM Ciolacu "does not want to
  be forced to wear a skirt"). Far-right influences rising (2024/2025 elections). Romanian
  Orthodox Church key opponent to civil partnerships. BUT: Bucharest Pride 20th anniversary
  (2025, AP — tens of thousands); ECJ landmark ruling (positive trajectory); civil society
  active (ACCEPT, MozaiQ, Museum of Queer History). [ilga-europe.org 2026, apnews.com]

## Score rationale
0.67 — Reduced Risk, ~tied with LTU (0.66)/HUN (0.68), above SRB (0.64), below LVA (0.69).
- Above SRB (0.64): EU member + Schengen (no border friction; Serbia is not Schengen); no
  police-brutality case (Serbia had Feb-2024 torture/sexual-harassment); **ECJ landmark cross-
  border LGR ruling** (Serbia has no equivalent EU-law protection); Bucharest Pride 20th
  with tens of thousands (Serbia's Pride under heavy police protection); no not-Schengen border
  friction; no HRT disruptions (Serbia had testosterone shortages).
- ~tied with LTU (0.66)/HUN (0.68): Romania's court-based LGR (exists but restrictive) +
  ECJ landmark cross-border ruling is marginally better than Lithuania's LGR deadlock (17-year
  Constitutional Court refusal) or Hungary's banned-LGR-on-books (post-Tisza). BUT Romania's
  anti-discrimination law doesn't explicitly cover GI (Lithuania's is similarly absent;
  Hungary's is also weak); Romania's hate-crime law doesn't explicitly cover GI (ECRI gap);
  far-right rising in 2025 elections (comparable to Hungary's Orbán-legacy / Lithuania's
  Gražulis). Net: all three are conservative Eastern European EU states with restrictive LGR
  + anti-LGBTI rhetoric but no criminalisation + EU rule-of-law protections + active civil
  society.
- Below LVA (0.69): Romania's far-right rising (2025 elections, Georgescu) + Orthodox Church
  + anti-LGBTI election rhetoric + anti-discrimination/hate-crime law GI-gaps are marginally
  worse than Latvia's (less extreme documented rhetoric; ECtHR Hanovs is homophobic not
  trans-specific). But both are close.
