## Key findings (with sources)
- Law of 25 June 2017, in force 1 Jan 2018: legal gender + name change by self-
  declaration WITHOUT medical conditions. Constitutional Court (19 June 2019) removed
  the condition that forced a married trans person to divorce before recognition.
  [justice.belgium.be (CAPTCHA; from summary), be.brussels]
- Gender Act art. 4 (since 2014): discrimination on gender identity/expression treated
  as sex discrimination — comprehensive protection in employment, goods/services,
  housing. Same-sex marriage (2003, world's 2nd), adoption (2006). [outrightinternational,
  igvm-iefh.belgium.be (from summary), discoveringbelgium]
- Hate crime AND hate speech law cover SO/GI/GEx/sex characteristics; transphobia a
  separately registered motive; bias motive an aggravating sentencing factor. Belgium
  adopted Europe's first law against femicides (2023), with protections for gender
  minorities. [outrightinternational, hatecrime.osce.org]
- ILGA Rainbow Map 2026: Belgium #4-5, 85% (top tier). Used as ONE signal among many,
  not as the score basis. [rainbowmap.ilga-europe.org]
- Rising violence (real): VRT (May 2025) via Unia/IGVM — gay men and transgender people
  "increasingly the target of discrimination and physical aggression"; Unia closed 136
  SO-related cases. RTBF investigation (Feb 2025): dating-app ambush attacks on queer
  men (luring/robbery/assault — mainly gay men). FRA LGBTIQ survey: 15% attacked in 5
  yrs, 27% avoid certain locations for fear of assault, 53% avoid holding hands in
  public. [vrt.be, ket.brussels, fra.europa.eu (from summary)]
- No legislative rollback; stable progressive consensus (academic: anti-gender
  mobilisations studied but found not influential in Belgium). [aup-online.com]
- "One of the safest countries in the world for LGBTQIA+ travelers" (Intrepid);
  Brussels has dedicated LGBTQIA+ municipal infrastructure. [intrepidtravel.com,
  be.brussels]

## Visitor-focused summary
Top-tier legal framework: self-ID since 2018, comprehensive anti-discrimination law,
hate crime and hate speech both criminalised for gender identity, transphobia a
tracked motive, and Europe's first femicide law with gender-minority protections —
all in a politically stable, EU-rule-of-law environment with no rollback movement.
The downside is a documented rise in anti-LGBTQ violence and intimidation (Unia/VRT
2025), with trans people and gay men named as the increasing targets, plus a dating-
app ambush pattern affecting queer men in urban nightlife. For a visitor this is a
low-risk destination with the usual urban-nightlife caution; the legal exposure is
zero and the day-to-day environment is welcoming.
