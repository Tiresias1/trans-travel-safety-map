  advisory explicitly flags "laws targeting persons based on sexual orientation."** "The 2023
  Anti-Homosexuality Act (AHA) criminalizes consensual same-sex relations in Uganda. **The law
  applies to both visitors and residents.**" Directly tells tourists they're subject to the law.
  [travel.state.gov]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** No LGR; "no specific laws regulating gender identity" (UK
  CPIN). Intersex people can register a change of sex (described as "hermaphrodites" in law).
  No anti-discrimination law for SO/GI. [ecoi.net CPIN]
- **C Gendered spaces:** Not specifically documented (AHA targets same-sex conduct + promotion,
  not gender expression per se).
- **D/E Violence (SEVERE — 1,000+ violations, mobs, entrapment):** Reuters (Jun 2024):
  **1,000+ rights violations in 9 months** (arrests, torture, house evictions). HRW (May 2025):
  "authorities perpetrated widespread discrimination and violence against LGBT people in the
  two years since the AHA became law... spread misinformation and hatred against LGBT people."
  Guardian (Jan 2024): mobs outside trans people's homes ("grabbing me and shouting that I
  needed to die" — Arianna, in a safe house with 20 other trans people). AccessNow (2025):
  **digital entrapment** — police obtain evidence via dating apps → charge suspected
  homosexuals. AP (Feb 2024): 2 women arrested for kissing (first AHA arrests). Amnesty (Oct
  2024): "Everybody Here Is Having Two Lives or Phones" — online attacks "drastically
  increased"; technology-facilitated gender-based violence. HRAPF (Jan 2025): continued
  violations; Nakivale Refugee Settlement violence. No documented trans-TOURIST-specific
  killing. [reuters.com, hrw.org, theguardian.com, accessnow.org, apnews.com, amnesty.org]
- **F Police/state (SEVERE — state-perpetrated):** HRW: "Ugandan authorities perpetrated
  widespread discrimination and violence." Police arrests + torture + entrapment via digital
  evidence + house evictions (the 1,000+ violations are partly state-perpetrated). State-led
  "misinformation and hatred" enabling mob violence.
- **G Law (death penalty, national, applies to visitors):** Anti-Homosexuality Act (May 2023):
  **life imprisonment for same-sex; death penalty for "aggravated homosexuality"** (serial
  offenses, HIV-positive, etc.). Criminalises "promotion of homosexuality" (advocacy/support
  ban). **Applies to both visitors and residents** (US State Dept). Constitutional Court (Apr
  2024): upheld the bulk (incl. life + death penalty); struck down healthcare-access restriction

  no anti-discrimination law. [hrw.org, travel.state.gov, ecoi.net CPIN]
- **H Detention/prison:** "Torture" documented (part of the 1,000+ violations — Reuters).

- **I Health/medication:** Constitutional Court struck down healthcare-access restriction
  (positive). BUT: Ark Wellness Hub (LGBTQ health clinic) "operating in an increasingly hostile

  Healthcare-access barriers persist due to anti-LGBT climate. [theguardian.com, hrw.org]
- **K State & media climate (Museveni, state-led hostility):** Museveni (since 1986):
  "ramped up crackdowns and discrimination against LGBT people to unprecedented levels." AHA
  institutionalised anti-LGBT climate; "spread misinformation and hatred." "Promotion of
  homosexuality" criminalised (advocacy ban). US travel advisory. BUT: civil society fighting
  back (HRW, Amnesty, AccessNow, HRAPF, Ark Wellness Hub, trans advocates — JURIST interview).

## Visitor-focused summary
Uganda is Do Not Travel for trans visitors — the 2023 Anti-Homosexuality Act imposes life
imprisonment for same-sex conduct and the **death penalty for "aggravated homosexuality,"**
and the US State Department explicitly warns that **"the law applies to both visitors and
residents."** Over 1,000 rights violations (arrests, torture, evictions) were documented in
nine months. Mobs have gathered outside trans people's homes ("grabbing me and shouting that
I needed to die"); police use digital entrapment (obtaining evidence via dating apps); the
government has "spread misinformation and hatred against LGBT people." The law also criminalises
the "promotion of homosexuality," meaning any advocacy or support is banned. There are no laws
regulating gender identity — the AHA targets same-sex conduct and promotion, not trans identity
per se — but trans people are swept up in the broader anti-LGBT crackdown, mobs, and state
violence. The Constitutional Court upheld the AHA in April 2024 (striking down only a few
provisions on healthcare access and renting).

## Score rationale
0.17 — Do Not Travel, between RUS (0.12) and NGA (0.19), below MYS (0.28)/KEN (0.38), above
SAU (0.05)/IRN (0.09)/BRN (0.07).
- Above RUS (0.12): Uganda has the **death penalty** for "aggravated" same-sex (Russia has
  no death penalty); the AHA **explicitly applies to visitors** (Russia's transition ban +
  extremist designation affect all but aren't explicitly visitor-targeted); 1,000+ documented
  violations + mobs + digital entrapment + "promotion" criminalised + state-led "misinformation
  and hatred." BUT Uganda doesn't criminalise trans IDENTITY per se (AHA is same-sex conduct +
  promotion; Russia's transition-ban is trans-specific).
- Below NGA (0.19): Uganda's AHA death penalty is **national** (Nigeria's is Sharia in 12
  northern states for Muslims only); the AHA **explicitly applies to visitors** (Nigeria's
  SSMPA applies to all but the visitor-specificity is less prominent); 1,000+ violations in 9
  months (more systematic than Nigeria's 556); "promotion" criminalised. BUT Nigeria has the
  directly-documented trans-specific **Abuja Area Mama killing** (found beaten on highway,
  misgendered, no arrest); Uganda's documented violence is more broadly LGBTQ+ (mobs, arrests)
  than trans-visitor-specific. Both are genuinely close.
- Above SAU (0.05)/IRN (0.09): Uganda's death penalty is for "aggravated" same-sex (not ALL
  same-sex like Iran; not gender-expression per se like Saudi); no documented state-custody
  rape (unlike Iran's Ghazal Marzban); no forced transitions (unlike Iran); no systematic
  border-targeting of trans visitors (unlike UAE). BUT the AHA's explicit visitor-application +
  1,000+ violations + mobs + entrapment + state-led hostility are extreme.
- Below KEN (0.38) decisively: Uganda has the death penalty + AHA-explicit-visitor-application
  + "promotion" criminalised + 1,000+ violations + mobs + state-led "misinformation" (Kenya
  has same-sex 14-year criminalisation + police extortion but landmark court rulings + "thousands
  visit" + no death penalty + no "promotion" ban + no state-led "misinformation").
