## Visitor-focused summary
Israel is the Middle East's most progressive destination for trans visitors, with a strong
legal framework (no criminalisation, anti-discrimination protections, court-based LGR with
recent dead-name removal, publicly funded transition healthcare) and Tel Aviv as a regional
queer hub. BUT two traveler-specific risks pull the score down. First, **Ben-Gurion Airport
has no regulations for interacting with trans travelers** — documented humiliating
questioning and detainment, with surgery demanded for gender recognition ("If you don't
undergo surgery, you're still a male"). Every foreign visitor transits Ben-Gurion — this is
a high-probability axis-A friction. Second, violence is rising sharply: 433 homophobic/
transphobic physical incidents in 2025 (dating-app traps, organized ambushes, trans people
"bearing the brunt"), with Trump's anti-trans rhetoric reportedly inflaming the local climate.
The right-wing Netanyahu coalition and the Israel-Hamas war context have weakened
protections.
