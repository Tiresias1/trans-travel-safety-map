# Poland (POL) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.72** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/security:** Schengen (no internal border for EU travellers); Canada
  advisory standard (passport theft, unofficial taxis). No documented trans-specific
  border-police humiliation. BUT see axis H — the immigration-detention case of a foreign
  trans woman (axis A/H overlap).
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** LGR is court-based, **medically gatekept**, with "absence
  of a clear legal basis" for document changes; sex change only noted infrequently after
  reassignment (resident-facing, but signals a restrictive framework). No self-ID.
  [bazhum.muzhp.pl]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no cross-dressing statute. Low
  axis-C (better than the UK's in-force facilities code).
- **D Public/social reaction:** Białystok Equality March (2019) attacked with bottles,
  paving stones, firecrackers, hateful slurs; Lublin arrests. Amnesty "A Thousand Cuts"
  (2025): technology-facilitated gender-based violence against Poland's LGBTI community
  on X, 2019-2025 (doxxing/online harassment). Canada advisory: women alone in rural
  areas face harassment/verbal abuse. Axis-D climate more hostile than Western European
  peers, esp. outside cities. [amnesty.org, travel.gc.ca]
- **E Violence & predation:** Leicester hate-crime report: significant underreporting/
  underrecording of anti-LGBTI hate crimes; systemic legal/policy gaps, law-enforcement
  and judicial shortcomings, poor victim support. Pride-event violence (axis D). No
  documented dating-app-ambush pattern (unlike Italy/Austria).
- **F Police:** Białystok/Lublin — police arrested counter-protestors (protective at
  Pride). At detention level, see axis H (foreign trans woman staff failed to recognise).
- **G Law:** No cross-dressing statute; no propaganda law; no same-sex marriage (civil
  unions absent — actually Poland has NO same-sex civil unions either; Prosecutor ceased
  investigating foreign same-sex marriages June 2025, reversing PiS policy). No same-sex-
  conduct criminalisation. Hate-speech law (art. 256) reform DRAFT (March 2025) to add
  gender identity/orientation — incoming, not yet enacted.
- **H Detention/prison placement (severe, foreign-traveler-specific):**
  - **Association for Legal Intervention: a Vietnamese trans woman placed in a Polish
    "guarded center for male foreigners"** (immigration detention for foreigners) despite
    being on HRT since 2018; she repeatedly told staff she was trans and needed HRT —
    not recognized. A directly foreign-trans-traveler axis-H case.
  - **ECtHR W.W. v. Poland (11 July 2024, app 31842/20): Poland violated a trans woman's
    Art. 8 (private life) rights by denying HRT in prison**; €8,000 damages. Documented
    systemic axis-H failure (HRT denial to a trans prisoner).
  - Placement by biology/registry; no specialized trans section analogous to Italy's
    Rebibbia G8 documented; OHCHR submission notes NTPM/Helsinki Foundation pushing for
    self-determination in prisons (i.e., not currently in place). Axis-H worse than
    Italy's (no mitigation).
- **I Health/medication:** HRT-denial-in-prison case (axis H) shows medication
  continuity is at risk in detention; normal tourist carry-in within Schengen
  uneventful.
- **J Family/diaspora:** Poland is a transit/destination for LGBTQI+ displacement (fleeing
  Belarus/Russia/Chechnya) — not a family-diaspora-outing pattern for visitors per se.
- **K State & media climate (negative baseline, improving trajectory):** UN SOGIE
  independent expert (Nov 2024): "seismic shift away from hostile rhetoric" under the
  Tusk coalition (from Dec 2023, replacing the Law and Justice/PiS government);
  Hate-speech law draft expanding to GI (March 2025); Prosecutor ceased investigating
  foreign same-sex marriages (June 2025). BUT "still lags in legal protections" — ILGA
  Rainbow Map: Poland LAST among EU countries, 41/45 in Europe (policy-completeness
  metric — NOT a score input, used here as one weak baseline signal). HRW 2025: reforms
  "slow to materialize and insufficient." Net: low baseline, improving trajectory.

## Visitor-focused summary
Poland is reduced-risk with two genuinely traveler-relevant weaknesses and a low but
improving social-climate baseline. In detention, Poland has documented severe failures
with foreign trans travelers directly: a Vietnamese trans woman was held in a male
immigration-detention center and her HRT needs ignored; and the European Court of Human
Rights found Poland violated a trans prisoner's rights by denying her HRT (2024, €8,000
damages). There is no specialized trans prison section analogous to Italy's Rebibbia.
Socially, the climate was deeply hostile under PiS ("LGBT-free zones") and is improving
under Tusk (hate-speech reform draft, March 2025), but a low baseline persists — Pride
marches have been violently attacked (Białystok 2019), online anti-LGBT harassment is
documented (Amnesty 2025), and rural areas draw harassment warnings. Bathrooms are
unrestricted and there is no cross-dressing statute.

BRA (0.55)/USA (0.52).
- Below ITA/AUT: Poland's axis-H is worse (foreign-trans-woman in male immigration

  mitigated placement); lower social-climate baseline (Białystok Pride violence, online
  TFGBV, underreporting gaps); restrictive medical-gatekept LGR (axis B friction).
- vs GBR (0.74, thin gap): Poland has no in-force bathroom rollback (UK does — daily,
  traveler-affecting) and an improving Tusk trajectory, BUT its documented axis-H
  detention failures (foreign-trans-woman-in-male-detention + ECtHR violation) +
  Pride violence + low baseline tip it just below. Probability×severity: UK is higher-
  probability/lower-severity (daily bathroom friction, no criminal penalty); Poland is
  lower-probability/higher-severity (detention HRT-denial, Pride violence) — they are
  close; the systemic axis-H failures + lower baseline settle Poland a notch below.
- The ILGA "last-in-EU" ranking is NOT a score input (policy-completeness); the score
  reflects the verified traveler-axis evidence + improving-but-low baseline.
