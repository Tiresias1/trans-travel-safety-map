# Croatia (HRV) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.72** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/documents:** EU + Schengen (since Jan 2023) — no internal border
  for EU travellers; border checks for non-EU. No documented trans-specific border
  friction. Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (partial LGR):** LGR procedures exist since 2015 (court/
  administrative, registration of gender change in personal documents) — **not self-ID**,
  "not accessible in a quick and transparent manner," and **partial** (birth certificate,
  personal ID, passport only — not education/work/non-state docs). Resident-facing
  friction; no tourist document-outing cases. No X marker. [tgeu.org CoE report]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no cross-dressing statute. The
  Constitution bans same-sex marriage (2013 referendum) — resident-facing family law,
  not traveler-outing. Low axis-C.
- **D Public/social reaction (moderate-conservative, Catholic):** ECRI: "LGBTI persons
  continue to face prejudice and discrimination in everyday life." Anti-gender movement
  active in 2024 (ILGA). Zadar accommodation-refusal case: a host cancelled a Brazilian
  gay couple's booking (2018) — **prosecuted and charged in 2024** (enforcement works,
  but discrimination occurs). US State Dept: crimes involving violence targeting LGBT
  people. No documented mob-violence, trans-tourist-rape, or dating-app-ambush. misterb&b:
  Dalmatian coast (Dubrovnik/Hvar/Split) international-tourism-open; Zagreb gay scene;
  rural/Catholic areas less tolerant. [ilga-europe.org, state.gov, misterbandb.com]
- **E Violence & predation:** No documented organised dating-app-ambush; no mob-violence-
  against-trans. Lower-severity than Serbia/Greece/Italy.
- **F Police:** ILGA Police review: ombudsman criticised **President Milanović for
  outing** the Economy Minister (suggesting he might be gay) — an institutional outing
  incident (axis B/F). US State Dept: unjustified police violence incl. pushbacks against
  irregular migrants (general, not trans-specific). Academic article discusses need for
  trans-sensitive search procedures (hrcak). No documented trans-specific police-brutality
  case (contrast Serbia Feb-2024 / Italy Milan). [ilga-europe.org, state.gov, hrcak.srce.hr]
- **G Law (weaker than Western peers):** Anti-Discrimination Act (21 grounds) + Gender
  Equality Act; same-sex life partnerships legal since 2014 (no marriage equality —
  constitutional ban). No conversion-therapy ban documented. No criminalisation.
  [wikipedia, equalitylaw.eu]
- **H Detention/prison (low-confidence — thin evidence):** No Ludwig Boltzmann / CPT /
  ECtHR trans-detainee case surfaced; academic/policy awareness of trans-sensitive search
  (hrcak). Assume EU-typical biology/registry default but **flag: axis-H thin — partly
  source scarcity, not confirmed safety.**
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen/EU HRT carry-in uneventful; no documented disruptions.
- **K State & media climate (conservative-Catholic, moderate):** Anti-gender movement
  active; Catholic influence; but "among the most advanced ex-socialist/Yugosphere
  countries" (Wikipedia); constitutional gender equality; ombudsman active (criticised
  the President's outing). Moderate-conservative, no extreme legislation.

## Visitor-focused summary
Croatia is reduced-risk with a cleaner documented traveler-axis profile than its Balkan
neighbours: no mob violence, trans-tourist rape, police-brutality case, or foreign-trans-
detention case surfaced — and the Dalmatian coast's international-tourism culture is
broadly open, with Zagreb's gay scene the Balkans' most established. The framework is
weaker than Western Europe's: legal gender recognition (since 2015) is partial and not
self-ID, there is no marriage equality (a 2013 constitutional ban), and an anti-gender
movement is active. A 2024 prosecution of a host who refused a gay couple shows
enforcement works, and the President was formally criticised for outing a minister. The
Catholic-conservative climate is more marked outside tourist areas.
