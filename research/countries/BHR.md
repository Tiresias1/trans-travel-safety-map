# Bahrain (BHR) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.12** · Band: Do Not Travel

## Axis findings
- **A Border:** No directly-documented trans-visitor case (unlike UAE/Qatar). But Cairo 52:
  "Bahrain has a longstanding history of denying independent human rights researchers and
  advocates from entering the country" — restrictive access.
- **B Documents:** No LGR for trans people. **Cairo 52 (Feb 2024): Bahraini court rejected
  a trans man's application for LGR and gender-affirming healthcare, citing Islamic Sharia.**
  The plaintiff sought Ministry of Health medical-committee examination — denied.
  [cairo52.com]
- **G Law:** Penal Code: same-sex conduct criminalised (unspecified penalty; HDT lists
  criminalisation). No LGR; no anti-discrimination law. Gender-affirming healthcare denied
  via Sharia (court ruling). Cairo 52: "systemic legal and institutional discrimination."
- **D/E/F:** Cairo 52: "local media outlets often serve as primary sources of information
  regarding arrests of transgender individuals" — arrests documented but details scarce
  (Bahrain restricts human-rights-researcher access). No documented trans-specific killing.
- **K:** Conservative Gulf state; Sharia-influenced judiciary; anti-trans institutional
  discrimination (court-cited-Sharia-to-deny-LGR).

## Score rationale
0.12 — ~tied with QAT (0.14)/ARE (0.13). Same Gulf-state profile: same-sex criminalised +
no LGR + conservative anti-LGBT climate. Bahrain has the **court-cited-Sharia-to-deny-LGR**
(unique negative: a court explicitly invoking Sharia to deny a trans person's rights) +
gender-affirming-healthcare-denied-via-court. BUT no documented trans-visitor case (unlike
UAE's X-ray/deportation/sentencing or Qatar's Guerrero Aviña conviction) + lower profile.
