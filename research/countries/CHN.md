# China (CHN) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.53** · Band: Elevated Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit (airport discrimination, visitor-specific):** Thai transgender
  YouTuber stopped at a Chinese airport, alleged discriminatory/sexist treatment — "No one
  got into trouble except me. I was stopped because I'm a katoey." A directly foreign-trans-
  visitor axis-A/F case. [thethaiger.com]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (very restrictive LGR):** LGR requires gender-affirming surgery
  AND **parental consent at ANY age** (even adults; if parents deceased, must prove it).
  Very restrictive. No self-ID; no anti-discrimination law. [foreignpolicy.com,
  outrightinternational.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law documented. Not a major documented
  friction axis (unlike the US's criminal-ban states or the UK's facilities code).
- **D/E Violence & predation (authoritarian crackdown, GBV risk):** CCP crackdown on
  LGBTQ+ people/"foreign ideologies" (RFA 2024); Jin Xing (transgender TV icon) performance
  ban (Jan 2025, Guangzhou); Pride-month social-media/film censorship (HRW Jun 2026);
  Swiss drag queen "Soya the Cow" faced censorship/cancellation on China tour (axis-D/K,
  foreign-performer-specific). UCL scoping review: trans people in China face "elevated
  risk of GBV." No documented tourist-rape/mob/dating-app-ambush. [rfa.org, hrw.org,
  swissinfo.ch, discovery.ucl.ac.uk]
- **F Police/coercion:** Guardian (Jan 2024): LGBTQ+ advocates face "jail and forced
  confession"; house arrest by unsupportive families ("systemic persecution"). Airport
  discrimination (axis A/F). No documented police-profiling-sweeps of trans women (unlike
  Turkey's Istanbul-Beyoğlu). [theguardian.com]
- **G Law:** No criminalisation of trans identity (decriminalised 1997; de-listed as mental
  disorder 2001). "Don't encourage, don't oppose, don't promote" baseline. BUT: no legal
  protections for gender minorities; cybersecurity law restricts LGBTIQ content deemed to
  "disrupt social order" (extensive censorship); **conversion therapy NOT legally banned**
  (Ling'er won compensation via court — shows courts CAN protect, but no legislative ban);
  LGR requires surgery + parental consent. [outrightinternational.org, wikipedia.org]
- **H Detention/prison/psychiatric (severe — forced conversion therapy):** **Ling'er — 97
  days involuntary psychiatric commitment + 7 sessions of electroshock "conversion"
  therapy** (family pressure + medical-system complicity; tied to a bed; won 60,000-yuan
  compensation, Nov 2024 — unprecedented). A severe axis-H case showing the medical/
  psychiatric system can be weaponized against trans people. CTSN (survivor network)
  formed 2023 in response to government crackdown on LGBT NGOs — operates semi-publicly/
  abroad. [nytimes.com, theguardian.com, rfa.org, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, ohchr.org]
- **I Health/medication:** China is a regional gender-affirming-care hub (budget-friendly
  surgery); BUT parental-consent barrier for surgery even as adults. HRT access generally
  available.
- **K State & media climate (authoritarian, deteriorating under Xi):** "Intensifying
  repression and promotion of normative [gender/family values]" (HRW); shrinking space for
  LGBT people; NGO crackdown 2023; Pride/film censorship Jun 2026; Jin Xing ban Jan 2025.
  Deteriorating trajectory. BUT: no active criminalisation of trans identity; "don't
  encourage" baseline; courts CAN provide some protection; care-tourism hub.

## Visitor-focused summary
China is elevated-risk — authoritarian but not criminalising. The most directly visitor-
specific finding is airport discrimination: a Thai trans YouTuber was singled out and
subjected to discriminatory treatment at a Chinese airport. The most severe documented
axis-H case is forced psychiatric commitment: a trans woman was held for 97 days and
subjected to electroshock "conversion" therapy under family pressure (she won unprecedented
compensation), showing the medical/psychiatric system can be weaponized — though this
targeted a resident, not a tourist. Under Xi Jinping, the space for LGBTQ+ organizing and
expression is shrinking (Pride/film censorship, NGO crackdown, the Jin Xing performance ban,
drag-performance cancellation). There is no criminalisation of trans identity, no bathroom
restriction law, and courts can provide some protection, but there are no anti-discrimination
protections and LGR requires surgery and parental consent at any age.
