## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/documents:** Schengen (EES deployed Mar 2026); no documented
  trans-specific border-police humiliation; foreign X markers accepted; no foreign
  advisories against Greece. Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** LGR court-based (Law 4491/2017) — no medical/sterilisation
  requirement since 2017; the 2024 marriage-equality law (5089/2024) established
  SOGIESC equal-treatment guarantees in goods/services, education, healthcare,
  housing. Resident-facing mostly; no tourist document-outing cases. No X marker.
  [outrightinternational.org, ilga-europe.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law. Low axis-C.
- **D Public/social reaction (social reality lags law — real):** FRA 2024: 63% of
  Greeks avoid often/always holding hands (EU-27: 53%); 36% avoid certain locations for
  fear of assault (EU-27: 29%); only 35% fairly/very open (EU-27: 51%) — higher
  avoidance/fear than the EU average. **UK Foreign Office issued an LGBTQ+ travel
  warning for Greece (Apr 2024).** [fra.europa.eu, thepinknews.com, euronews.com]
- **E Violence & predation (documented):** ILGA 2025 review — March, a violent mob of
  ~150 people targeted two trans people in Thessaloniki, assaulting them and hurling
  bottles. 38 anti-LGBTI hate crimes documented in 2022; "hate crimes continued to be a
  serious issue"; a Black migrant trans woman attacked (July 10). Zak Kostopoulos
  (LGBTI activist) killed; appeals court upheld guilty verdicts (axis-E; gay-activist
  case). [ilga-europe.org 2025/2024 reviews]
- **F Police (documented abuse, ECtHR):** *Electra Leda Koutra and Anastasia Katzaki
  v. Greece* (ECtHR) — transgender sex workers detained and mistreated by police; a
  lawyer representing a trans woman was mistreated and placed in a cell for ~20 minutes.
  Amnesty: unlawful use of force in police operations, excessive force in demonstrations.
  [ilga-europe.org case-law, amnesty.org]
- **G Law (strong):** No criminalisation; conversion therapy banned for minors (2022);
  intersex genital mutilation criminalised (under 15); hate-crime law covers SO/GI;
  marriage equality (16 Feb 2024, first Orthodox-majority country). 2026 Crete appeals
  court convicted French tourists for a 2017 homophobic attack (hate-crime enforcement
  works). [wikipedia, outrightinternational, ilga-europe.org 2026 review]
- **H Detention/prison:** Ludwig Boltzmann national report: "significant gaps and
  inconsistencies" in how LGBTIQ-detainee legal standards are implemented in practice
  (placement detail not explicitly sourced, EU-typical biology/registry default).
  Comparable to other EU countries; no specialized-section or reform-trajectory noted.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (strong positive trajectory):** Greece ranked 7th in
  ILGA-Europe's 2025 Rainbow Map; rapid legal progress (conversion-therapy ban, intersex
  surgery ban, National Strategy for Equality, marriage equality 2024); progressive
  government; Wikipedia: "among the most advanced in Europe." BUT Trans Rights Index
  2025: 21/32 indicators (backsliding outpaces progress regionally). [outrightinternational,
  thepressproject.gr]

## Visitor-focused summary
Greece is a reduced-risk destination with a clear law-versus-practice gap. The legal
framework is among Europe's strongest (ILGA #7, conversion-therapy and intersex-surgery
bans, hate-crime law, marriage equality, a progressive government with an improving
trajectory, and no trans-specific border or bathroom restrictions). But the social
reality lags: a mob of about 150 assaulted two trans people in Thessaloniki; an ECtHR
case documented police detention and mistreatment of trans sex workers; surveys show
Greeks are markedly more avoidant of public LGBTI visibility than the EU average; and the
UK Foreign Office has issued an LGBTQ travel warning for Greece. Major tourist
destinations (Mykonos, Athens's Gazi, Thessaloniki) are welcoming; outside them the
climate is rougher than the law suggests.
