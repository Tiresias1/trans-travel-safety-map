# United Arab Emirates (ARE) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.13** · Band: Do Not Travel

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit (DIRECTLY VISITOR-SPECIFIC, SEVERE, MULTIPLE DOCUMENTED CASES):**
  - **Israeli trans women: "They X-rayed us to check our genitals"** — detained for hours at
    Dubai airport, anatomy checked via X-ray vs passport records, denied entry, passports
    confiscated, mocked (ynetnews — directly documented trans-visitor border case).
  - **Thai trans model Rachaya Noppakaroon: deported at Dubai border** (Expo 2020
    performer, male passport, despite all travel documents) (Vice).
  - **Gigi Gorgeous: detained at Dubai airport immigration** ("I was told you are
    transgender. You cannot come into the country") (TMZ 2016).
  - **Indian trans person: denied entry to Dubai** despite valid UAE-issued visa (ThePrint/
    FreePressJournal — consumer complaint dismissed as sovereign function).
  - **Brazilian trans woman "Maria": arrested in Dubai apartment** by 4 plainclothes police
    accusing her of drug trafficking (Metropoles 2022).
  - **Singaporean trans woman Nur Qistina Fitriah Ibrahim: sentenced to 1 year prison in
    Abu Dhabi** "for dressing in a feminine way" (AP).
  These are ALL directly trans-traveler-specific — the UAE has the most extensively
  documented trans-visitor border/airport discrimination of any country researched.
  [ynetnews.com, vice.com, tmz.com, theprint.in, metropoles.com, apnews.com]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** No LGR; no legal recognition of trans identity. Cairo 52:
  "recognition of legal gender identity is significantly constrained." [cairo52.com]
- **C Gendered spaces:** Cross-dressing/gender expression is actively targeted — Cairo 52:
  "transgender expression... frequently results in the apprehension, confinement, or expulsion
  of non-Emirati tourists and residents." (axis C/G overlap).
- **D/E Violence & predation:** The multiple documented cases (X-ray, deportation, 1-year
  prison, apartment arrest) are themselves the axis-D/E/F evidence. No documented killing
  (unlike Saudi's Mohammad custodial death).
- **F Police:** Dubai airport immigration targets trans visitors; Abu Dhabi police sentenced
  a Singaporean trans woman; plainclothes police arrested a Brazilian trans woman in her
  apartment.
- **G Law (criminalisation):** Federal criminal code (2022): same-sex relations criminalised
  (up to 6 months; Art 409). Abu Dhabi criminal code: "unnatural sex" — up to **14 years**.
  Dubai criminal code: "sodomy" — up to **14 years**. Trans gender expression targeted (no
  specific cross-dressing statute documented, but general "immorality"/"indecency" provisions
  used — the Singaporean was sentenced for "dressing in a feminine way"). No LGR. No anti-
  discrimination. Sharia is "a main source of legislation" (Art 7 constitution).
  [humandignitytrust.org, outrightinternational.org, cairo52.com]
- **H Detention/prison:** Singaporean trans woman: 1 year prison (Abu Dhabi). Brazilian
  trans woman arrested. Multiple airport detentions. No documented custodial death (unlike
  Saudi).
- **I Health/medication:** No documented HRT-specific customs cases, but the general
  anti-trans climate + drug-trafficking accusations (Maria case) make medication import
  hazardous.
- **K State & media climate (actively targets trans visitors):** Cairo 52: state "employs
  various legal mechanisms to explicitly acknowledge and penalize nonconformity with
  traditional gender roles" and "apprehension, confinement, or expulsion of non-Emirati
  tourists." The state actively targets trans visitors at the border.

## Visitor-focused summary
The UAE is one of the most thoroughly documented trans-traveler-hostile countries in the
world. At the border, trans visitors have been X-rayed to check their genitals (Israeli trans
women), deported for having a male passport (Thai model, Gigi Gorgeous), denied entry despite
valid visas (Indian trans person), and sentenced to prison for "dressing in a feminine way"
(Singaporean, 1 year, Abu Dhabi). A Brazilian trans woman was arrested in her Dubai apartment
by plainclothes police. Same-sex conduct is criminalised (up to 14 years). The state "employs
various legal mechanisms to explicitly acknowledge and penalize nonconformity with traditional
gender roles" and actively targets "non-Emirati tourists." There is no legal gender
recognition. BUT: the UAE does not have the death penalty for same-sex conduct (unlike Saudi/
Iran), no documented custodial death of a trans person (unlike Saudi), and some trans visitors
do enter successfully if their passport gender matches their presentation.

## Score rationale
0.13 — Do Not Travel, ~tied with RUS (0.12), above IRN (0.09)/SAU (0.05)/BRN (0.07), below
MYS (0.28).
- ~tied with RUS (0.12): the UAE's directly-documented trans-visitor border targeting (X-ray/
  deportation/sentencing — HIGH-probability, every visitor transits Dubai airport) is comparable
  to Russia's transition-ban + extremist-designation (which affect residents more than short-
  term visitors). The UAE's border targeting is MORE directly visitor-specific; Russia's is
  more broadly hostile. Neither has the death penalty.
- Above IRN (0.09)/SAU (0.05): no death penalty (unlike Iran/Saudi); no documented custodial
  death (unlike Saudi's Mohammad); some trans visitors enter if passport matches (the UAE's
  targeting is triggered by passport/presentation mismatch, not blanket). BUT the documented
  X-ray/deportation/sentencing cases show systematic targeting — the "some enter" caveat is
  conditional on not being flagged.
- Below MYS (0.28): Malaysia's Sharia applies to Muslims (not directly to non-Muslim foreign
  visitors); the UAE's targeting applies to ALL visitors (regardless of religion — Dubai
  airport immigration checks anatomy vs passport for everyone). The UAE is more systematically
  hostile to trans VISITORS than Malaysia.
