# Chile (CHL) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.71** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** No trans-specific border friction; foreign documents recognised.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (self-ID 2019, good framework):** Gender identity law (2019) —
  self-determination (no medical requirements, administrative procedure). F&M: "No
  physiological alteration requirement; No psychiatric diagnosis requirement." Marriage
  equality + adoption (March 2022). Third-sex option for intersex (since 2006). Anti-
  discrimination (Zamudio Law, Law 20.609). [outrightinternational.org, fandmglobalbarometers.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law documented.
- **D/E Violence (documented, significant):** **Ever: trans person murdered and dismembered**
  ("el crimen de odio más grande de Chile" — BioBioChile Jan 2024). **Hotel transphobic attack
  (Samoa, Santiago):** 20-year-old trans man Brayan + girlfriend beaten with bottles/sticks,
  held against their will for nearly an hour; attacker said "I want to kill you" (ChileVision).
  Supreme Court case (187-2023): trans woman murdered — Court of Appeals overturned femicide
  conviction (victim didn't "legally qualify as woman" — records listed male) — **BUT Supreme
  Court restored conviction** (positive: recognition of trans woman as femicide victim).
  Isabella Panes (first trans police officer): institutional discrimination in Carabineros
  (relegated to admin, private life exposed, denied transition-surgery coverage, depression).
  Paris López (trans woman, HIV+, street-living): protection action against Santiago mayor
  for municipal violence/discrimination. [biobiochile.cl, chilevision.cl, law.cornell.edu,
  theclinic.cl, resumen.cl]
- **F Police:** UN HRC commended Chile's police measures to promote LGBTIQ+ rights (Mar 2024).
  BUT: Isabella Panes institutional discrimination (Carabineros — relegated, exposed, denied
  coverage). No documented police-brutality-against-trans case (unlike Serbia/India/Pakistan).
  [ungeneva.org, eluniversal.cl]
- **G Law:** Self-ID (2019); marriage equality (2022); hate-crime law incl. SO+GI (2012);
  anti-discrimination (Zamudio Law). BUT: **2024 "year of regression"** (Movilh — first
  rollback since 1990; lawmakers rejected nondiscrimination law reforms Jun 2024); 2025:
  "quiebre del consenso social, político e institucional" (breakdown of consensus on LGBTIQ+
  rights). Regression trajectory. [washingtonblade.com ×2, movilh.cl]
- **H Detention/prison (POSITIVE — better than most European countries):** **Gendarmería
  protocol (Dec 2019/Jan 2020): trans inmates placed in establishments matching their gender
  identity; may express assumed gender; examined by doctor for hormone/surgical/mental-
  health needs upon entry.** "Que puedan manifestar el género asumido" + segmented
  incarceration. An explicitly gender-identity-based prison-placement policy — BETTER than
  France/Germany/UK/Denmark where trans women are placed in men's prisons. [latercera.com]
- **I Health/medication:** Healthcare access generally available; Isabella Panes denied
  transition-surgery coverage by Carabineros (institutional gap).
- **K State & media climate (progressive law, regression trajectory 2024-25):** Progressive
  framework (self-ID, marriage equality, hate-crime law, trans congresswoman Emilia
  Schneider); UN commended police; positive Gendarmería protocol. BUT: "year of regression"
  (2024); "consensus breakdown" (2025); lawmakers rejecting reforms; Movilh documents
  rollback in all three branches of government. A negative trajectory from a strong baseline.

## Score rationale
0.71 — Reduced Risk, below SGP/ARG (0.72-0.73), above ZAF (0.66), ~tied with GBR (0.71).
- Below ARG (0.73): Chile's regression trajectory (2024 "year of regression," 2025 "consensus
  breakdown," lawmakers rejecting reforms) + Ever murder/dismemberment + hotel transphobic
  attack + Isabella Panes institutional discrimination offset Chile's self-ID 2019 + marriage
  equality + positive Gendarmería protocol. Argentina's Milei hostility + 227 hate crimes +
  prison-housing decree are more severe in absolute terms, but Argentina's self-ID is older
  (2012) and tourist-welcoming Buenos Aires is more established.
- Above ZAF (0.66): Chile has self-ID (2019) + marriage equality (2022) + positive Gendarmería
  axis-H protocol (gender-identity-based prison placement — SA has documented issues +
  Equality Court ruling is positive but reactive not proactive). Chile's violence is lower
  than SA's (no Grindr-hostage pattern; no healthcare-system anti-trans violence documented).
  Chile's regression trajectory is a concern but its baseline is stronger.
- The Gendarmería protocol is a genuine axis-H institutional positive that most European
  countries lack — an argument for placing Chile above some European peers despite the
  regression trajectory. But the Ever murder + hotel attack + "consensus breakdown" keep it in
  the Reduced Risk band, below the top.
