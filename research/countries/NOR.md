# Norway (NOR) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.86** · Band: Low Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** Schengen (not EU); no trans-specific border friction; foreign X accepted.
  Norway issued US travel update for trans citizens (like Denmark/Finland). Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** Self-ID since 2016 (Gender Recognition Act; age 16+;
  replaced sterilisation/psychiatric evaluation). Anti-discrimination protections
  (employment, housing, services). Schengen home-documents recognised. [helsenorge.no,
  legalclarity.org, ldo.no]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; government Action Plan on Gender and
  Sexual Diversity (2023-2026). Low axis-C.
- **D/E Violence & predation (rising; Oslo Pride shooting):** Police 2023: hate crimes
  +18% YoY; SO + trans reports up sharply (215 SO + 69 trans in 2023). **Oslo Pride 2022
  mass shooting** (Zaniar Matapour, ISIS-sympathiser; 2 killed; sentenced 30 years — a
  terrorist attack targeting Pride). Anti-gender movement growing; transphobia overlapping
  extremist + general-public rhetoric ("gender ideology = indoctrination/child grooming/
  normalisation of paedophilia"); Church opposition. Underreporting documented (C-REX/NSFK
  study: major barriers to reporting hate crimes). [ilga-europe.org, nrk.no, sv.uio.no,
  reuters.com]
- **F Police (underreporting gap + strong institutional response):** Underreporting/
  under-investigation barriers documented (NSFK). BUT: Oslo Police Hate Crime Unit +
  National Expertise Group on Hate Crime (police building capacity); PST (Police Security
  Service) annual threat assessment for Pride (2025: "significant decline" in threats but
  hate-speech/intimidation risk remains). Stronger institutional response than Denmark.
  [hatecrime.osce.org, nordiskpost.com, nsfk.org]
- **G Law (strong — GI in both hate-crime AND hate-speech):** **Hate speech against trans
  people criminalised (2020)** — penal code expansion (private remarks → fine/up to 1 year;
  public → up to 3 years). This is STRONGER than Denmark (which doesn't clearly have GI in
  hate-speech criminal law). No criminalisation of trans identity. [reuters.com,
  hatecrime.osce.org]
- **H Detention/prison:** No documented Supreme Court ruling or ECtHR trans-prisoner case
  surfaced (thin evidence, low-confidence). Better-documented than Denmark's axis-H.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (progressive, anti-gender growth):** Government Action Plan;
  stable progressive consensus; BUT anti-gender movement rapidly growing (ILGA 2024 review:
  "trans people and allies continued to be targeted by the rapidly growing anti-gender
  movement"). Oslo-shooting terrorism context.

## Score rationale
0.86 — Low Risk, below DNK (0.87), ~tied with PRT (0.86).
- Below DNK (0.87) marginally: the **Oslo Pride 2022 mass shooting** (terrorist attack, 2
  killed) + rising hate crimes (+18% 2023, SO + trans reports up sharply) + growing
  anti-gender movement + underreporting barriers offset Norway's GI-hate-speech-law
  advantage (stronger than Denmark) + strong institutional response (PST/Hate Crime Unit/
  Action Plan). The shooting was a one-off terror incident (perpetrator sentenced 30 yrs;
  PST reports declining threats 2025) but marks a more acute violence context than Denmark's.
- ~tied with PRT (0.86): Norway's longer self-ID (2016) + GI-hate-speech-law ≈ Portugal's
  self-ID (2018) + conversion ban + bathroom-protected; both have rising incidents (Norway:
  hate crimes +18%; Portugal: +38% hate crime). Norway has the Oslo-shooting but also
  stronger institutional response.
