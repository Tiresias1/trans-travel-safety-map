# Taiwan (TWN) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.80** · Band: Low Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit/documents:** Foreign visitors' home documents recognised at entry;
  no trans-specific border friction documented. Axis-A low for short-term tourists.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (surgery-gatekept, reform stalled):** LGR requires
  **sex-reassignment surgery** (2008 Ministry of Interior order); blocks ~90% of trans
  people from matching ID. Multiple court rulings (2024-25, Taipei/Kaohsiung HACs) allow
  case-by-case changes WITHOUT surgery — but the surgery requirement remains law; reform
  stalled ("government continues to flounder on transgender rights in 2025," Taiwan Insight
  Jan 2026; "transgender rights continued to take a back seat"). Resident-facing; for
  visitors, home documents are what matter at border. [taiwaninsight.org, legalclarity.org,
  taipeitimes.com, newbloommag.net]
- **C Gendered spaces (progressive — better than Japan):** No bathroom restriction law.
  **Government investing NT$280M (US$8.76M) to double gender-neutral public bathrooms
  (623→1,246 by 2029).** Taipei City has trans-friendly service guidelines. BUT Focus
  Taiwan (Nov 2025): only 5 of 149 government-listed all-gender restrooms meet standards
  (implementation gap). Still: proactive gender-neutral-bathroom investment, better than
  Japan's binary-onsen friction. [taipeitimes.com, focustaiwan.tw, medium.com]
- **D/E Public/social reaction (moderate; rising anti-gender discourse):** "Continued
  transphobia" (Taiwan Insight); anti-gender movement since 2018, online-discourse waves
  (Oct 2024 after Lisbeth Wu's case); Amnesty: digital violence against LGBTI people
  continues post-marriage-equality. Taipei held vigil for victims of anti-trans violence
  (implies violence occurs). BUT: 3,000 at Taiwan Trans March (Oct 2025); progressive civil
  society; no documented mob-violence, trans-tourist-rape, or dating-app-ambush.
  [taiwaninsight.org, newbloommag.net, amnesty.org, jurist.org]
- **F Police:** Trans woman Viva questioned about gender during police inspection (axis
  F/D — outing via police). No documented police-brutality case. Taipei City trans-friendly
  service guidelines (institutional accommodation). [taiwanplus.com, medium.com]
- **G Law:** Same-sex marriage (2019, first in Asia); Gender Equality Act; no criminalisation;
  no bathroom restriction law; surgery-requirement for LGR is the structural gap (no self-ID
  reform). No conversion-therapy ban documented.
- **H Detention/prison:** Thin evidence; Chang Gung University dormitory case (trans woman
  forced into men's dorms — signals placement-by-biology in institutional settings). No
  documented prison case. Low-confidence.
- **I Health/medication:** HRT carry-in: no documented customs friction; Taiwan has a
  mature gender-affirming-care infrastructure (regional healthcare hub).
- **K State & media climate (progressive-but-stagnating + rising anti-gender):**
  Progressive direction (marriage equality, gender-neutral-bathroom investment, trans march
  visible); BUT government "floundering" on LGR reform, anti-gender movement growing,
  "actively harmed in pending legislation" (2025). Ipsos 2025 survey: reduced support.

## Score rationale
0.80 — Low Risk, below JPN (0.82), above GRC (0.75)/ITA (0.76).
- Below JPN (0.82): Taiwan has rising anti-gender discourse (online waves, anti-gender
  counter-movement since 2018) + government stagnation on LGR reform ("floundering",
  "actively harmed") + documented police-outing (Viva case) + vigil-for-anti-trans-violence
  — offsetting its marriage-equality + gender-neutral-bathroom-investment + visible-trans-
  march advantages. Japan's near-zero practical incidents + Supreme Court positive trajectory
  (2023 sterilisation ruling) keep it a hair above.
- Above GRC (0.75)/ITA (0.76): Taiwan has no documented severe incidents (no mob-violence,
  tourist-rape, police-brutality ECtHR case, or UK-Foreign-Office travel warning), unlike
  Greece (Thessaloniki mob + ECtHR police abuse) and Italy (tourist-rape + Milan police
  beating + Rebibbia men's-prison). Taiwan's proactive gender-neutral-bathroom investment
  (axis-C) is also better than either.
- At the Low/Reduced boundary (0.80): Taiwan is a progressive Asian destination with a
  visible trans community and proactive facility policies, but its surgery-gatekept LGR +
  government stagnation + rising anti-gender discourse keep it from reaching Japan's level.
