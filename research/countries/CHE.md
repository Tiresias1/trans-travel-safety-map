## Key findings (with sources)
- **Self-ID since 1 Jan 2022:** any person resident & registered in a Swiss commune
  may change sex marker + first name by PERSONAL DECLARATION before the civil registrar
  — no court, no medical. Binary only (female/male); cantonal flexibility (some communes
  allow sex or name change only individually). 350 people changed in big cities in the
  first year (80 Zurich, 47 Basel, 46 Bern, 36 Geneva). [epfl.ch, swissinfo.ch,
  legalclarity.org, ebg.admin.ch]
- Marriage for all since 1 Jul 2022 (64.1% referendum Sep 2021). [legalclarity.org]
- **Hate-crime/hate-speech law gap (real):** Art. 261bis Criminal Code (since 1 Jul
  2020) criminalises public incitement to hatred/discrimination on SEXUAL ORIENTATION
  — but NOT on gender identity. GI discrimination addressed only via civil/employment
  law (Gender Equality Act GLG; Labour Code art. 328 OR employer duty of care;
  Constitution art. 8 interpretation). OSCE: "Switzerland's Criminal Code contains no
  hate crime provision; hate crimes addressed under anti-discrimination criminal
  provisions" (which are SO-only). Federal Supreme Court upheld Soral homophobic
  hate-speech conviction (SO). [ebg.admin.ch, hatecrime.osce.org (from summary),
  legalclarity.org, transwelcome.ch, rainbowmap.ilga-europe.org]
- Hate-crime reporting rising: LGBTIQ Helpline/Pink Cross/TGNS — ~6 hate crimes/week
  reported, 309 cases in 2024, a FIVEFOLD increase since 2020. Low absolute level
  (pop. ~8.8M) but real upward trend. [swissinfo.ch, pinkcross.ch (from summary)]
- **National Action Plan to combat hate crime against LGBTIQ persons** adopted by
  Federal Council 28 Jan 2026 (first national plan, 2026-2030; victim support,
  prevention, monitoring) — positive institutional trajectory. [ebg.admin.ch,
  swissinfo.ch]
- New gender-neutral sexual criminal law (2024). Strong employer duty of care for
  trans employees (transwelcome.ch). No non-binary recognition (NEK ethics statement).
- Anti-gender mobilisations present but resisted (RESIST project). No rollback attempt.
  "One of the safest destinations in the world for queer travelers"; extremely low
  general crime. [lgbtmadonna.com (403; from summary), unhelp, swissinfo.ch]

## Visitor-focused summary
Self-ID legal gender recognition (since 2022, no medical/court), same-sex marriage,
gender-neutral rape law, employer protections, no rollback, and exceptionally low
general crime make Switzerland a low-risk destination. The notable legislative
shortcoming is that the criminal hate-speech/hate-crime provision covers sexual
orientation but not gender identity — so a trans person specifically targeted for
being trans does not benefit from the GI aggravation (though a perceived-SO angle
often applies). Rising reported hate crimes (~6/week, up 5× since 2020) are at a low
absolute level; the government's first National Action Plan (2026-2030) signals a
pro-trans institutional direction.
