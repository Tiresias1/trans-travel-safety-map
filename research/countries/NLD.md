# Netherlands (NLD) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.88** · Anchor: TOP-OF-SCALE

## Key findings (with sources)
- WorldPride Amsterdam 2026 official trans-traveller guidance (Aug 2026): no visa or
  entry restrictions based on gender identity; passports with changed markers or "X"
  are valid; Schengen does not refuse entry on gender identity; possible brief
  administrative questions with X markers; no medical documents required.
  [pride.amsterdam/en/safety-and-travel-information/]
- Legal: LGR requires an expert letter from a medical professional (not full
  self-determination); non-binary "X" recognised. Constitution art. 1 amended (2023)
  to prohibit discrimination on sexual orientation and gender. Hate-crime
  aggravation law entered force 1 July 2025 (discriminatory motive = +⅓ maximum
  penalty). Discrimination in goods/services banned. [outrightinternational.org],
  [equaldex.com/region/netherlands], [government.nl (New Justice laws 2025)],
  [ohchr.org UPR midterm (from summary)]
- Government data: 17% of transgender people report having experienced physical or
  sexual violence; 2022: one-third of discrimination reports to police concerned
  sexual orientation; National Programme against Discrimination active.
  [government.nl (Combating discrimination and violence)]
- Rising hostility: anti-LGBTQ discrimination/violence reports up 81% since 2022;
  anti-trans reports +51% in a year (2025→2026, 4,800 total reports). WorldPride
  opening weekend (Jul 2026): 70+ discrimination reports — against the backdrop of
  the Berlin Pride van attack. [nltimes.nl 2026-04-19, 2026-07-27]
- Institutional response strong: Amsterdam mayor ordered priority investigation of
  Pride-period incidents; expanded "Safer Spaces" reporting network; police chief
  publicly committed to rainbow community safety. [nltimes.nl 2026-06-16, 06-22, 07-23]
- ILGA Rainbow Map 2026 rank: #13. [pride.amsterdam page]
- Amsterdam general safety high (pickpocketing etc. affect everyone).
  [amsterdamtourism.org]
- FRA LGBTIQ Survey 2024 factsheet (PDF fetch failed): from summary — 45% of Dutch
  respondents avoid holding hands in public (EU-27: 53%); some report feeling unsafe
  in public spaces.

## Visitor-focused summary
Very low risk, with a visible recent uptick in hostility. Official guidance confirms
no entry or document barriers for trans visitors; strong legal protections; hate-crime
law with aggravation; energetic institutional support (WorldPride safety apparatus).
However, reported anti-LGBTQ incidents have risen sharply since 2022 (anti-trans
reports +51% year-on-year), and lifetime physical/sexual violence among trans people
(17%) is above the EU's best performers. Everyday risk remains harassment-level rather
than violence-level for visitors in tourist areas.
