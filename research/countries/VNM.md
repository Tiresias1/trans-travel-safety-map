# Vietnam (VNM) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.63** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** No trans-specific border friction documented. An immigration officer
  was suspended for tearing a Taiwanese tourist's flight ticket at Phu Quoc airport (May 2025)
  — general misconduct, not trans-specific.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (LGR recognized in principle, NOT implemented):** Civil Code
  (2015), Article 37: "sex reassignment" recognized — individuals who have changed gender have
  the right to apply for civil-status change (landmark — first time Vietnam recognized trans
  people). BUT: **"did not provide for a transparent and accessible procedure"** (HRW/IRCC);
  **Gender Affirmation Law stalled 8+ years** (UNDP: "Eight Years and Counting"). No anti-
  discrimination law for SO/GI. Same-sex marriage: ban removed (2013) but not recognised. No
  criminalisation of trans identity or same-sex conduct. [undp.org, outrightinternational.org,
  irb-cisr.gc.ca]
- **C Gendered spaces:** Not specifically documented.
- **D/E Violence (lower than neighbors — no trans-specific killing documented):** No documented
  trans-specific killing (unlike Indonesia's Rodrigo / Pakistan's 3 shot in Karachi / Bangladesh's
  Shila). No documented mob violence or dating-app-ambush. BUT: SOGICE (conversion efforts)
  documented (Lighthouse Vietnam 2024: "Exploring the Lived Experiences of Conversion Efforts
  Among LGBTIQ+ People in Vietnam"). Healthcare-system discrimination: trans people face
  "stigma and deeply ingrained systemic barriers" + "fear of discrimination and lack of trans-
  competent providers" (UN Vietnam, APTN). Trans women high HIV risk + limited care access
  (TransAction study). [lighthousevietnam.org, vietnam.un.org, weareaptn.org, tandfonline.com]
- **F Police:** No documented police-brutality-against-trans case. BUT: one-party
  authoritarian state — "arbitrary arrests and detentions" + "ill-treatment of activists in
  prisons" (Amnesty 2024, general not trans-specific); "short-termed enforced disappearances"
  of critics (OMCT/VAC). [amnesty.org, omct.org]
- **G Law:** No criminalisation of trans identity/same-sex. LGR recognized in principle
  (Civil Code 2015) but not implemented. No anti-discrimination law. Same-sex marriage ban
  removed but not recognised. Amnesty: "LGBTI people's rights lacked meaningful protection in
  both law and practice."
- **H Detention/prison:** "Ill-treatment of activists in prisons" (Amnesty — general, not
  trans-specific). No trans-prisoner case documented. Low-confidence.
- **I Health/medication:** Healthcare discrimination documented (stigma, lack of trans-
  competent providers, red tape — Saigoneer: "Healthcare Providers Are Respectful, but Red
  Tape Still Hurts"). Gender-affirming-care access limited. No documented HRT customs issues.
  [saigoneer.com, vietnam.un.org]
- **K State & media climate (one-party, "most tolerant" but stalled + Pride cancellation):**
  DW (Sep 2025): "one of Asia's most tolerant countries toward sexual minorities." BUT: HCMC
  abruptly **cancelled its flagship Pride march + 5 LGBTQ events** (late Sep 2025) — "shocked
  activists"; "Is Vietnam turning against LGBTQ+ rights?" (DW). Hanoi Pride proceeded (local
  decision, not national policy). Amnesty: "rights lacked meaningful protection in both law
  and practice." Positive: UNDP/MOFA exploring national strategies against stigma/discrimination/
  violence; civil society active (Lighthouse, ICS, APTN); UNDP engaged on Gender Affirmation
  Law. [dw.com, amnesty.org, undp.org]

## Score rationale
0.63 — Reduced Risk, below ZAF (0.66)/SRB (0.64), above CHN (0.53), below PHL (0.69)/THA (0.77).
- Below ZAF (0.66): Vietnam has no criminalisation + no trans-specific killing (SA has
  documented hate-crime attacks + Grindr-ambush + healthcare-system violence), BUT Vietnam
  has a weaker legal framework (no Constitution equivalent, no PEPUDA, no LGR implementation,
  no anti-discrimination law) + HCMC Pride cancellation + one-party authoritarian (SA is a
  democracy with Equality Court + Constitution). SA's institutional-legal response is stronger.
- Below SRB (0.64): Vietnam's HCMC Pride cancellation + stalled LGR (8 years) + no anti-
  discrimination law + one-party authoritarian offset Vietnam's no-criminalisation + lower
  violence + "most tolerant" reputation + UNDP engagement. Serbia has documented police
  brutality (Feb 2024) which Vietnam doesn't, but Serbia's democratic civil society is more
  functional than Vietnam's one-party state.
- Above CHN (0.53): Vietnam is less hostile than China — no criminalisation (China also has
  no criminalisation, but "don't encourage/don't oppose" baseline is deteriorating under Xi:
  Pride censorship, Jin Xing ban, NGO crackdown, forced psychiatric commitment, airport
  discrimination against Thai YouTuber). Vietnam has: LGR recognized in principle (stalled,
  but exists on paper; China has LGR requiring surgery + parental consent), lower documented
  hostility, no trans-specific killing, no airport discrimination, "most tolerant" reputation,
  UNDP engagement. But Vietnam's one-party authoritarianism and stalled LGR keep it close to
  China rather than closer to Thailand/Philippines.
- Below PHL (0.69)/THA (0.77): Philippines has deeper social acceptance (bakla traditions,
  Manila Pride vibrant, CHR active) + democracy; Thailand has deep kathoey social acceptance +
  Welcome-Pride immigration + marriage equality + care-hub. Vietnam's one-party authoritarianism
  + stalled LGR + HCMC Pride cancellation + healthcare discrimination offset its "most tolerant"
  reputation + no-criminalisation.
