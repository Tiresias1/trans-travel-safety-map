# Qatar (QAT) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.14** · Band: Do Not Travel

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit (directly trans-visitor-specific arrest + conviction):** **Manuel Guerrero
  Aviña, a British-Mexican national, arrested at Doha airport** (2022 World Cup period),
  detained 6+ weeks without charge, interrogated about sexual relations, subjected to ill-
  treatment, convicted of "homosexual conduct" (6-month suspended sentence + fine — Amnesty:
  "grossly unfair trial"). A DIRECTLY trans/queer-visitor-specific arrest, detention, and
  conviction at Qatar's border. [amnesty.org]
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** No LGR; no legal recognition of trans identity.
- **C Gendered spaces:** Trans gender expression criminalised under general morality
  provisions. No specific bathroom law documented.
- **D/E Violence:** HRW (Oct 2022): Qatar Preventive Security Department "arbitrarily arrested
  LGBT people and subjected them to ill-treatment in detention" (as recently as Sept 2022,
  World Cup period). [hrw.org]
- **F Police:** Preventive Security Department forces arrest + ill-treat LGBT people (HRW).
  Guerrero Aviña case: interrogation about sexual relations + ill-treatment in detention.
- **G Law (criminalisation, applies to all incl. tourists):** Penal Code 2004: "sodomy" and
  "sexual intercourse" between same-sex — maximum **7 years' imprisonment** (both men + women
  criminalised). Human Dignity Trust: "death penalty" listed (for same-sex under Sharia
  interpretation, though its application is unclear). No LGR; no anti-discrimination law.
  [humandignitytrust.org]
- **H Detention/prison:** Guerrero Aviña detained 6+ weeks; HRW documents ill-treatment in
  detention.
- **I Health/medication:** No documented HRT-specific issues, but the general anti-LGBT climate
  + criminalisation make medication carry-in hazardous.
- **K State & media climate:** HRW World Report 2024/2025: "Qatari laws discriminate against
  women" + LGBT+ people. 2022 World Cup period: documented arrests/abuse of LGBT people by
  security forces.

## Score rationale
0.14 — Do Not Travel, ~tied with ARE (0.13), below RUS (0.12) marginally, below MYS (0.28).
- ~tied with ARE (0.13): both Gulf states with same-sex criminalisation (Qatar 7 years;
  UAE 14 years) + directly-documented trans/queer-visitor arrest (Qatar: Guerrero Aviña
  convicted; UAE: X-ray/deportation/sentencing of trans visitors). UAE has more documented
  cases (X-ray, multiple deportations, 1yr prison for cross-dressing) + higher penalty (14
  years); Qatar has the Guerrero Aviña conviction (6-month suspended) + "death penalty"
  listed (application unclear). Very close.
- Below MYS (0.28): Qatar has same-sex criminalised nationally (7 years, applies to all incl.
  tourists; Malaysia's s377A/B is 20 years but Sharia applies to Muslims + Malaysia has no
  national penal-code criminalisation equivalent to Qatar's). BUT Malaysia criminalises
  cross-dressing via Sharia (Qatar uses general morality provisions, not a specific cross-
  dressing statute). Both have documented visitor-specific arrests.
