# Latvia (LVA) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.69** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** EU + Schengen; no trans-specific border friction; foreign X accepted.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (no LGR framework):** Trans people cannot legally change their
  gender marker — there is **no legal framework for LGR** (LSM, June 2025: "a struggle for
  recognition"; Eren, a trans man who fled Russia, couldn't legally transition without
  jeopardising his marriage). Resident-facing; no tourist document-outing cases. [eng.lsm.lv]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom law; no cross-dressing statute.
- **D Public/social reaction (conservative, less-documented-extreme than Lithuania):** No
  Gražulis-equivalent "degenerated" rhetoric documented; no propaganda-style minors law;
  no systemic-trans-workplace-harassment study surfaced. But ECtHR documented a homophobic
  attack on a lecturer (Deniss Hanovs) at Kengarags market (Nov 2021). [eng.lsm.lv,
  echr.coe.int]
- **F Police (institutional failure, homophobic not trans-specific):** **ECtHR Hanovs v.
  Latvia (18 July 2024): Latvia failed to prosecute a homophobic hate crime** — violation
  of Art. 3 + 8; €10,000 compensation. A documented state failure to protect/investigate
  anti-LGBT violence. BUT: administrative court OVERTURNED a denial of asylum to a Ghanaian
  LGBTI asylum seeker (April 2025) — a positive institutional response on protection.
  [hudoc.echr.coe.int, ilga-europe.org]
- **G Law:** No criminalisation; no LGR framework (structural gap). EU/Schengen.
- **H Detention/prison:** Low-confidence (thin evidence); no documented trans-detainee case.
- **I Health/medication:** Schengen HRT carry-in uneventful.
- **K State & media climate (conservative, improving modestly):** New Hope (Jaunā Vienotība)
  progressive-led coalition; ILGA Rainbow Map mid-table. Less-extreme-documented trajectory
  than Lithuania's CC-refusal negative inflection.

## Score rationale
0.69 — above LTU (0.66), around HUN (0.68), below EST (0.77).
- Above LTU (0.66): less-extreme documented trans-specific hostility (no Gražulis "degenerate"
  rhetoric, no propaganda-style minors law, no systemic-workplace-harassment study); Latvia's
  documented institutional failure (ECtHR Hanovs) is homophobic, not trans-specific; the
  administrative-court asylum protection is a positive signal.
- ~tied with HUN (0.68): both have a structural LGR gap (Latvia: no framework; Hungary:
  ban on books post-Tisza) and conservative climate; Hungary has the BVerfG extradition-block
  (axis-H) + 16-yr-demonization-legacy (ending) vs Latvia's ECtHR hate-crime-prosecution
  failure (axis-F) + no-LGR-framework.
- Below EST (0.77): no LGR framework vs Estonia's medical-gatekept-but-existing procedure;
  no marriage equality (Estonia has it); no national action plan; ECtHR-prosecution failure.
