# Malta (MLT) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.94** · Anchor: TOP-OF-SCALE

## Key findings (with sources)
- Ranked #1 on ILGA-Europe Rainbow Map every year since 2015; 2025 still first of 49.
  Legal gender recognition by self-determination (GIGESC Act 2015): no surgery,
  sterilisation, or diagnosis needed; "X" gender markers available.
  [rainbowmap.ilga-europe.org/countries/malta/], [equaldex.com/region/malta],
  [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_rights_in_Malta]
- Constitutional-level equality; discrimination on gender identity/expression banned
  nationwide since 2004; conversion therapy banned (first EU country, 2016).
  [wikipedia], [gaylawnet.com/laws/mt.html]
- US State Dept: Malta is "a safe environment for foreign LGBT travellers"; LGBT+
  Danger Index: 10th safest in world; OSAC recognises high degree of liberty.
  [wikipedia citing State Dept]
- Vella (2025, Routledge Companion to Gender & Crime): Malta records **one of the
  lowest rates of SOGIE physical/sexual victimisation across the EU27**, but LGBTIQ+
  people report a higher share of (non-physical) harassment and online hatred;
  reporting rates to police remain low. Police force adopted hate-crime SOPs (2023/24).
  [um.edu.mt/library/oar/handle/123456789/138807], [ilga-europe.org 2023 malta.pdf]
- Trans inmates: 2024 Univ. of Malta study found prisons' culture lags national law
  (binary assumptions, healthcare gaps, social alienation of TGD inmates) — relevant
  to arrest scenarios, though risk of arrest for a law-abiding visitor is minimal.
  [um.edu.mt/library/oar/handle/123456789/142152]
- ECtHR case M.S. v. Malta (pending 2025): concerns an LGBTI *asylum seeker's*
  detention/refoulement — migrants, not tourists; noted only as institutional signal.
  [icj.org]
- Migration context: NGOs document systemic neglect/discrimination against LGBTIQ+
  migrants (Repubblika 2024-25) — residents/migrants, out of visitor scope, used only
  as hostility signal (weak: policy hostility is directed at migration status, not
  trans status).
- Social attitudes: Eurobarometer 2019: 67% support same-sex marriage; 73% support
  equal rights. MaltaToday (2018): "daily life can still be a struggle for some".
- Fetch failures (403/PDF): refworld.org GIGESC text, ILGA-Europe 2023 & 2026 Malta
  PDFs, ICJ third-party submission PDF, Amnesty 2015/16. Content available in
  summaries; no material gap.

## Visitor-focused summary
Risk to a trans visitor is minimal and comparable to the best-performing countries:
no criminalisation, broad anti-discrimination law, self-ID documents, X passports,
robust police hate-crime procedures, and one of the EU's lowest rates of physical
victimisation. The main residual risk is non-violent harassment (incl. online),
and ordinary legal-trouble scenarios (prison placement practice) lag the law on paper.
No trans-specific travel-advisory warnings found from any government source.
