# Saudi Arabia (SAU) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.05** · Anchor: BOTTOM-OF-SCALE

## Key findings (with sources)
- Uncodified Wahhabi Sharia legal system: same-sex conduct punishable in theory by
  death, flogging, and imprisonment; judges have broad discretion. Trans people and
  "cross-dressing" specifically criminalised via "imitation of the opposite sex" /
  morality provisions; police routinely arrest on gender expression. No legal gender
  recognition; gender-affirming care banned (intersex only). [legalclarity.org (2
  pages), cairo52.com, outrightinternational.org, en.wikipedia.org]
- Trans-specific death in custody: Mohammad (Pakistani trans woman, Riyadh 2017) —
  beaten to death in Azizia prison after a party of transgender Pakistanis was raided;
  35 others detained. [bbc.com/news/world-asia-39881527]
- Enforcement: 5 men arrested for cross-dressing on social media (Northern Borders,
  recent); trans women "arrested, detained, tortured and prosecuted on morality-related
  grounds" (Outright). CPVPV (morality police) active post-2016-reform.
  [gulfnews.com, outrightinternational.org]
- Tourist-facing contradiction: Saudi Tourism Authority FAQ says "everyone is welcome…
  visitors are not asked to disclose such personal details" (CNN); Vision 2030 actively
  courts tourists — but this marketing softening does not change the legal framework;
  some LGBTQ travellers have visited without incident (House of Saud, misterb&b —
  403/500 fetch fails, from summaries).
- Social-media prosecutions: Amnesty/ALQST (June 2026) document 8 visitors imprisoned
  after unfair trials for social media posts — including posts published BEFORE
  entering the Kingdom. [amnesty.org]
- US State Dept advisory: no codified penal code; fines/imprisonment/deportation/
  exit bans; citizens detained months without charge; avoid public displays of
  affection. [travel.state.gov (403; from summary)]
- UK GOV.UK: "serious penalties" incl. imprisonment and deportation for offences
  against local laws; public-decency regulations published with fine tables.
  [gov.uk, visitsaudi.com penalties PDF (from summary)]

## Visitor-focused summary
A trans visitor who is discovered faces criminal exposure via morality/“imitation”
provisions enforced by police and morality patrols, with documented torture and at
least one custodial death of a trans person; same-sex conduct is theoretically a
capital offence. Documents: no recognition whatsoever; transitioning-related content
on your phone or social media has led to visitors' imprisonment. The tourist-industry
"everyone welcome" messaging applies to discretion, not safety: the legal risk of
being discovered — or of being outed via device searches or pre-existing posts — is
among the most severe in the world.
