# Singapore (SGP) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.72** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** No trans-specific border friction; efficient immigration. Low axis-A.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy:** LGR exists but requires **genital surgery** (prohibitive cost);
  very difficult process; no self-ID. No anti-discrimination law covering SO/GI — the 2025
  Workplace Fairness Act **explicitly excludes SO/GI** from protections. No same-sex marriage
  (constitutional amendment defines marriage as man-woman). [transgendersg.com, pinkdot.sg,
  outrightinternational.org]
- **C Gendered spaces:** No bathroom restriction law; no documented friction. Low axis-C.
- **D/E Public/social reaction (stalled, softening):** TransgenderSG study (Feb 2025):
  **65%+ of trans Singaporeans report negative workplace experiences** (harassment, verbal
  abuse). Reuters (Jun 2023): "softening of attitudes and growing tolerance" post-377A repeal;
  drag performer feels "much more confident and safe." Intrepid: "progress has stalled;
  discrimination quietly tolerated." Radio code groups homosexuality with "paedophilia and
  incest" as "lifestyles" not allowed on airwaves (SCMP — institutional prejudice signal).
  No documented mob-violence, trans-tourist-rape, or dating-app-ambush. [transgendersg.com,
  reuters.com, intrepidtravel.com, scmp.com]
- **F Police:** No documented police-brutality case; very low general crime. No religious-
  police (contrast Malaysia's Jawi). Low axis-F.
- **G Law:** No criminalisation of trans identity (F&M: "No criminalization of gender
  identity or expression"). s377A repealed (Nov 2022, effective Jan 2023). BUT: no anti-
  discrimination law; marriage = man-woman (constitutional); LGR requires genital surgery;
  radio-code prejudice. [fandmglobalbarometers.org, outrightinternational.org, mha.gov.sg]
- **H Detention/prison (POSITIVE — unusual):** Straits Times: Singapore Prison Service —
  10 trans inmates; **no assaults reported**; managed housing (limited transparency but no
  documented abuse — notably BETTER than France/Germany/UK/Denmark which all have documented
  men's-prison placement of trans women + abuse). Small sample (10 inmates) but a positive
  signal. [straitstimes.com]
- **I Health/medication:** Regional medical hub; gender-affirming care available; BUT LGR
  requires genital surgery (expensive gatekeeping). HRT access available.
- **K State & media climate (conservative, softening-but-stalled):** s377A repeal a positive
  inflection; "softening attitudes." BUT: "progress has stalled"; Workplace Fairness Act
  excludes SO/GI; radio-code prejudice; "very, very difficult for us to live" (currents
  newsletter); no anti-discrimination protections. Conservative city-state, improving slowly.
