# Spain (ESP) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.90** · Anchor: TOP-OF-SCALE

## Key findings (with sources)
- Ley 4/2023 ("Trans Law"): self-ID legal gender recognition (16+, no diagnosis); broad
  anti-discrimination guarantees for LGTBI people incl. in goods/services; conversion
  therapy restricted. Organic Law 4/2023 amended Penal Code art. 22.4 (aggravating
  factor for gender identity/expression motives) and art. 510 (hate crimes extended to
  trans/LGBTI people). [boe.es/eli/es/l/2023/02/28/4], [hrw.org 2023],
  [alonsosala.com/en/blog/organic-law-4-2023...]
- Bathroom use: no legal restriction on trans people using facilities per their gender
  in Spain — contrast piece (Jul 2026) with UK post-Supreme-Court ruling; using
  gendered facilities matching presentation is legal. [euroweeklynews.com 2026-07-09]
- Enforcement is real: first transphobia-aggravation conviction in Vigo (Jul 2026,
  WhatsApp threats, €300 fine); six-month sentence for gang harassing/transphobic
  harassment of a trans person in Valencia (incl. minors); Benidorm local-police
  transphobia case proceeding to court (2020-2021, described as "intolerable" by
  regional president). [larazon.es], [observatoriodelitosdeodio...],
  [theolivepress.es (403; from summary)]
- Negatives: ILGA-Europe 2023 review: "anti-trans rhetoric continued to gain ground";
  hate crimes "a serious issue" (under-reporting, rising complaints). Madrid region
  partially revoked its regional trans/LGBTI rights laws (Dec 2023, PP) — political
  backlash signal. Torremolinos venue excluding LGBTI patrons (Jan 2025) — authorities
  (city, Junta de Andalucía, Ministry of Equality) acted against it. 
  [ilga-europe.org 2023 spain.pdf (from summary; fetch failed)], [reuters.com (403;
  from summary)], [ilga-europe.org 2026 review (from summary)]
- Tourism industry: Turespaña markets Spain as reference LGBTQ destination; Intrepid:
  "safe and welcoming", public opinion positive, more conservative in some rural
  areas. [tourspain.es], [intrepidtravel.com]
- Dating-app violence: gangs using Grindr etc. to drug/rob/assault gay tourists
  (2025-2026 reporting, incl. alleged serial killer targeting gay men). This targets
  *gay men* primarily (out of trans score scope) but is a real queer-tourist safety
  issue worth flagging as general context. [thespanisheye.com (403), theolivepress.es
  (403; both from summaries)]
- GOV.UK Spain advice: no trans-specific warnings; general ID-check and petty-crime
  guidance. [gov.uk]

## Visitor-focused summary
Very low risk. Self-ID law since 2023, comprehensive anti-discrimination protection,
gender identity explicitly covered by hate-crime law with visible enforcement (convictions
with transphobia aggravation), no restrictions on bathroom use, strong social acceptance
especially in cities/tourist areas. Residual: heated political debate and some regional
rollback (Madrid), documented harassment incidents, occasional police misconduct
(one Benidorm case), and a dating-app crime wave targeting queer tourists (mostly gay
men) in tourist zones.
