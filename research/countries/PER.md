# Peru (PER) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.48** · Band: Elevated Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** No trans-specific border friction documented; foreign documents
  recognised.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (court-based LGR, blocked, no self-ID):** LGR via court —
  "expensive, complicated, can take many years" (Outright). Advocates pushing for
  administrative self-ID since 2016 — blocked by conservative legislative majorities. No
  same-sex marriage recognition. No anti-discrimination law covering GI (Outright: a 2011
  anti-discrimination law exists but coverage of GI unclear/inconsistent).
  [outrightinternational.org, agenciapresentes.org]
- **C Gendered spaces (LEGISLATED BATHROOM BAN — nationally):** **Congress approved
  (Commission Feb 2025, Pleno Apr 2025): trans people banned from using public bathrooms
  matching their gender** — must use birth-sex bathrooms. Also bans "inclusive signage such as
  'women in their diversity'." Ombudsman criticised. IDEHPUCP: "discriminación legalizada."
  Smartfit gym case: trans woman Valensue told she must use men's bathrooms (based on DNI sex) —
  INDECOPI **dismissed** the discrimination complaint (resolution 1532-2024). A DIRECT,
  nationally-legislated axis-C restriction (comparable to US state bans / UK EHRC code, but
  national + legislated). [infobae.com, idehpucp.pucp.edu.pe, perusupportgroup.org.uk]
- **D/E Violence (documented, significant; enforcement works in severe cases):** **54 TLGBI
  assassinations 2020-2023** (~14/yr; "trans women are the most affected" — Universidad
  Peruana Cayetano Heredia observatory). **Azul Rojas Marín: 3 police officers sentenced to 17
  years for torture and sexual abuse of a trans woman** (Dec 2025 — "unprecedented for Peru
  and the Inter-American Court of Human Rights"; prosecutor sought 20 years). Discoteca case
  (Arequipa, Mar 2025): court sentenced venue staff to 1yr 9mo for denying entry to 3 trans
  women — "hito para la comunidad LGTBI" (milestone). No documented trans-TOURIST-specific
  killing. [agenciapresentes.org, bbc.com, infobae.com ×2]
- **F Police (documented torture WITH enforcement):** Azul Rojas case: 3 officers tortured +
  sexually abused a trans woman; **17-year sentence** (Dec 2025). Enforcement works in severe
  cases (unprecedented). No documented dating-app-entrapment (unlike Egypt).
- **G Law (no criminalisation but anti-trans rollback):** No criminalisation of same-sex/trans
  identity. BUT: **bathroom ban** (axis C, above) + **presidential decree classifying trans as
  "mental disorder"** (May 2024, in Essential Health Insurance Plan — walked back Jun 2024 after
  massive protests + HRW criticism) + **May 2025 law** (HRW: "threatens free speech, trans
  rights" — vague/overly broad provisions could suppress expressions of identity). Court-based
  LGR (expensive, years-long, blocked). No same-sex marriage. [hrw.org ×2, reuters.com,
  outrightinternational.org]
- **H Detention/prison:** Azul Rojas was police-station violence (not prison-placement).
  No documented prison-placement case.
- **I Health/medication:** "Mental disorder" decree (May 2024, walked back Jun 2024) caused
  confusion in healthcare access. HIV prevalence high among trans women (Springer study).
  No documented HRT customs issues.
- **K State & media climate ("anti-rights state," rollback, bathroom ban):** Presentes:
  "frente a un estado anti-derechos" (anti-rights state). Bathroom ban (Feb-Apr 2025).
  "Mental disorder" decree (May 2024, walked back after protests). May 2025 law threatening
  trans rights (HRW). "A Week of Setbacks: Gender, LGBTQ+ and Reproductive Rights Under
  Attack" (Peru Support Group Apr 2025). Conservative majorities blocking self-ID LGR since
  2016. BUT: Azul Rojas 17yr sentence (enforcement); discoteca-denial milestone; civil
  society active (Presentes, IDEHPUCP, Universidad Cayetano Heredia observatory); massive
  protests forced "mental disorder" walk-back.

## Score rationale
0.48 — Elevated Risk, below MEX (0.50), above TUR (0.47), below BRA (0.55)/COL (0.55)/CHL (0.71).
- Below MEX (0.50): Peru has a **nationally-legislated bathroom ban** (Mexico has no bathroom
  restriction law) + "anti-rights state" + "mental disorder" decree (walked back) + May 2025
  anti-trans law + no self-ID LGR (blocked since 2016) + no same-sex marriage. These offset
  Mexico's higher violence (71 trans murders/yr vs Peru's ~14/yr) + Mexico's 22-state LGR
  advantage.
- Above TUR (0.47): Peru has no criminalisation of same-sex/trans (Turkey has de facto
  criminalisation via morality laws + presidential "Year of Family" + police profiling +
  transphobic attacks). Peru's bathroom ban is severe (axis C) but Peru has no criminalisation
  + Azul Rojas 17yr sentence (enforcement) + lower violence (~14/yr vs Turkey's transphobic-
  attacks) + no passport-carry-ID-checks (Turkey has daily outing risk).
- Below BRA (0.55)/COL (0.55): Brazil/Colombia have stronger legal frameworks (STF self-ID /
  Constitutional Court self-recognition + marriage equality) despite higher violence. Peru's
  court-only-blocked LGR + bathroom ban + "anti-rights state" + no same-sex marriage are weaker.
- Below CHL (0.71): Chile has self-ID (2019) + marriage equality (2022) + positive Gendarmería
  axis-H. Peru has court-only-blocked LGR + bathroom ban + "mental disorder" decree + "anti-
  rights state" + no same-sex marriage.
