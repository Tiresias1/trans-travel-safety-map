# South Korea (KOR) — Research Note (traveler-outing axes, 2026-08-21)
Score: **0.73** · Band: Reduced Risk

## Axis findings (taxonomy A–K)
- **A Border/transit:** No trans-specific border friction; home documents recognised.
  No foreign advisories.
- **B Documents/bureaucracy (court-based LGR, surgery historically required, courts
  moving):** LGR via court; Supreme Court (Nov 2022): trans people can change legal gender
  regardless of minor children; regional court (May 2024, Yeongdong): 5 trans people allowed
  change WITHOUT surgery ("demanding surgery amounts to [against the law]"). Case-by-case,
  no legislative reform. **No comprehensive anti-discrimination law** (Outright: "does not
  have a comprehensive antidiscrimination law covering SO, gender identity"). Resident-facing.
- **C Gendered spaces (legally protected but frictional):** Court (Jul 2024): police advice
  to an LGBTQ+ person to "avoid public restrooms" ruled a **rights violation** — court
  protection exists. BUT: NHRCK flags school bathroom friction ("trans teens, even using
  school bathrooms involves questions of safety"); NHRCK: school assigning trans boy to
  girls' room = discrimination (accommodation needed); **hospital-ward "transgender blind
  spot"** (Health Ministry can't answer how to accommodate trans patients; patient rooms
  by biology). [koreatimes.co.kr, english.hani.co.kr, koreaherald.com]
- **D/E Public/social reaction (conservative, rapidly changing):** Nationwide survey (585
  TGNB young adults): 67.4% heard homophobic/transphobic remarks from teachers in secondary
  school. misterb&b: "broadly positive" for Seoul gay neighborhoods; Seoul Pride one of
  Asia's largest; "remarkable and rapid social change" + "remarkable tension." No documented
  mob-violence, trans-tourist-rape, or dating-app-ambush. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov,
  misterbandb.com, hrw.org]
- **F Police/military:** Police "advised" trans person to avoid restrooms (court struck
  it down). **Mandatory military: 12.1% of AMAB trans experienced sexual harassment or
  violence; 29% classified "maladjusted"** (conscription-specific, affects Korean citizens
  not foreign visitors). Military Criminal Act Art 92-6 criminalises same-sex military
  conduct (Constitutional Court upheld 2023) — military context, not civilian. [english.hani.co.kr,
  pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov, outrightinternational.org]
- **G Law:** No civilian criminalisation of trans identity; no same-sex marriage; no
  comprehensive anti-discrimination law; LGR via court (positive trajectory); no bathroom
  restriction law (court protected trans restroom access). Military same-sex-conduct
  criminalisation (not relevant to foreign visitors).
- **H Detention/prison:** Hospital-ward placement friction (NHRCK); no documented prison
  case. Low-confidence.
- **I Health/medication:** No documented HRT customs friction.
- **K State & media climate (conservative, changing rapidly):** No marriage equality; no
  anti-discrimination law; BUT growing Pride/visibility; NHRCK active; courts protective.
  2024 martial-law crisis (Yoon impeachment) — political instability, not trans-specific.

## Score rationale
0.73 — Reduced Risk, below TWN (0.80) and THA (0.77), ~tied with CZE (0.74)/POL (0.72).
- Below TWN (0.80): no marriage equality (Taiwan has it), no anti-discrimination law (gap),
  conservative society (67.4% school transphobia), institutional friction (hospital-ward
  blind spot, school bathrooms), military sexual-harassment data.
- Below THA (0.77): no kathoey cultural-acceptance equivalent; no Welcome-Pride
  immigration campaign; no anti-discrimination law; military conscription issues. Thailand's
  deep social acceptance + visitor infrastructure exceed Korea's growing-but-contentious
  visibility.
- ~tied with CZE (0.74)/POL (0.72): similar conservative-but-changing + institutional-
  friction profile. Korea has no documented severe visitor-incident (cleaner than
  Italy/Greece/Serbia), but its **no-anti-discrimination-law** gap (unlike EU peers which
  have EU anti-discrimination law) + conservative baseline offset. Courts are protective
  (Korea Times restroom ruling; Supreme Court LGR) + Seoul Pride large + no criminalisation.
