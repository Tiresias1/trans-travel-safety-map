# Iceland (ISL) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.95** · Anchor: TOP-OF-SCALE

## Key findings (with sources)
- Act on Gender Autonomy No. 80/2019: self-defined gender registration (age 15+, no
  diagnosis/surgery); non-binary registration; informed-consent healthcare model.
  X markers on documents. [government.is (JS-rendered, content from search summary)],
  [outrightinternational.org/.../iceland], [equaldex.com/region/iceland]
- Equal treatment outside the labour market prohibited on gender identity/expression/
  sexual characteristics (Act on Equal Treatment Outside the Labour Market). Gender
  Equality Act no. 150/2020 covers registered-neutral gender. [government.is],
  [equalitylaw.eu country report 2024 (PDF fetch failed; from summary)]
- Pink Iceland (Nov 2023) — dedicated trans/non-binary TRAVELLER guide: "as a trans or
  non-binary traveller, you shouldn't have to be worried about your safety";
  misgendering in hospitality usually unintentional; most swimming pools in the
  capital area offer private/gender-neutral changing rooms (naked showering norm
  before pools); using binary-coded changing rooms is legal and common; two queer
  venues in Reykjavík, most bars queer-friendly. [pinkiceland.is]
- Hate crime: no standalone hate-crime offence; motive (incl. gender identity) is an
  aggravating sentencing factor (General Penal Code art. 70, as amended 2022); hate
  speech explicitly criminalised. Potential-hate-crime reports small but rising:
  16 in 2023, 11 in 2024, 7 by mid-2025; Samtökin '78 calls for stronger legislation;
  no systematic national hate-crime statistics. [hatecrime.osce.org/...legislation-iceland],
  [112.is/en/abuse/hate-crime], [icelandreview.com (Queer Rights Group...)]
- GOV.UK Iceland advice: LGBT+ travellers section is purely factual-positive (same-sex
  activity legal, relationships recognised); no trans-specific warning.
  [gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/iceland/safety-and-security]
- OECD (2025): legal LGBTI inclusivity strong; above OECD average on discrimination
  protections. [oecd.org (PDF fetch failed; from summary)]
- 112.is (emergency services): dedicated pages on hate crime and abuse against queer
  people — institutional support exists. [112.is]
- ILGA-Europe Annual Review 2025 (PDF fetch failed; from summary): asylum
  restrictions noted — migrants, out of visitor scope.
- Note: "Iceland Prepares Guidelines for LGBTQ+ Travellers" (Iceland Review) concerns
  Icelanders travelling ABROAD, not visitors to Iceland — not scored.

## Visitor-focused summary
Extremely low risk: self-ID law, X markers, broad equal-treatment law, very low
general crime, dedicated institutional support, and a local travel industry that
actively accommodates trans visitors (gender-neutral facilities widely available).
Residuals: small but rising numbers of potential hate-crime reports (low absolute
levels), no standalone hate-crime offence, and occasional misgendering.
