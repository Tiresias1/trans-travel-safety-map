# Afghanistan (AFG) — Research Note
Researched: 2026-08-21 · Score: **0.02** · Anchor: ABSOLUTE BOTTOM

## Key findings (with sources)
- Same-sex conduct criminalised (Penal Code 2017 sodomy provisions, max 2 years) BUT
  under Taliban rule the applicable standard is Sharia, with death penalty for
  same-sex relations (Taliban statements + historical practice: stonings, wall-crushing).
  Human Dignity Trust: "maximum penalty of death… evidence of enforcement in recent
  years." [humandignitytrust.org (403; from summary), reuters.com (401; from summary),
  dw.com, database.ilga.org (from summary)]
- PVPV "Morality Law" (31 Jul 2024, codified Aug 2024): Article 22 lists "lesbianism"
  and "homosexuality" among "specific evils" overseers must prevent; morality ministry
  enforces; UN experts (Mar 2026) condemn "surging" public corporal punishment incl.
  for "moral crimes"; Supreme Court Jan 2026 escalation. [afghanistanjustice.org
  translation + decree page, ohchr.org (403; from summary), info-res.org PVPV report
  (PDF fail; from summary)]
- Taliban practice against LGBTIQ people: HRW 2022 report ("Even If You Go to the
  Skies, We'll Find You") — beatings, sexual violence by Taliban commanders; Outright
  2023 ("A Mountain on My Shoulders") — systematic persecution; CNN Nov 2024 —
  widespread sexual/physical violence in detention, forced confessions; Rainbow
  Afghanistan documentation of torture, forced anal examinations. [hrw.org (fetched),
  outrightinternational.org, cnn.com (too large; from summary), jurist.org]
- Trans-specific: DW (2021): trans Afghans in hiding, "face the death penalty";
  EUAA guidance: LGBTIQ persons (incl. trans) at risk of persecution/death; targeted
  house searches; families denounce members. [dw.com, euaa.europa.eu (fetched, both
  guidance pages)]
- UK CPIN: LGBTI people among priority groups at risk from Taliban; very strong
  grounds to depart from pre-2021 findings. [gov.uk CPIN PDF (from summary)]
- Visitor relevance: Afghanistan is effectively closed to tourism; the de facto
  regime has detained foreign nationals as leverage; anyone perceived as violating
  Islamic norms (incl. gender presentation) is subject to morality-law enforcement.

## Visitor-focused summary
The most hostile environment on the scale for anyone trans: the Taliban regime
codified "homosexuality" and "lesbianism" as evils its morality ministry must prevent,
applies the death penalty under its interpretation of Sharia, and its practice against
LGBTIQ people includes documented torture and sexual violence in detention. Trans
Afghans hide in windowless rooms to survive; the regime's own judges apply escalating
public corporal punishment for moral crimes. Any trans visitor discovered — through
appearance, documents, or device searches — could not expect any form of protection
and would face a detention system where sexual assault of LGBTIQ detainees is routine.
