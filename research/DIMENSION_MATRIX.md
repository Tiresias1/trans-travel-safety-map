# DIMENSION MATRIX — mandatory coverage spec (2026-10-07 pilot)

Every record (country or dossier region) must answer EVERY dimension with
either (a) a sourced finding, or (b) an explicit "no source found" marker.
Silence is not an answer. The US pilot exists to calibrate this: the most
documented country on earth missed its entire federal-custody story because
research was event-driven, not dimension-driven.

Dimensions (id — what a trans VISITOR faces):

D1  criminalisation — same-sex-conduct statutes, penalties, enforcement
    reality, police leverage (extortion/exposure/detention)
D2  expression — laws or enforced norms targeting gender expression/presentation
D3  documents & LGR — marker-change rules, passport/ID policy as it affects
    entry and movement
D4  entry & visa — visa categories, admission rules, traveller-specific bans
    or markings
D5  border & screening — airport/border screening practice, outing risk,
    detention of travellers, refoulement/transit exposure
D6  facilities — bathroom/changing-facility law and enforcement
D7  custody — police + prison + immigration detention: placement rules,
    medical care (hormones) in custody, documented abuse; what happens if a
    visitor is arrested
D8  care access — gender-affirming care availability, funding bans, hospital
    practice, what a visitor needing care faces (INCLUDING trans youth:
    minors travel, carry medication, and are a major political target; youth-
    care bans and record-collection campaigns are visitor-relevant to families
    and to the climate they signal)
D9  violence & hate crime — pattern data, enforcement, prosecutions
D10 discrimination law — employment/housing/services protections and their
    enforcement machinery
D11 state & media hostility — official rhetoric, institutional targeting
    (investigations, bounties, terrorism designations), censorship, press climate
D12 partner recognition — marriage/partnership recognition as it reaches
    travellers and the signal it sends
D13 youth & education — school/sports/education rules touching trans youth
    (travelling students, exchange, sports participation), and the youth-
    specific political battleground (recording how far the state goes against
    minors signals overall hostility)

For ADM1 dossier regions: D3/D4/D5 (and any parent-managed dimension) inherit
the parent's finding automatically (the parent framework layer); the region
researches only LOCAL deltas and the dimensions it locally governs.
