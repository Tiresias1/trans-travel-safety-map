# PREREGISTRATION — rule 10b compatibility audit
Written before any measurement. 2026-11-13.

## Estimand ambiguity resolved first (review instruction)
The score is **conditional on discovery**, with ONE explicit exception: where the state
engineers discovery (mandatory marker disclosure, registration, surveillance, checkpoint
checking), that engineering is itself rated as exposure. Strictly conditional otherwise; the
probability of avoiding discovery earns no credit. Rule 10b now says exactly this, and the
methodology repeats it.

## Why an audit rather than a full re-derivation
Rule 10b sharpens what rule 9 already said ("severity dominates probability of outing") in a
direction the dossiers already push (visitor-focused, anonymised, rarely describing
pass-ability). It is therefore a candidate clarification, not a proven re-write. But that is a
hypothesis to test, not a conclusion.

## Design
Sample, stratified as the review requested:
- 8 countries whose dossiers describe engineered discovery (registration, marker disclosure,
  checkpoint checking, surveillance).
- 6 countries where passing or discretion could plausibly have influenced a judgement (visible
  third-gender populations, small or highly visible jurisdictions, high documentation friction).
- 6 countries sampled at random from the remaining pool.
- 8 divisions from parents with engineered-discovery mechanisms.

Each target rated under the CURRENT rubric (r2026-11-13) with 16 pairwise encounters, blind, no
writes, then compared with its stored score (produced under r2026-11-12).

Cost: 28 targets x 16 pairs ≈ 448 pairs.

## Prespecified materiality
Compatibility is considered NOT established, and targeted re-rating triggered, if any holds:
  (a) mean absolute shift between new-rubric ratings and stored scores exceeds 0.03;
  (b) any stratum shows a mean signed shift exceeding 0.02 in one direction (systematic change);
  (c) any individual target shifts by more than 0.08.
Otherwise compatibility is reported as supported within the audit's scope, with the noise floor
stated, and scores stay stamped r2026-11-12 with compatibility noted as tested-but-not-proven.

## What this cannot do
It samples; it does not prove every score unchanged. A clean audit supports a scoped
compatibility claim only.
