# Development instructions: country and ADM1 scoring

Purpose: obtain defensible, reasonably comparable scores without allowing ADM1 scores to overwrite country scores or silently introducing a new scale. These instructions incorporate the audit and the independent method review in this directory. They are a development handoff, **not approval to roll out a new scoring method**.

## Decisions and constraints already given by the user

1. A country receives its own independently assessed country score. **Never compute it from ADM1 scores**, directly or indirectly. For blind country comparisons, relevant additional regional restrictions/protections may be summarised as evidence, subject to a reviewed general method. No regional score enters the national scoring calculation.
2. Every ADM1 comparison must start with the **full national comparison level**, then evaluate applicable local restrictions, protections and enforcement. Do not confuse the national level with a few mentions of federal law in display prose. How to encode the level and avoid counting national evidence twice remains a design decision for raters/reviewers.
3. ADM1 scores should be mutually comparable. A rough comparison between their distribution and the independent national score is a **diagnostic**, not a mandatory mean, a recentering target, or grounds to change the country. Explain meaningful departures; do not make all divisions equal to, or lower than, their parent.
4. Assess trans-specific risk to visitors, not general danger or unrelated LGBQ issues. The bottom endpoint **0.00** is an outed trans visitor facing near-certain imprisonment **and likely execution**. Intermediate points deliberately lack fixed definitions. Do not add new numerical anchors or present band labels as endpoint definitions. Do not credit passing or stealth.
5. Distinguish curiosity/social friction from hostility portraying trans people as threats, and from state threat-framing. Assess actual scope, operation, consequences and visitor relevance; don't substitute keyword counts for evidence.
6. Changes begin with a **small reviewed subset including the US**. Do not perform a full-country or 3,241-division re-derivation until a pilot and its methodology are reviewed and approved. No scores may be patched to hit a desired outcome.
7. Do not put development, provisional, audit or calibration language into the site interface. Do not change the user-approved US 0.330 while investigating. No Grok or Muse. Use cheap agents such as MiMo V2.6 Flash at medium thinking for code and routine evidence work. No regex prose surgery. One git writer; never commit credentials.

## What has been established, and what has not

- The production anchored ADM1 lane compares a blinded national text bundle plus division text against fixed foreign anchors; its score is the anchor score plus a paired rating difference. **It does not numerically start from the parent country's score.** The parent bundle omits the out-of-scope field and caps source summaries at eight. This is a construction mismatch with item 2, not proof that every individual regional score is wrong. See `adm1-trace.md` and the historical source at commit `3d3fb7a2`.
- The archived 39 US country-level encounter ratings average 0.264359 and range 0.10–0.48. The user-approved 0.330 is a separate decision, not that archived mean. The earlier four-model assessments matter: three suggested a higher US number and one a lower number, but they shared a dossier and were shown the country and existing scores. The later Sol opinion was prompted to regard the higher range as settled. Read `prior-us-reviews.md`; retain dissent.
- The non-publishing current-prompt pilot returned 17/18 usable results. Against the same comparator USA was rated 0.35 and 0.13 in opposite-order calls. This suggests a stability problem worth testing, but **one call per order cannot separate order effects from random draw variation**. See `pilot-results.ndjson`, `pilot-metadata.json`, `pilot-run.txt` and `HANDOFF.md`. The prior Nepal/UK direct-rating battery is a different instrument and does not prove a correct gap.
- `AUDIT.md` and `FINDING.md` in the prior Nepal/UK experiment now carry retractions. Different national and division averages do not by themselves establish miscalibration, scope inflation or missing propagation.
- The user's country-score rule is not yet universal: ten countries are in the staged subset; 133 still have country scores derived from division means. **Do not represent the work as finished.** The US override currently makes the archived-log verifier fail; do not claim 3,474/3,474 reproduce or relabel the override as a logged rating.

## Phase 1 — country-level diagnostic, no score writes

- Audit the 39 archived US country comparisons, correctly mapping prompt A/B through `flip`. Examine partner choice, dossier inputs, claims and scope, and whether similar exposures receive comparable reasoning. Report row IDs, source support and counterexamples. Distinguish partner/context sensitivity, presentation-order effects, stochasticity, factual scope, and weighting; do not assume which one caused the US result.
- Consult raters/reviewers neutrally on a small pilot design. Include US, UK and Nepal; use clearly documented comparator-selection criteria rather than selecting countries to obtain desired rankings. Repeat both presentation orders with identical dossiers/model/rubric first; later change one factor at a time. Consider an isolated-country condition and matched examples. Preserve every failure, exact rendered input/output, order, model/settings, evidence version, usage and hash. Specify sample, budget, decision criteria and stopping rule **before** running; obtain required review/approval.
- Report repeat dispersion and reversals rather than declaring a precise truth from averages of correlated models. Compare with the earlier US opinions, including the dissent, without treating either 0.264 or 0.330 as a required result. The initial pilot is a diagnostic, not a replacement instrument.

## Phase 2 — ADM1 construction in shadow mode

- Investigate and propose a national-level-plus-local-assessment interface for reviewer/user decision. The independent country score and complete national evidence are the starting point; local evidence is evaluated under the same rubric. Specify how the national comparison level enters, how the direction and size of any local difference are obtained, and how to avoid charging regional facts twice when they also appear in national evidence. **Do not assume an additive formula is automatically valid.**
- Do not simply restore the superseded in-country lane: first inspect its actual prompts, assumptions and the contradiction noted in `adm1-trace.md` (it supplies a number but also claims no existing scores are given). Keep existing anchored results for comparison, not as automatically correct targets.
- Evaluate a no-local-change control; US California/Texas as contrasting local examples; England for devolved jurisdiction; and Bagmati as thin-evidence handling. Include examples from other countries after reviewers select a justified sample. Preserve comparable presentation, order-swaps/repeats, uncertainty and failures. Keep outputs separate from published data and use stable division IDs, not names.
- Assess relative placement across countries using matched exposure profiles and blinded cross-checks, not randomly selected incomparable partners alone. A foreign-anchor ladder may reduce partner-selection noise but its paired-difference arithmetic and comparable-interval assumptions require testing; fixed anchors alone do not prove bias cancels.
- Review whether results consistently carry the national conditions while allowing genuine local deviations. A roughly similar ADM1 average is a **signal** for investigation, not a hard requirement; distribution summaries depend on the chosen weighting and must never be fed back into the country score.

## Phase 3 — decisions and staged rollout

- First present source-evidence findings, shadow results and residual uncertainty to reviewers using neutral questions and the full prior evidence. Return material methodological choices to the user, including how to represent the parent level and how to summarise local effects in national blind dossiers.
- Only after approval, implement a bounded subset with tests for: no ADM1-to-country scoring path; repeatable parent inputs; no missing/fabricated evidence; no silent comparison failures; stable IDs; country and ADM1 provenance; preservation of intentional overrides; and score/rank/band consistency. Never ship a one-off case correction as the general method.
- Keep the original national and ADM1 outputs, exact configs, and rollback artefacts. Expand only after the subset is reviewed. Do not trigger an unnecessary full rerun just because a prompt or code version changes; assess material compatibility first.

## Display text is separate work

Review each original/proposed text for all local substance, numbers, names, context and style before applying. Keep long source text rather than silently truncating; eliminate internal/model self-reference via LLM rewrites, never regex substitution. The 36 records without `displaySummary` still fall back to the full `summary`; address them without inventing facts or weakening verification. No country or ADM1 scoring changes belong in this lane.
