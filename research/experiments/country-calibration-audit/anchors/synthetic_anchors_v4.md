Purpose: calibrate visitor-specific transgender risk under the revised endpoint. Date: 2026-11-12. Author model: meta/muse-ember. Every jurisdiction, institution, case and finding below is fictional. The endpoint is: imprisonment highly likely after discovery, with the potential for execution.

## Target 0.35

### 0.35-A

Enforcement is routine and discretionary, with private airlines required to verify registration status before boarding. The consequence is exclusion and humiliation rather than imprisonment, yet appeals go to the same agency and are almost never granted. Uncertainty remains because the register criteria are unpublished, but refusals are consistent for visibly transgender travelers. Legal advocates say the rule applies universally to foreign visitors and leaves no effective remedy for those turned away.

### 0.35-B

Courts formally accept complaints, but judges defer to police testimony and dismiss claims for lack of proof.
