## Review

**An honestly attributed independent AI review is a reasonable pre-outcome procedural amendment for this narrowly mechanical probe check.** A human is not technically necessary to inspect a response envelope, arithmetic, actual reported usage/cost, and file hashes. The stated delegation context supports that approach, but this advice **does not itself establish user authorization**.

The existing “human review” requirement remains binding until explicitly amended. The fact that code cannot distinguish human from AI does not permit silently substituting one for the other.

The smallest defensible amendment would:
- Change only eligibility for **operational probe review**, before scoring, documenting the rationale and preserving the previous wording/history.
- Require a fresh reviewer distinct from the writer and coordinator, with explicit **AI attribution**, model/session identity, reviewed artifact hashes, findings, and scope.
- Preserve both fixed probes, no retries, shared attempt/spend accounting, unknown-liability stops, source-preflight requirements, and all other gates.
- Update bound procedural documents and regenerate/review affected receipt hashes; never describe AI review as human sign-off.
- Require review of actual archived responses and billing fields—not merely the writer’s summary. Missing, inconsistent, or ambiguous billing must stop progression.

### Boundaries

This substitution does **not** authorize:
- **Substantive source verification:** that requires its own claim-level evidence review and acceptance criteria; a transport probe establishes nothing about factual completeness.
- **Unknown-charge reconciliation:** do not clear a liability based on another model’s inference. Obtain authoritative billing/account evidence and retain the existing human/operator reconciliation requirement.
- **Score publication or methodological changes:** neither follows from a schema check. Those remain governed by their separate approvals.
- **Claims of verified provider behavior:** successful probes establish observations about those requests only, not future routing, token overhead, or prices.

**Residual risk:** “Independent” AI sessions can share model biases and miss the same defects. Mechanical checks should therefore be machine-validated where practical, with independent artifact inspection as a supplement. Honest attribution and bounded claims reduce—not eliminate—that risk.

**OK with notes for the proposed procedural amendment only; not permission to execute or score.**
