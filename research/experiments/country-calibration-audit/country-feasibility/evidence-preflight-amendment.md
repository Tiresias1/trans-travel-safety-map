## 3. Scope and limits of anything this diagnostic can support

This exception applies **only** to the exact frozen four-country inputs and a
bounded, **non-publishing methodological feasibility diagnostic**. It does not
certify comprehensive factual accuracy. Unchecked claims above remain **source
assertions, not established findings**. Diagnostic outputs cannot substantiate
destination **scores, rankings, orderings, travel advice or country
comparisons**; no target score or ordering may be chosen, and no revision or
interpretation may be selected to obtain a preferred score or order.
Abstention (`status: insufficient_evidence, score: null`) is preserved. Outputs
describe instrument behavior on these inputs — they are not validated statements
about destination conditions, and no source-certainty claim may exceed what the
evidence supports.

## 4. Stop rules and output review (pre-committed)

* **Stop** on any material contradiction, lost qualification, misleading legal
  or factual certainty, attribution error, truncation, or score/ranking leakage
  discovered in or before the diagnostic — resolve only under a new frozen
  version (rebuild + re-hash), never mid-run.
* **Outputs must be reviewed for misuse of uncertain claims** before any use:
  single-sourced allegations presented as fact, dropped qualifications, upgraded
  certainty, or any conversion of disclosed uncertainty into either safety or
  danger. Review questions are fixed in advance: does any output rely on an
  unchecked claim as established, strip an attribution or unknown, assert a
  ranking/order, or contradict a frozen qualification?
* The follow-up review requires exact-input/hash verification, the amendment and
  unresolved-claim register (Section 2) plus an independent scope sign-off to
  exist before outcomes, and a fresh independent procedural sign-off under the
  existing integrity controls. **No old receipt may be reused and this audit may
  not be relabelled "exhaustive."** If a qualification must be added to
  provider-visible context, that is a new frozen input version, not a mid-run
  change. Other execution, budget and probe gates are outside this amendment and
  remain untouched.
