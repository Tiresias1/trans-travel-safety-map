<p id="mwEw">Lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer (LGBTQ) persons in  <b id="mwFA">Vanuatu</b> may face legal challenges not experienced by non-<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ" title="LGBTQ" class="mw-redirect" id="mwFQ">LGBTQ</a> residents. Same-sex sexual activity is legal, but households headed by same-sex couples are not eligible for the same legal protections available to opposite-sex married couples. In November 2024, legislative action certified a ban on any religious, civil and traditional marriages between two people of the same sex.<sup about="#mwt18" class="mw-ref reference" <p id="mwIA">In 2011, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu" title="Vanuatu" id="mwIQ">Vanuatu</a> signed the "<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_rights_at_the_United_Nations" title="LGBT rights at the United Nations" class="mw-redirect" id="mwIg">joint statement on ending acts of violence and related human rights violations based on sexual orientation and gender identity</a>" at the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations" title="United Nations" id="mwIw">United Nations</a>, condemning violence and discrimination against LGBTQ people.<sup about="#mwt24" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-4" id="mwJA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwJQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJg">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwKA">VPride Foundation is a Ni-Vanuatu human rights group that advocates for LGBTQ rights, freedom of speech and freedom of religion. The group, established in 2009, has organised many events to raise awareness of LGBTQ people in Vanuatu. Some of these events have been attended by government officials.<sup about="#mwt27" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-sista_5-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"sista"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-sista-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-sista-5" id="mwKQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwKg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKw">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwLA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" id="mwLQ" data-mw='{"autoGenerated":true}'/></section><section data-mw-section-id="1" id="mwLg" aria-labelledby="Law_regarding_same-sex_sexual_activity"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Law_regarding_same-sex_sexual_activity">Law regarding same-sex sexual activity</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vanuatu&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Law regarding same-sex sexual activity"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwLw">Same-sex sexual activity is legal in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu" title="Vanuatu" id="mwMA">Vanuatu</a>. Since the Penal Code (Amendment) Act 2006 commenced in 2007, the age of consensual sex in Vanuatu has been 15 years regardless of sex or sexual orientation.<sup about="#mwt28" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Submission_1-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Submission"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Submission-1" id="mwMQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwMg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwMw">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwNA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="2" id="mwNQ" aria-labelledby="Recognition_of_same-sex_relationships"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Recognition_of_same-sex_relationships">Recognition of same-sex relationships</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vanuatu&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Recognition of same-sex relationships"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<span class="mw-empty-elt" about="#mwt29" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwNg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"main","href":"./Template:Main"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"Recognition of same-sex unions in Vanuatu"}},"i":0}}]}'><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1368532237" typeof="mw:Extension/templatestyles" about="#mwt30" data-mw='{"name":"templatestyles","attrs":{"src":"Module:Hatnote/styles.css"},"body":{"extsrc":""}}'>.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+.mw-empty-elt+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}@media print{body.ns-0 .mw-parser-output .hatnote{display:none!important}}</style></span><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable" about="#mwt29" id="mwNw">Main article: <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recognition_of_same-sex_unions_in_Vanuatu" title="Recognition of same-sex unions in Vanuatu">Recognition of same-sex unions in Vanuatu</a></div>
<p id="mwOA">Vanuatu does not recognise same-sex unions in any form.<sup about="#mwt33" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwOQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwOg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwOw">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwPA">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt35" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-7" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-7"}}'><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwPQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwPg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwPw">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwQA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwQQ">In October 2013, the Minister for Internal Affairs issued a warning which said that pastors are not allowed to preside over same-sex marriages.<sup about="#mwt38" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-8" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-8"}}'><a href="#cite_note-8" id="mwQg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwQw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRA">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwRg">In June 2014, the Vanuatu Law Commission discussed the issue of same-sex marriage, noting developments in neighbouring <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia" title="Australia" id="mwRw">Australia</a> and especially <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand" title="New Zealand" id="mwSA">New Zealand</a>. The Commission noted that the <i id="mwSQ">Marriage Act (Cap 60)</i> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language" title="French language" about="#mwt39" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwSg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"langx","href":"./Template:Langx"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"fr"},"2":{"wt":"Loi sur le mariage (chap. 60)"}},"i":0}}]}'>French</a><span about="#mwt39">: </span><i lang="fr" about="#mwt39">Loi sur le mariage (chap. 60)</i><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_French-language_text" about="#mwt39" id="mwSw"/>; <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bislama_language" title="Bislama language" about="#mwt40" typeof="mw:Transclusion" class="mw-redirect" id="mwTA" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"langx","href":"./Template:Langx"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"bi"},"2":{"wt":"Mared Akt (Japta 60)"}},"i":0}}]}'>Bislama</a><span about="#mwt40">: </span><i lang="bi" about="#mwt40">Mared Akt (Japta 60)</i><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_Bislama-language_text" about="#mwt40" id="mwTQ"/>) does not in its current form prohibit the recognition of same-sex marriages (neither does it expressly permit them). It also stated that legalizing same-sex marriage would allow LGBTQ people to "fulfil their sexual preference or sexual orientation without repression or fear of being prosecuted", but found that religious opposition to legalization would be high.<sup about="#mwt43" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-9" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-9"}}'><a href="#cite_note-9" id="mwTg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwTw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwUA">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwUQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwUg">In November 2024, legislative action certified a ban on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage" title="Same-sex marriage" id="mwUw">same-sex marriage</a> - regardless of religious, civil or traditional type marriages.<sup about="#mwt44" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-qnews.com.au_3-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"qnews.com.au"}}'><a href="#cite_note-qnews.com.au-3" id="mwVA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwVQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwVg">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwVw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="3" id="mwWA" aria-labelledby="Discrimination_protections"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Discrimination_protections">Discrimination protections</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vanuatu&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Discrimination protections"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwWQ">There is no legal protection against all forms of discrimination based on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwWg">sexual orientation</a> or <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_identity" title="Gender identity" id="mwWw">gender identity</a>.<sup about="#mwt47" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-10" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-10"}}'><a href="#cite_note-10" id="mwXA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwXQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXg">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwYA">There is no general prohibition of employment discrimination based on sexual orientation in Vanuatu. However, Section 18.2(f) of the <i id="mwYQ">Teaching Service Act 2013</i> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language" title="French language" about="#mwt48" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwYg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"langx","href":"./Template:Langx"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"fr"},"2":{"wt":"Loi de 2013 sur les services d&apos;enseignements"}},"i":0}}]}'>French</a><span about="#mwt48">: </span><i lang="fr" about="#mwt48">Loi de 2013 sur les services d'enseignements</i><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_French-language_text" about="#mwt48" id="mwYw"/>) establishes the obligation of the Vanuatu Teaching Service Commission not to discriminate in recruitment, promotion, professional development, transfer and all other aspects of the management of its employees on the basis of "sexual preference".<sup about="#mwt51" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Teaching_11-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Teaching"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-Teaching-11"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Teaching-11" id="mwZA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwZQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZg">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt52" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-12" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-12"}}'><a href="#cite_note-12" id="mwaA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwaQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwag">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwbA">The <i id="mwbQ">Right to Information Act 2016</i> (<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language" title="French language" about="#mwt53" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwbg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"langx","href":"./Template:Langx"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"fr"},"2":{"wt":"Loi de 2016 sur le droit à l&apos;information"}},"i":0}}]}'>French</a><span about="#mwt53">: </span><i lang="fr" about="#mwt53">Loi de 2016 sur le droit à l'information</i><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_French-language_text" about="#mwt53" id="mwbw"/>; <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bislama_language" title="Bislama language" about="#mwt54" typeof="mw:Transclusion" class="mw-redirect" id="mwcA" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"langx","href":"./Template:Langx"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"bi"},"2":{"wt":"Raet blong Information Akt blong 2016"}},"i":0}}]}'>Bislama</a><span about="#mwt54">: </span><i lang="bi" about="#mwt54">Raet blong Information Akt blong 2016</i><link rel="mw:PageProp/Category" href="./Category:Articles_containing_Bislama-language_text" about="#mwt54" id="mwcQ"/>) gives Vanuatu citizens the right to access government-held documents, including documents held by ministries and government departments, statutory and regulatory bodies, public hospitals, local government councils, and any organizations providing public service and receiving government funds. Certain types of documents not available to the public include those containing "personal information", which is defined by the Act as "[...] information relating to the race, gender, sex, marital status, national, ethnic or social origin, colour, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwcg">sexual orientation</a>, age, physical or mental health, well-being, disability, religion, conscience, belief, culture, language and birth of the person; [...]".<sup about="#mwt57" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-13" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-13"}}'><a href="#cite_note-13" id="mwcw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwdA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwdQ">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwdg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwdw">Section 2(2)(j) of the <i id="mweA">Harmful Digital Communications Act 2024</i> states that a digital communication must not denigrate an individual by reason of his or her colour, race, ethnic, island or national origins, political affiliation, religion, gender, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mweQ">sexual orientation</a>, or disability.<sup about="#mwt60" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-harmful_digital_communicatons_act_14-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"harmful digital communicatons act"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-harmful_digital_communicatons_act-14"}}'><a href="#cite_note-harmful_digital_communicatons_act-14" id="mweg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwew"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwfA">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwfQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<section data-mw-section-id="4" id="mwfg" aria-labelledby="Public_Prosecutor_protections"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Public_Prosecutor_protections">Public Prosecutor protections</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vanuatu&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Public Prosecutor protections"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwfw">There are some protections based on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwgA">sexual orientation</a> and sexual preference issued by The Office of the Public Prosecutor:</p>

<ul id="mwgQ"><li id="mwgg">The Prosecution Policy 2003, states: "The Public Prosecutor may not let his personal views of the ethnic or national origin, gender, religious beliefs, political views or sexual preference of an offender, victim or witness influence his decisions."<sup about="#mwt63" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-prosecution_guidelines_15-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"prosecution guidelines"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-prosecution_guidelines-15"}}'><a href="#cite_note-prosecution_guidelines-15" id="mwgw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwhA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwhQ">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwhg">]</span></span></a></sup></li>
<li id="mwhw">The Prosecutors Code (Gazette no. 13 of 2017) states: "Prosecutors must also have regard to whether the offence was motivated by any form of discrimination against the victim’s ethnic or national origin, gender, disability, age, religion or belief, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwiA">sexual orientation</a> or <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_identity" title="Gender identity" id="mwiQ">gender identity</a>; or the suspect demonstrated hostility towards the victim based on any of those characteristics. The presence of any such motivation or hostility will mean that it is more likely that prosecution is required. In deciding whether a prosecution is required in the public interest, prosecutors should take into account the views expressed by the victim about the impact that the offence has had. In appropriate cases, this may also include the views of the victim’s family."<sup about="#mwt66" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-prosecutors_code_16-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"prosecutors code"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-prosecutors_code-16"}}'><a href="#cite_note-prosecutors_code-16" id="mwig"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwiw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwjA">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwjQ">]</span></span></a></sup></li>
<li id="mwjg">The General Principles for obtaining the best evidence from vulnerable witnesses to Sexual and Gender-Based Violence offences, states: "Vulnerable witnesses should not be discriminated against, irrespective of their race, colour, religion, beliefs, age, family status, culture, language, ethnicity, national or social origin, citizenship, gender, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwjw">sexual orientation</a>, political or other opinions, disability, status of birth, property or other condition. Professionals working in or with the criminal justice sector should be aware of individual differences that can impact a person’s ability to fully participate in the criminal justice process."<sup about="#mwt67" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-prosecution_guidelines_15-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"prosecution guidelines"}}'><a href="#cite_note-prosecution_guidelines-15" id="mwkA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwkQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkg">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkw">]</span></span></a></sup></li></ul>

</section></section><section data-mw-section-id="5" id="mwlA" aria-labelledby="Social_conditions"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Social_conditions">Social conditions</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vanuatu&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Social conditions"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwlQ">Vanuatu is a socially conservative country. Gay and lesbian travellers (and citizens) are advised to avoid public displays of affection. There is no active gay scene in Vanuatu.<sup about="#mwt70" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-17" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-17"}}'><a href="#cite_note-17" id="mwlg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwlw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwmA">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwmQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwmg">The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Day_Against_Homophobia,_Transphobia_and_Biphobia" title="International Day Against Homophobia, Transphobia and Biphobia" class="mw-redirect" id="mwmw">International Day Against Homophobia, Transphobia and Biphobia</a> was first celebrated in Vanuatu in 2016. The event was organised by VPride Foundation.<sup about="#mwt73" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-18" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-18"}}'><a href="#cite_note-18" id="mwnA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwnQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwng">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwnw">]</span></span></a></sup> In October 2016, the group, in correlation with other human rights groups including <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanuatu_Women's_Centre" title="Vanuatu Women's Centre" id="mwoA">Vanuatu Women's Centre</a>, held a workshop on LGBTQ rights and women's rights. The Minister of Health attended the event, and discussed issues such as better access to healthcare for people living with HIV/AIDS.<sup about="#mwt74" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-sista_5-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"sista"}}'><a href="#cite_note-sista-5" id="mwoQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwog"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwow">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwpA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
