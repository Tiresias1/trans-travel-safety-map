We advocate for secular governance as the foundation for protecting these rights, working to secure their full recognition and to dismantle the systemic discrimination they face building toward a society grounded in pluralism, equality, and inclusion for all. Activity All events and activities carried out by the organization About us Founded in 2014, Liberals Mauritania is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing the values of liberalism, secularism, and freedom of belief. We advocate for the protection of minority rights within society, working toward a Mauritania that recognizes and safeguards the inherent human rights and dignity of every individual.
