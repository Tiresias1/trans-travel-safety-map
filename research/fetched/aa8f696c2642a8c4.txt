The LGBT Legal Situation In Seychelles The legal situation regarding LGBT rights in Seychelles has experienced significant changes in recent years. This positive amendment illustrates the government’s commitment to promoting equality and human rights for all. However, despite the decriminalization of homosexuality, certain disparities still persist with regard to legal protections for the LGBT community. The Employment Act provides protections against employment discrimination based on sexual orientation, but other areas, like housing and public accommodations, lack such legal safeguards.
