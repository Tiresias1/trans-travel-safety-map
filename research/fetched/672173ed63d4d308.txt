<p id="mwnA"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage" title="Same-sex marriage" id="mwnQ">Same-sex marriage</a> has been legal in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Virgin_Islands" title="United States Virgin Islands" id="mwng">United States Virgin Islands</a> since July 9, 2015, as a result of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_United_States" title="Supreme Court of the United States" id="mwnw">U.S. Supreme Court</a>'s decision in <i id="mwoA"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obergefell_v._Hodges" title="Obergefell v. Hodges" id="mwoQ">Obergefell v. Hodges</a></i>. On June 26, 2015, the Supreme Court ruled that same-sex couples have a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Constitution" title="United States Constitution" class="mw-redirect" id="mwog">constitutional right</a> to marry under the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equal_Protection_Clause" title="Equal Protection Clause" id="mwow">Equal Protection</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Due_Process_Clause" title="Due Process Clause" id="mwpA">Due Process</a> clauses of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourteenth_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution" title="Fourteenth Amendment to the United States Constitution" id="mwpQ">Fourteenth Amendment</a>, legalizing same-sex marriage in the U.S. Virgin Islands. On June 30, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_of_the_United_States_Virgin_Islands" title="Governor of the United States Virgin Islands" class="mw-redirect" id="mwpg">Governor</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Mapp" title="Kenneth Mapp" id="mwpw">Kenneth Mapp</a> said the territorial government would comply with the ruling, and on July 9 he signed an executive order requiring the government to extend marriage rights to same-sex couples.<sup about="#mwt65" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-15" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-15"}}'><a href="#cite_note-15" id="mwqA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwqQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwqg">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwqw">]</span></span></a></sup> The first two same-sex couples applied for <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage_license" title="Marriage license" id="mwrA">marriage licenses</a> on July 13, and were issued licenses on July 21 after the 8-day waiting period between applying for and receiving marriage licenses.<sup about="#mwt68" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-16" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-16"}}'><a href="#cite_note-16" id="mwrQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwrg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwrw">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwsA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" id="mwsQ" data-mw='{"autoGenerated":true}'/></section><section data-mw-section-id="1" id="mwsg" aria-labelledby="Background"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Background">Background</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Same-sex_marriage_in_the_United_States_Virgin_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwsw">The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statute" title="Statute" id="mwtA">statutes</a> of the U.S. Virgin Islands state that "marriage is hereby declared to be a civil contract which may be entered into between a male and a female in accordance with the law."<sup about="#mwt71" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-17" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-17"}}'><a href="#cite_note-17" id="mwtQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwtg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwtw">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwuA">]</span></span></a></sup> In May 2014, Senator Judi Buckley introduced draft legislation to the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislature_of_the_Virgin_Islands" title="Legislature of the Virgin Islands" id="mwuQ">Legislature of the Virgin Islands</a> to legalize <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage" title="Same-sex marriage" id="mwug">same-sex marriage</a>. Called the <i id="mwuw">Civil Marriage Equality Act</i>, it would have replaced the wording "between a male and a female" with "between two persons". It included language that would have allowed anyone authorized to perform a wedding ceremony to decline to do so for any reason. She anticipated that it would take several months for the language to be reviewed.<sup about="#mwt74" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-fierce_18-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"fierce"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-fierce-18"}}'><a href="#cite_note-fierce-18" id="mwvA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwvQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwvg">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwvw">]</span></span></a></sup> Buckley expected that Governor <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_de_Jongh" title="John de Jongh" class="mw-redirect" id="mwwA">John de Jongh</a>, who she said would sign the legislation, would leave office in January 2015 before the bill would come to a vote.<sup about="#mwt77" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-unlikely_19-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"unlikely"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-unlikely-19"}}'><a href="#cite_note-unlikely-19" id="mwwQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwwg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwww">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwxA">]</span></span></a></sup> Supporters of the legislation included Liberty Place, an LGBT advocacy organization based in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Croix" title="Saint Croix" id="mwxQ">Saint Croix</a>.<sup about="#mwt78" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-fierce_18-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"fierce"}}'><a href="#cite_note-fierce-18" id="mwxg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwxw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwyA">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwyQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwyg">A group of church leaders organized <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Voice_Virgin_Islands" title="One Voice Virgin Islands" id="mwyw">One Voice Virgin Islands</a> to oppose the legislation and plan a petition drive that aimed to collect 50,000 signatures.<sup about="#mwt79" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-fierce_18-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"fierce"}}'><a href="#cite_note-fierce-18" id="mwzA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwzQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwzg">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwzw">]</span></span></a></sup> The group authored a letter to territorial officials that some of its members found objectionable because it included the suggestion that some government officials were homosexual.<sup about="#mwt80" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-fierce_18-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"fierce"}}'><a href="#cite_note-fierce-18" id="mw0A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw0Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw0g">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw0w">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt81" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-unlikely_19-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"unlikely"}}'><a href="#cite_note-unlikely-19" id="mw1A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw1Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw1g">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw1w">]</span></span></a></sup> The group's president, New Vision Ministries Pastor James Petty, said: "We do not wish to be America's same-sex paradise".<sup about="#mwt82" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-fierce_18-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"fierce"}}'><a href="#cite_note-fierce-18" id="mw2A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw2Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw2g">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw2w">]</span></span></a></sup> Pastor Lennox Zamore said that he rejected the argument that legalizing same-sex marriage would benefit the local economy: "We don't want to balance our books by bringing the sex industry – whether it is same sex or not – to the Virgin Islands".<sup about="#mwt85" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-20" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-20"}}'><a href="#cite_note-20" id="mw3A"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw3Q"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw3g">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw3w">]</span></span></a></sup> The marriage bill was not voted on before the end of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislative_session" title="Legislative session" id="mw4A">legislative session</a>.</p>

<p id="mw5A">The decision of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Supreme_Court" title="United States Supreme Court" class="mw-redirect" id="mw5Q">U.S. Supreme Court</a> in <i id="mw5g"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obergefell_v._Hodges" title="Obergefell v. Hodges" id="mw5w">Obergefell v. Hodges</a></i> on June 26, 2015, that invalidated bans on same-sex marriage under the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourteenth_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution" title="Fourteenth Amendment to the United States Constitution" id="mw6A">Fourteenth Amendment</a>, was met with mixed reactions from U.S. Virgin Islands officials. On June 27, Presiding Judge Michael Dunston of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Virgin_Islands_Superior_Court" title="United States Virgin Islands Superior Court" id="mw6Q">Superior Court</a> said the territory would comply with the ruling, though he thought that <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage_license" title="Marriage license" id="mw6g">marriage licenses</a> could not be issued to same-sex couples until the statutes were amended.<sup about="#mwt97" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-21" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-21"}}'><a href="#cite_note-21" id="mw6w"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw7A"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw7Q">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw7g">]</span></span></a></sup> On June 30, Governor <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Mapp" title="Kenneth Mapp" id="mw7w">Kenneth Mapp</a> announced he would issue an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_order" title="Executive order" id="mw8A">executive order</a> directing government agencies and departments to comply with the ruling. He said:<sup about="#mwt100" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-22" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-22"}}'><a href="#cite_note-22" id="mw8Q"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw8g"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw8w">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw9A">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mw9w">Mapp signed the executive order on July 9, although Lieutenant Governor <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osbert_E._Potter" title="Osbert E. Potter" class="mw-redirect" id="mw-A">Osbert E. Potter</a> was not on hand to attest to Mapp's signature, as required before the order could go into effect.<sup about="#mwt105" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-23" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-23"}}'><a href="#cite_note-23" id="mw-Q"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw-g"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw-w">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mw_A">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt108" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-24" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-24"}}'><a href="#cite_note-24" id="mw_Q"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mw_g"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mw_w">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQA">]</span></span></a></sup> The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_presidents_of_the_Virgin_Islands_Legislature" title="List of presidents of the Virgin Islands Legislature" id="mwAQE">President of the U.S. Virgin Islands Legislature</a>, Neville James, a vocal opponent of same-sex marriage, refused to sign off on the executive order in Potter's stead.<sup about="#mwt111" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-25" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-25"}}'><a href="#cite_note-25" id="mwAQI"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAQM"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQQ">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQU">]</span></span></a></sup> However, Judge Dunston declared that the Superior Court would act to comply with <i id="mwAQY">Obergefell</i> in the absence of action from the Legislature and licenses would be issued for two same-sex couples who had applied on July 13.<sup about="#mwt114" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-26" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-26"}}'><a href="#cite_note-26" id="mwAQc"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAQg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQk">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQo">]</span></span></a></sup> Potter was expected to return to the territory and sign the executive order on July 15, but was unable to do so after Mapp left the territory the day before, due to Potter serving as acting governor.<sup about="#mwt117" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-27" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-27"}}'><a href="#cite_note-27" id="mwAQs"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAQw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQ0">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAQ4">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt120" class="mw-ref reference" class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwARA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARE">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARI">]</span></span></a></sup> On July 27, both Governor Mapp and Lieutenant Governor Potter were in the territory together for the first time since before the order was drafted, meaning that Potter would be able to sign the order.<sup about="#mwt123" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-29" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-29"}}'><a href="#cite_note-29" id="mwARM"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwARQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARU">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwARY">]</span></span></a></sup> On Tuesday, July 28, Potter signed the order, effectively requiring all agencies to comply with the Supreme Court ruling.<sup about="#mwt126" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-30" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-30"}}'><a href="#cite_note-30" id="mwARc"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwARg"><span class="cite-bracket" <p id="mwARs">The marriage license process is administered by the Superior Court. A couple may apply online and have their license emailed to them or apply in-person at the courthouse, with an application fee of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_dollar" title="United States dollar" id="mwARw">$</a>200. It takes approximately one week for the application to be processed. A licensed judge, minister or officiant may perform the wedding ceremony alongside two witnesses.<sup about="#mwt129" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-31" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-31"}}'><a href="#cite_note-31" id="mwAR0"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwAR4"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwAR8">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASA">]</span></span></a></sup> The first same-sex couple to marry in the U.S. Virgin Islands was Rick Weinstein and Tom Eggleston, who had been together for more than 40 years,  in a private ceremony in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Amalie,_U.S._Virgin_Islands" title="Charlotte Amalie, U.S. Virgin Islands" id="mwASE">Charlotte Amalie</a>.<sup about="#mwt132" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-32" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-32"}}'><a href="#cite_note-32" id="mwASI"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwASM"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASQ">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASU">]</span></span></a></sup> In 2024, the U.S. Virgin Islands was named one of the best marriage destinations for same-sex couples in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caribbean" title="Caribbean" id="mwASY">Caribbean</a>.<sup about="#mwt135" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-33" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-33"}}'><a href="#cite_note-33" id="mwASc"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwASg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASk">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwASo">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
