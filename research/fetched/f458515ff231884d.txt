      <h2> <span class="field field--name-title field--type-string field--label-hidden">Hate crime legislation in Portugal</span>
 </h2>
    

  
  <div class="node__summary">
    <p>  </p>
  </div>
  <div class="node__summary node__content">
    
            <div class="clearfix text-formatted field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden field__item"><p><strong>(Decree Law 48/95, last amended by Law 15/2024 of January 15, 2024.)&nbsp;</strong><br><br>(Unofficial translation, emphasis added)<br><br><strong>Excerpts related to Hate crime offences&nbsp;</strong><br><br><strong>Article 132 – Aggravated Murde</strong>r<br>1. When death is produced under circumstances that reveal a special reprehensibility or perversity, the offender shall be punished with imprisonment from 12 to 25 years.</p><p>2. The following circumstances reveal the special reprehensibility or perversity that is referred to in the previous paragraph, namely, the circumstance that the agent:<br>(...)<br><strong>(f) is determined by racial, religious, political hatred or based on the victim’s colour, ethnic or national origin, sex, sexual orientation or gender identity;</strong><br>(...)</p><p><strong>Article 145 – Aggravated assault (serious bodily harm)</strong><br>1. If the offenses to the physical integrity are produced under circumstances that reveal a special reprehensibility or perversity, the offender shall be punished:<br>a) With a prison sentence of up to 4 years in the case of Article 143 [simple assault/bodily harm];<br>b) With a prison sentence of 1 to 5 years in the case of paragraph 2 of Article 144-A [preparatory acts for female genital mutilation];<br>c) With a prison sentence of 3 to 12 years in the case of article 144 [serious assault/grievous bodily harm] and paragraph 1 of article 144-A [female genital mutilation].<br>2. The circumstances described in<strong> Article 132 of paragraph 2</strong> are likely to reveal the offender’s special reprehensibility or perversity, among others.<br><br><strong>Article 155 – Aggravation</strong><br>1 – When the acts provided for <strong>in articles 153 to 154-C [Article 153 – Threat, Article 154 – Coercion, Article 154-A – Stalking, Article 154-B - Forced marriage, Article 154-C - Preparatory acts</strong>] are carried out:<br>[…]<br>e) <strong>By the determining circumstance provided for in Article 132(2)(f)</strong><br>the offender is punished with imprisonment for not more than two years or with fine for not more than 240 days, in the case of article 153 and 154-C, and with sentence of imprisonment from one to five years, in the case of paragraph 1 of article 154 and article 154-A, and with sentence of imprisonment from one to eight years, in the case of article 154-B.</p><p><strong>Excerpts related to Hate speech offences</strong><br><br><strong>Article 240 - Discrimination and incitement to hatred and violence&nbsp;</strong><br>1. Anyone who:&nbsp;<br>a) Founds or establishes an organization or engages in propaganda activities that incite or encourage discrimination, hatred, or violence against a person or group of people based on their <strong>ethnic-racial origin, national or religious origin, color, nationality, ancestry, territory of origin, religion, language, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression or sexual characteristics, or physical or mental disability;</strong> or<br>b) Participates in the organizations referred to in the previous paragraph, in the activities they undertake, or provides them with assistance, including financing;&nbsp;<br>is punished with imprisonment for 1 to 8 years.&nbsp;<br>2. Anyone who, publicly, by any means intended for dissemination, especially through the apology, denial, or gross trivialization of crimes of genocide, war, or crimes against peace and humanity:&nbsp;<br>a) Incites acts of violence against a person or group of people because of their ethnic-racial origin, national or religious origin, color, nationality, ancestry, territory of origin, religion, language, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression or sexual characteristics, physical or mental disability;&nbsp;<br>b) Defames or insults a person or group of people because of their ethnic-racial origin, national or religious origin, color, nationality, ancestry, territory of origin, religion, language, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression or sexual characteristics, physical or mental disability;&nbsp;<br>c) Threatening a person or group of people because of their ethnic-racial origin, national or religious origin, color, nationality, ancestry, territory of origin, religion, language, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or sexual characteristics, physical or mental disability; or&nbsp;<br>d) Inciting discrimination, hatred, or violence against a person or group of people because of their ethnic-racial origin, national or religious origin, color, nationality, ancestry, territory of origin, religion, language, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or sexual characteristics, or physical or mental disability;&nbsp;<br>is punishable by imprisonment of 6 months to 5 years.&nbsp;<br>3. When the crimes provided for in the previous paragraphs are committed through a computer system, the court may order the deletion of computer data or content.</p></div>
