<p id="mwBw"><b id="mwCA"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ" title="LGBTQ" class="mw-redirect" id="mwCQ">LGBTQ</a> rights in the Northern Mariana Islands</b> have evolved substantially in recent years. <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage" title="Same-sex marriage" id="mwCg">Same-sex marriage</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_adoption" title="LGBT adoption" class="mw-redirect" id="mwCw">adoption</a> became legal with the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_the_United_States" title="Supreme Court of the United States" id="mwDA">Supreme Court's</a> ruling in the case of <i id="mwDQ"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obergefell_v._Hodges" title="Obergefell v. Hodges" id="mwDg">Obergefell v. Hodges</a></i> in June 2015. However, the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States" title="United States" id="mwDw">U.S.</a> territory does not ban discrimination based on <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwEA">sexual orientation</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_identity" title="Gender identity" id="mwEQ">gender identity</a>, except in relation to government employees. Gender changes are legal in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Mariana_Islands" title="Northern Mariana Islands" id="mwEg">Northern Mariana Islands</a>, provided the applicant has undergone <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_reassignment_surgery" title="Sex reassignment surgery" class="mw-redirect" id="mwEw">sex reassignment surgery</a>.</p>

It has been described as openly bisexual, though this is disputed.<sup about="#mwt11" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" </section><section data-mw-section-id="2" id="mwLw" aria-labelledby="Legality_of_same-sex_sexual_activity"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Legality_of_same-sex_sexual_activity">Legality of same-sex sexual activity</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legality of same-sex sexual activity"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwMA">Same-sex sexual activity has been legal in the Northern Marianas since 1983.<sup about="#mwt20" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwMQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwMg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwMw">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwNA">]</span></span></a></sup></p> 

</section><section data-mw-section-id="3" id="mwNQ" aria-labelledby="Recognition_of_same-sex_relationships"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Recognition_of_same-sex_relationships">Recognition of same-sex relationships</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Recognition of same-sex relationships"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<span class="mw-empty-elt" about="#mwt21" typeof="mw:Transclusion" id="mwNg" data-mw='{"parts":[{"template":{"target":{"wt":"main article","href":"./Template:Main_article"},"params":{"1":{"wt":"Same-sex marriage in the Northern Mariana Islands"}},"i":0}}]}'><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1368532237" typeof="mw:Extension/templatestyles" about="#mwt22" data-mw='{"name":"templatestyles","attrs":{"src":"Module:Hatnote/styles.css"},"body":{"extsrc":""}}'>.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+.mw-empty-elt+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}@media print{body.ns-0 .mw-parser-output .hatnote{display:none!important}}</style></span><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable" about="#mwt21" id="mwNw">Main article: <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands" title="Same-sex marriage in the Northern Mariana Islands">Same-sex marriage in the Northern Mariana Islands</a></div>

<p id="mwOA">Same-sex marriage became legal in the Commonwealth on June 26, 2015, when the United States Supreme Court ruled that it is unconstitutional to ban same-sex marriages. Prior to this, there was no prohibition on same-sex marriage in the law of the territory, nor do the statutes specify the sex of the parties to a marriage between citizens of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Mariana_Islands" title="Northern Mariana Islands" id="mwOQ">Northern Mariana Islands</a>.<sup about="#mwt25" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwOg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwOw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwPA">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwPQ">]</span></span></a></sup> Other provisions assume the parties to a marriage are not of the same sex. With respect to a marriage involving one or more non-citizens, the statutes say: "The male at the time of contracting the marriage be at least 18 years of age and the female at least 16 years of age..."<sup about="#mwt28" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-7" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-7"}}'><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwPg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwPw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwQA">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwQQ">]</span></span></a></sup> Statutes concerning divorce assume that the partners to a marriage are man and wife.<sup about="#mwt31" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-8" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-8"}}'><a href="#cite_note-8" id="mwQg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwQw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRA">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRQ">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt34" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-9" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-9"}}'><a href="#cite_note-9" id="mwRg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwRw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwSA">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwSQ">]</span></span></a></sup> These laws have yet to be changed to reflect the Supreme Court ruling.</p>

<p id="mwSg">The first marriage between a same-sex couple occurred on the island of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saipan" title="Saipan" class="mw-redirect" id="mwSw">Saipan</a> on July 22, 2015.<sup about="#mwt37" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-10" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-10"}}'><a href="#cite_note-10" id="mwTA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwTQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwTg">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwTw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="4" id="mwUA" aria-labelledby="Adoption_and_family_planning"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Adoption_and_family_planning">Adoption and family planning</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Adoption and family planning"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwUQ">As a result of <i id="mwUg">Obergefell v. Hodges</i>, same-sex couples are permitted to adopt. In addition, lesbian couples can access assisted reproduction services, such as <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_vitro_fertilization" title="In vitro fertilization" class="mw-redirect" id="mwUw">in vitro fertilization</a>. Territory law recognizes the non-genetic, non-gestational mother as a legal parent to a child born via donor insemination, but only if the parents are married.<sup about="#mwt40" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-11" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-11"}}'><a href="#cite_note-11" id="mwVA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwVQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwVg">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwVw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="5" id="mwWA" aria-labelledby="Discrimination_protections_and_hate_crime_law"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Discrimination_protections_and_hate_crime_law">Discrimination protections and hate crime law</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Discrimination protections and hate crime law"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwWQ">The Northern Mariana Islands does not protect people from discrimination on the basis of their <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexual_orientation" title="Sexual orientation" id="mwWg">sexual orientation</a> or <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_identity" title="Gender identity" id="mwWw">gender identity</a>. </p>

<p id="mwXA">Discrimination against government employees on the basis of their sexual orientation is illegal.<sup about="#mwt43" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-12" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-12"}}'><a href="#cite_note-12" id="mwXQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwXg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXw">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwYA">]</span></span></a></sup> The Marianas Visitors Authority and the Civil Service Commission are similarly banned under law from discriminating on account of sexual orientation.<sup about="#mwt46" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-13" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-13"}}'><a href="#cite_note-13" id="mwYQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwYg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwYw">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZA">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt49" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-14" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-14"}}'><a href="#cite_note-14" id="mwZQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwZg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZw">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaA">]</span></span></a></sup> Such discrimination is subject to "disciplinary action".</p>

<p id="mwaQ">The Administrative Code of the Northern Mariana Islands prohibits bullying, harassment and discrimination against public school students, employees and parents. §60-20-401 states:<sup about="#mwt52" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-school_15-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"school"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-school-15"}}'><a href="#cite_note-school-15" id="mwag"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwaw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwbA">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwbQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
<dl id="mwbg"><dd id="mwbw">All students, employees and parents have the right to be free from discrimination and harassment on the basis of race, creed, religion, color, sex, sexual orientation, national origin, age, cultural or socio-economic status or disabling condition. Employees shall not participate in, or permit others to engage in, any act of discrimination against students, parents or coworkers based on the above factors or in retaliation for the exercise of any of their rights.</dd></dl>

<p id="mwcA">Bullying is defined in §60-20-403 as:<sup about="#mwt53" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-school_15-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"school"}}'><a href="#cite_note-school-15" id="mwcQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwcg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwcw">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwdA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
<dl id="mwdQ"><dd id="mwdg">As used in this regulation, "bullying, harassment, or intimidation" means intentional conduct, including verbal, physical, or written conduct or an intentional electronic communication that creates a hostile educational environment by substantially interfering with a student’s educational benefits, opportunities, or performance, or with a student's physical or psychological well-being and is:
<dl id="mwdw"><dd id="mweA">(i) Motivated by an actual or a perceived personal characteristic including race, national origin, marital status, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, religion, ancestry, physical attributes, socioeconomic status, familial status, or physical or mental ability or disability;</dd></dl></dd></dl>

<p id="mweQ">Lesbian, gay, and bisexual persons are allowed to serve openly in the American Armed Forces.<sup about="#mwt56" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-16" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-16"}}'><a href="#cite_note-16" id="mweg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwew"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwfA">[</span>16<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwfQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwfg">As of January 2023, the territory's <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hate_crime" title="Hate crime" id="mwfw">hate crime</a> law covers crimes motivated by hatred based on sexual orientation and gender identity.<sup about="#mwt59" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-17" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-17"}}'><a href="#cite_note-17" id="mwgA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwgQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwgg">[</span>17<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwgw">]</span></span></a></sup> Such crimes are also covered federally under the <i id="mwhA"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Shepard_and_James_Byrd_Jr._Hate_Crimes_Prevention_Act" title="Matthew Shepard and James Byrd Jr. Hate Crimes Prevention Act" id="mwhQ">Matthew Shepard and James Byrd Jr. Hate Crimes Prevention Act</a></i>.<sup about="#mwt62" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-18" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-18"}}'><a href="#cite_note-18" id="mwhg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwhw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwiA">[</span>18<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwiQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="6" id="mwig" aria-labelledby="Gender_identity_and_expression"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Gender_identity_and_expression">Gender identity and expression</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Gender identity and expression"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwiw">Transgender persons in the Northern Mariana Islands may change their legal gender following <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sex_reassignment_surgery" title="Sex reassignment surgery" class="mw-redirect" id="mwjA">sex reassignment surgery</a> and a name change. The <i id="mwjQ">Vital Statistics Act of 2006</i>, which took effect in March 2007, states that: "Upon receipt of a certified copy of an order of the CNMI Superior Court indicating the sex of an individual born in the CNMI has been changed by surgical procedure and whether such individual’s name has been changed, the certificate of birth of such individual shall be amended as prescribed by regulation."<sup about="#mwt65" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-19" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-19"}}'><a href="#cite_note-19" id="mwjg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwjw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkA">[</span>19<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkQ">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="7" id="mwkg" aria-labelledby="Living_conditions"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Living_conditions">Living conditions</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Northern_Mariana_Islands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Living conditions"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwkw">The first <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pride_parade" title="Pride parade" id="mwlA">pride parade</a> in the Northern Mariana Islands was held in July 2018.<sup about="#mwt68" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-20" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-20"}}'><a href="#cite_note-20" id="mwlQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwlg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwlw">[</span>20<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwmA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwmQ">Since legalization in June 2015, the territory has become a popular marriage destination for same-sex couples, especially among <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China" title="China" id="mwmg">Chinese</a> tourists. In the first half of 2018, 77% of all marriages were same-sex marriages.<sup about="#mwt71" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-21" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-21"}}'><a href="#cite_note-21" id="mwmw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwnA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwnQ">[</span>21<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwng">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
