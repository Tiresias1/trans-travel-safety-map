<p id="mwCg">The legal code regarding lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer (LGBTQ) rights in Vatican City is based on the Italian <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanardelli_Code" title="Zanardelli Code" id="mwCw">Zanardelli Code</a> of 1889, since the founding of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_state" title="Sovereign state" id="mwDA">sovereign state</a> of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City" id="mwDQ">Vatican City</a> in 1929.</p>

<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" id="mwDg" data-mw='{"autoGenerated":true}'/></section><section data-mw-section-id="1" id="mwDw" aria-labelledby="Criminal_law"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Criminal_law">Criminal law</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vatican_City&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Criminal law"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwEA">Since 1890, the territory of what is now Vatican City has had no criminal laws against non-commercial, private, adult and consensual <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_sexual_activities" title="Same-sex sexual activities" class="mw-redirect" id="mwEQ">same-sex sexual activity</a>.<sup about="#mwt9" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-1" id="mwEg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwEw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFA">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFQ">]</span></span></a></sup> The age of consent is currently set at 18 years for all persons, regardless of gender; in the case of sexual relations within marriage – only in the cases of marriages recognized as valid by the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church" id="mwFg">Catholic Church</a> and by the laws of Vatican City<sup about="#mwt12" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-2" id="mwFw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwGA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGQ">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGg">]</span></span></a></sup> – the legal age is set at 14 years old.<sup about="#mwt15" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-3"}}'><a href="#cite_note-3" id="mwGw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwHA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHQ">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwHw">Foreign diplomats, in order to be accredited, must not be part of a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_parenting" title="LGBT parenting" class="mw-redirect" id="mwIA">same-sex family</a>,<sup about="#mwt18" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-4" id="mwIQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwIg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwIw">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJA">]</span></span></a></sup> and must not be <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divorce" title="Divorce" id="mwJQ">divorced</a>.<sup about="#mwt21" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwJg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwJw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKA">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKQ">]</span></span></a></sup> In 2008, Jean-Loup Kuhn-Delforge, who is an <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coming_out" title="Coming out" id="mwKg">openly</a>-<a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay" title="Gay" id="mwKw">gay</a> diplomat, and who is in a <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_solidarity_pact" title="Civil solidarity pact" id="mwLA">civil pact</a> with his partner, was rejected by <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church" id="mwLQ">Roman Catholic</a> officials to be the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_French_ambassadors_to_the_Holy_See" title="List of French ambassadors to the Holy See" class="mw-redirect" id="mwLg">French ambassador to the Holy See</a>. In 2015, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_Stefanini" title="Laurent Stefanini" id="mwLw">Laurent Stefanini</a>, an openly-gay practising Catholic diplomat was rejected by Roman Catholic officials to be the French ambassador to the Holy See, despite being single. He was backed by <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_France" title="President of France" id="mwMA">President</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francois_Hollande" title="Francois Hollande" class="mw-redirect" id="mwMQ">Francois Hollande</a> and was supported by France's top <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curia" title="Curia" id="mwMg">Curia</a> <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_(Catholic_Church)" title="Cardinal (Catholic Church)" id="mwMw">cardinal</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Louis_Tauran" title="Jean-Louis Tauran" id="mwNA">Jean-Louis Tauran</a>, who was the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camerlengo_of_the_Holy_Roman_Church" title="Camerlengo of the Holy Roman Church" id="mwNQ">Camerlengo of the Holy Roman Church</a>, and Cardinal <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André_Vingt-Trois" title="André Vingt-Trois" id="mwNg">André Vingt-Trois</a>, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Paris" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Paris" class="mw-redirect" id="mwNw">Archbishop of Paris</a>. France kept their ambassador position vacant from March 2015 to May 2016 in protest, before nominating another diplomat in May 2016.<sup about="#mwt24" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwOA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwOQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwOg">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwOw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt27" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-7" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-7"}}'><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwPA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwPQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwPg">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwPw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="2" id="mwQA" aria-labelledby="Recognition_of_same-sex_relationships"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Recognition_of_same-sex_relationships">Recognition of same-sex relationships</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vatican_City&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Recognition of same-sex relationships"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>

<p id="mwQQ"><a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City" id="mwQg">Vatican City</a> had always expressed disagreement with any civil recognition of same-sex unions, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage" title="Same-sex marriage" id="mwQw">same-sex marriage</a>, and the granting of <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_adoption" title="LGBT adoption" class="mw-redirect" id="mwRA">adoption rights to same-sex couples</a>.<sup about="#mwt30" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-8" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-8"}}'><a href="#cite_note-8" id="mwRQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwRg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRw">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwSA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwSQ">Although <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis" id="mwSg">Pope Francis</a> expressed support for same-sex civil unions outside of the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church" id="mwSw">Catholic Church</a> in 2023, he remained firm that "The Sacrament of Marriage is between a man and a woman as instituted by God."<sup about="#mwt33" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-9" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-9"}}'><a href="#cite_note-9" id="mwTA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwTQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwTg">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwTw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt36" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-10" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-10"}}'><a href="#cite_note-10" id="mwUA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwUQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwUg">[</span>10<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwUw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="3" id="mwVA" aria-labelledby="Discrimination_protections"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Discrimination_protections">Discrimination protections</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_Vatican_City&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Discrimination protections"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwVQ">The <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City" id="mwVg">Vatican</a> reserves the right to remove, suspend and dismiss immediately any official and employee who publicly admits to being <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay" title="Gay" id="mwVw">gay</a> or questions the general policy of the Vatican towards homosexuals.<sup about="#mwt37" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-11" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-11"}}'><a href="#cite_note-11" id="mwWA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwWQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwWg">[</span>11<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwWw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt40" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-12" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-12"}}'><a href="#cite_note-12" id="mwXA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwXQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXg">[</span>12<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwXw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwYA">In 2021, the Vatican foreign minister, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Gallagher_(bishop)" title="Paul Gallagher (bishop)" id="mwYQ">Archbishop Paul Richard Gallagher</a>, delivered a letter to the Italian ambassador to the Holy See, expressing "concerns" over a bill in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Parliament" title="Italian Parliament" id="mwYg">Italian Parliament</a> meant to legally protect LGBTQ Italians against <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violence_against_LGBT_people" title="Violence against LGBT people" class="mw-redirect" id="mwYw">violence</a> and <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heterosexism" title="Heterosexism" id="mwZA">discrimination</a>. The letter claimed the section of the bill prohibiting incitement of hatred on the basis of sexual orientation and gender identity constituted a violation of freedom of speech and of religion, and asked for the draft law's text to be reformulated.<sup about="#mwt43" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-13" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-13"}}'><a href="#cite_note-13" id="mwZQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwZg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZw">[</span>13<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaA">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt44" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-14" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-14"}}'><a href="#cite_note-14" id="mwaQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwag"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaw">[</span>14<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwbA">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt47" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-15" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-15"}}'><a href="#cite_note-15" id="mwbQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwbg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwbw">[</span>15<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwcA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>
