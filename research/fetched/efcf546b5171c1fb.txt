Yes Last Update: March 12, 2026 Consensual same-sex sexual conduct between adults was decriminalized in Saint Kitts and Nevis in 2022. In Jamal Jeffers et al. v The Attorney General of St. Christopher and Nevis , the High Court held that Sections 56 and 57 of the Offences Against the Person Act, which criminalized buggery and indecent assault against men, respectively, contravened the constitutional rights to protection of personal privacy and freedom of expression, and declared them to be void and of no force to the extent that they criminalized consensual sexual acts in private between adults. While LGBTIQ people lack other key protections, including nondiscrimination laws that protect equitable access to housing, employment, and education, decriminalization marks a significant step forward in the fight to end stigma and discrimination against sexual and gender minorities.
