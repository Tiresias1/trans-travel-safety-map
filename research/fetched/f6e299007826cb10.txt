<p id="mwCQ">Lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer (LGBTQ) people in the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahrawi_Arab_Democratic_Republic" title="Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic" id="mwCg">Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic</a> (SADR) face significant challenges not experienced by non-LGBTQ residents.</p>

</section><section data-mw-section-id="1" id="mwCw" aria-labelledby="Status"><div class="mw-heading mw-heading2"><h2 id="Status">Status</h2><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=LGBTQ_rights_in_the_Sahrawi_Arab_Democratic_Republic&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Status"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p id="mwDA">Multiple sources such as the Council for Global Equality, <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equaldex" title="Equaldex" id="mwDQ">Equaldex</a> and the <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Lesbian,_Gay,_Bisexual,_Trans_and_Intersex_Association" title="International Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans and Intersex Association" id="mwDg">International Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Trans and Intersex Association</a> describe <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_sexual_activity" title="Same-sex sexual activity" class="mw-redirect" id="mwDw">same-sex sexual activity</a> as being illegal in the SADR for both males and females, and punishable by up to three years in prison.<sup about="#mwt7" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-f087_1-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"f087"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-f087-1"}}'><a href="#cite_note-f087-1" id="mwEA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwEQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwEg">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwEw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt10" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-equaldex_2-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"equaldex"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-equaldex-2"}}'><a href="#cite_note-equaldex-2" id="mwFA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwFQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFg">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwFw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt13" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ILGA_3-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"ILGA"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-ILGA-3"}}'><a href="#cite_note-ILGA-3" id="mwGA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwGQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGg">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwGw">]</span></span></a></sup><sup about="#mwt16" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-v311_4-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"v311"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-v311-4"}}'><a href="#cite_note-v311-4" id="mwHA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwHQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHg">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwHw">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwIA">According to a 2009 <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USCRI" title="USCRI" class="mw-redirect" id="mwIQ">USCRI</a> report, Sahrawi government officials in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sahrawi_refugee_camps" title="Sahrawi refugee camps" id="mwIg">refugee camps in Algeria</a> "declared that they were holding some prisoners on charges of "homosexuality" even as they alleged facts that would more fittingly describe rape."<sup about="#mwt19" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-c044_5-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"c044"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-c044-5"}}'><a href="#cite_note-c044-5" id="mwIw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwJA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJQ">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwJg">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwJw">Ahmed Mulay Alli, diplomatic representative of the SADR in Mexico, was quoted in a 2010 report as saying "the Constitution of the SADR does not refer to homosexuality or any other sexual practice; that is, it does not touch on it, it does not punish it".<sup about="#mwt22" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Rivas_6-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"Rivas"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-Rivas-6"}}'><a href="#cite_note-Rivas-6" id="mwKA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwKQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKg">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwKw">]</span></span></a></sup> According to the Spanish Commission for Refugee Aid (CEAR), there is thus no type of protection for LGBTQ individuals in case of discrimination or violence. Most residents of the country are <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim" title="Muslim" class="mw-redirect" id="mwLA">Muslim</a>, and same-sex relationships are seen as a foreign phenomenon.<sup about="#mwt25" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-t427_7-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"t427"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-t427-7"}}'><a href="#cite_note-t427-7" id="mwLQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwLg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwLw">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwMA">]</span></span></a></sup></p>

<p id="mwMQ">The region was under <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Sahara" title="Spanish Sahara" id="mwMg">Spanish colonial rule</a> until 1976, and homosexuality was not decriminalized in Spain until 1979.<sup about="#mwt28" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-l131_8-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"l131"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-l131-8"}}'><a href="#cite_note-l131-8" id="mwMw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwNA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwNQ">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwNg">]</span></span></a></sup> Same-sex sexual activity is also illegal in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morocco" title="Morocco" id="mwNw">Morocco</a>,<sup about="#mwt31" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-hdt_9-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw='{"name":"ref","attrs":{"name":"hdt"},"body":{"id":"mw-reference-text-cite_note-hdt-9"}}'><a href="#cite_note-hdt-9" id="mwOA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwOQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwOg">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwOw">]</span></span></a></sup> which occupies most of the territory in <a rel="mw:WikiLink" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Sahara" title="Western Sahara" id="mwPA">Western Sahara</a> claimed by the SADR.</p>
