Home » PrideNZ.com Close × Search Browse Media On This Day Map Quotations Timeline Research Free Datasets Remembered About Contact ☰ Kia ora, Welcome PrideNZ.com is a website exploring the voices and opinions of Aotearoa New Zealand's LGBTQIA+ Rainbow communities through over 1,000 audio recordings of interviews and local events. Use the search or browse pages to find audio content of interest. The site also hosts an extensive rainbow timeline and links to over 18,000 articles published by GayNZ.com between 2001-2017. There are also pages dedicated to significant anniversaries , research and free datasets . And since 2020 we have been making the PrideNZ collection more accessible and discoverable by using various forms of Artificial Intelligence . If you can't find what you're searching for please contact us for assistance. 2026 - Homosexual Law Reform 40th anniversary July 2026 marks the 40th anniversary of the passing of the Homosexual Law Reform Act which decriminalised homosexual activity between consenting men. All of the written transcripts (Hansard) from the Parliamentary debates are now available here , with some of those same debates available as original audio recordings . A comprehensive timeline is available, along with images from some of the pro and anti-reform events can be viewed here . The Missing Pages Project As part of the events to mark the 40th anniversary of homosexual law reform, we've launched The Missing Pages Project. The online volunteer project aims to ensure that significant LGBTQIA+ Rainbow people, events, organisations, and legislation in New Zealand are properly documented and made freely available on Wikipedia.
