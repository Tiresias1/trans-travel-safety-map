It is constituted primarily to provide a service to Lesbian women, Gay men, Bisexual, Transgender and Intersex persons (LGBTI Persons) and is not formed for the purposes of coercing or persuading anyone to lead the lifestyle of any particular sexual orientation or preference or gender identity. LEARN MORE SUCCESS STORIES Before coming out, I felt like a prisoner, silently battling my sexuality. Then in 2013, I met H***** on social media, who introduced me to GALZ programmes at the GALZ DIC. There, I began to understand my sexuality from a broader perspective, connecting with others like me through workshops and empowerment programmes. Over time, I even referred other community members to access GALZ services. Today, I am a proud and empowered gay man, thanks to the support I received from GALZ. Loveness, GALZ Member " " Sometime last year, I was unwell and had lost my job after a long period of illness. My hands and legs were swollen, making it hard to work. During that difficult time, GALZ helped cover my hospital admission fees and provided counselling and other support that helped me get back on my feet. I can’t fully express it in words, but I remain deeply grateful for their kindness and support. " " Blessed, GALZ Member I was facing jail time after being arrested on charges of “sodomy”. It was one of the darkest moments of my life. GALZ stood by me, showing up in court and providing legal support free of charge. With their help, my case was acquitted. Their support gave me hope when I felt completely alone, and I will always be grateful for how they stood with me during that difficult time. Tendai, GALZ Member " " I had been taken out of school because my parents could not afford to pay my university fees. I came across a post on X (formerly Twitter) about the Munhu Muhu Scholarship and decided to apply. GALZ supported me through the scholarship, enabling me to finish my studies and graduate. Today, I am self-employed and building my future. I am forever grateful to GALZ for giving me that chance.
