The territory's constitution, enacted in 2010, prohibits discrimination based on sexual orientation. Contents Sexual activity Recognition of unions In the military References Recognition of unions Same-sex marriage became legal in the Pitcairn Islands on 14 May 2015, on the principle; however, none of the c. 50 Pitcairn Islanders currently residing in the territory is known to be in a same-sex relationship. In the military LGBT individuals may legally serve in the UK military, as the defence is the responsibility of the United Kingdom.
