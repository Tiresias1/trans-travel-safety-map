# MYS — Malaysia · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 16 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
RICH — the mandatory exception is confirmed and **sharper than expected**: **Kelantan in August 2026
enacted a state offence of undergoing or causing a gender change**, and had already brought its first
prosecution of a trans woman under the predecessor provision. Beyond Kelantan there is documented
**state-by-state divergence in religious-police practice and official posture**: Kedah (mass raid
targeting 30 trans women), Terengganu (official anti-gay signboards, with an exco member saying the aim
is to "incite hatred"), Johor (a RM400,000 state "rehabilitation" centre for people in same-sex
relationships), Selangor (religious-department manhunts *and* a state-government declaration against
"LGBT promotion"), Malacca (religious department detaining a person for dressing as a woman while
livestreaming), and Perak/Pahang/Selangor violence-and-response records. 9 of 16 units carry
unit-specific findings.

## Different-legal-system regions
**This is the mandatory-exception country with the most real legal content.**
- **Dual system:** Malaysia runs parallel civil and **Syariah (Sharia) courts**; Syariah criminal
  jurisdiction applies to **Muslims** and is legislated **state by state**, each state enacting its own
  Syariah Criminal Offences Enactment. Federal **Act 355 caps** state Sharia penalties at RM5,000, three
  years' imprisonment and six lashes — which is why state law is punitive-but-limited and why states keep
  trying to expand it. — https://legaldignity.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Analysis-of-Terengganu-Syariah-Criminal-Offences-Enactment.pdf
- **Kelantan** (`15666254B53975248776023`) — the outlier with the most expansive agenda: its **Syariah
  Criminal Code (I) Enactment 2019** (gazetted 2020) was **struck down almost entirely by the Federal
  Court on 9 February 2024**, which held that 16 of 18 provisions were outside the state's power because
  federal law already covered them (Sections 13 and 30 were upheld as constitutional). Kelantan's
  assembly then resolved to **re-enact** the voided provisions, and on **12–13 August 2026 passed a new
  Syariah Bill** replacing them. — https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2024/02/09/federal-court-rules-16-provisions-in-kelantan-criminal-enactment-unconstitutional-in-muslim-duos-challenge/117160 · https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/698433 · https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/13/kelantan-passes-strict-new-decency-rules-in-syariah-bill
- **Terengganu** (`15666254B71460903090065`) has its own **Syariah Criminal Offences (Takzir) Enactment
  2001** plus a **Hudud & Qisas Enactment 2002** that is *not* enforceable because of the federal cap, and
  amended its Takzir provisions from December 2022 — i.e. a state maintaining a shadow capital-punishment
  code it cannot currently use, while still legislating on it. — https://legaldignity.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Analysis-of-Terengganu-Syariah-Criminal-Offences-Enactment.pdf
- **Kedah** enforces under the older **Kedah Shariah Criminal Offences Enactment 1988** (the instrument
  cited in the 2020 mass raid below). — https://beta.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2020/10/30/rights-groups-concerned-over-probe-into-kedah-event-organiser-for-encouragi/1917958
- **Federal Territories** (Kuala Lumpur `15666254B97003483933575`, Labuan `15666254B4265207353082`,
  Putrajaya `15666254B54729125478011`) are under federal Shariah law rather than a state enactment, so
  the applicable text differs from any state's — an administrative-legal difference, not a moral-code
  difference.
- **Sabah** (`15666254B50935235229272`) and **Sarawak** (`15666254B8535925993004`) have their own,
  less expansive Shariah enactments and no equivalent gender-transition offence was found; no
  state-specific incident data was found either.

## Regions with findings

### Kelantan (`15666254B53975248776023`)
Direction: worse than national — the only Southeast Asian sub-national unit that has legislated gender transition itself as a crime, and has prosecuted under it.
- **Clause 17 of the new Kelantan Syariah Bill (passed 12–13 August 2026)** makes it an offence for a
  Muslim to **change their own gender, cause another person's gender to be changed, or induce someone to
  do so** without a reason permitted under Islamic law — punishable by a **fine up to RM3,000,
  imprisonment up to two years, or both**. The Bill also adds "protection of modesty" provisions; it
  replaces the 16 provisions of the 2019 Enactment that the Federal Court voided in February 2024. — https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/12/gender-change-protection-of-modesty-among-provisions-in-new-kelantan-syariah-bill · https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/13/kelantan-passes-strict-new-decency-rules-in-syariah-bill
- **The predecessor law was actually used:** Kelantan recorded its **first prosecution for the offence of
  gender transition** under the Syariah Criminal Code Enactment (I) 2019 — a case involving a **trans woman
  heard at the Kelantan Syariah Court in Kota Bharu**, after the **Kelantan Islamic Affairs Department
  (Jaheaik) arrested her in an operation targeting an event attended by a group of transgender
  individuals**. — https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/767244
- Sequence matters for scoring: the state was told by its own apex court that it lacked the power, and
  legislated again anyway. That is an institutional commitment, not a stray provision.

### Terengganu (`15666254B71460903090065`)
Direction: worse than national — the state's own executive says its purpose is to generate hostility.
- The Kuala Terengganu authority installed **warning signboards about homosexual practice at several
  strategic locations** in the state, framing homosexuality as "dirty"; when criticised, state local-government,
  housing and health exco member **Datuk Wan Sukairi Wan Abdullah confirmed this was deliberate**: the
  signboards were meant "to instil hatred towards such acts among the community", as part of educating
  people away from what contravenes religious values and societal norms. — https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/05/10/inciting-hatred-of-039dirty039-homosexuality-is-the-point-says-terengganu-exco-member · https://newswav.com/article/memang-kita-mahu-timbulkan-kebencian-terhadap-homoseksual-exco-terengganu-A2505_Xl9FsL
- **Justice for Sisters and 12 other civil-society organisations** demanded removal on 14 May 2025,
  calling the boards discriminatory and a human-rights violation — i.e. a documented official statement of
  intent to hostile effect, plus a documented civil-society counterweight. — https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2025/05/14/civil-groups-urge-terengganu-authorities-to-remove-anti-lgbt-signboards-warn-of-human-rights-violations/176752

### Johor (`15666254B42356713390762`)
Direction: worse than national — state-funded conversion infrastructure.
- Johor established a **RM400,000 centre to "rehabilitate" members of deviant groups and people in
  same-sex relationships**, described by the state Islamic religious affairs committee chairman **Mohd
  Fared Mohd Khalid** as designed for individuals with "distorted" beliefs and **those convicted by the
  Syariah Court for same-sex relationships**, "to help them resume a normal life". — https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/02/14/johor-sets-up-rehab-for-same-sex-relationships
- Independent ethnographic research documents how Malaysian trans women are channelled into **state and
  religious-institution "moral reform" programmes aimed at "rehabilitating" LGBTQ+ Muslims** — i.e. the
  rehabilitation apparatus is real, funded, and reaches trans women specifically. — https://www.sapiens.org/culture/trans-women-malaysia-conversion-therapy-islam/

### Kedah (`15666254B71428205180864`)
Direction: worse than national — a raid whose entire purpose was to isolate trans women.
- **27 October 2020:** the **Kedah Islamic Department raided a birthday party at which 30 transgender
  women, out of roughly 100 attendees, were singled out** and served notice to report back to the state
  religious department for investigation **under the Kedah Shariah Criminal Offences Enactment 1988**; the
  event organiser was separately probed for "encouraging vice", which rights groups condemned. — https://beta.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2020/10/30/rights-groups-concerned-over-probe-into-kedah-event-organiser-for-encouragi/1917958

### Selangor (`15666254B89722251658211`)
Direction: worse than national — the wealthiest, most "progressive-governed" state is not a safe harbour.
- **10 January 2026:** the Selangor government said it **"will not allow LGBT promotion"** in the state
  and called for an investigation into the organisation of a **"Glamping with Pride"** gathering, with the
  Youth, Sports and Entrepreneurship committee chairman **Mohd Najwan Halimi** saying the state "would not
  compromise on any attempts to spread or promote lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender culture". — https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/01/10/selangor-govt-says-it-will-not-allow-lgbt-promotion-calls-probe-into-glamping-with-pride/204863
- In December 2025 the **Sultan of Selangor** used Friday sermons across state mosques to **warn Muslims
  against LGBT practices** — the monarchy-plus-religious-bureaucracy channel operating statewide. — https://www.nst.com.my/news/nation/2025-12-1335977/sultan-selangor-warns-against-lgbt-practices
- Historically Selangor's religious department **JAIS** is the agency that pursued the entrepreneur **Nur
  Sajat**, issuing an arrest warrant and launching a manhunt over her presentation, which made Selangor the
  most visible arena for cross-dressing enforcement in Malaysia. — https://www.therakyatpost.com/news/malaysia/2021/03/01/sajat-vs-jais-heres-a-tldr-of-the-drama-surrounding-the-arrest/
- Selangor also has the strongest documented *prosecution* record for violence against trans people: after
  a 32-year-old transgender woman died of multiple injuries following an attack by a group of youths in
  **Klang**, **four suspects were arrested and charged**, and Reuters reported campaigners feared a rise in
  hate crimes rather than denying the motive. — https://www.reuters.com/article/world/us/death-of-transgender-woman-in-malaysia-sparks-fears-of-rising-hate-crime-idUSL8N1YM12C/ · https://www.nst.com.my/news/crime-courts/2018-12-443248/four-be-charged-over-deadly-assault-transgender-person-klang

### Malacca (`15666254B45695250728659`)
Direction: worse than national — religious authorities detaining someone for presenting as a woman in public.
- The **Melaka Islamic Religious Department (JAIM) detained a 25-year-old man dressed and behaving like a
  woman while he was doing a TikTok livestream in Banda Hilir** in the early hours — a state religious
  agency acting against public gender expression as such, in a state whose tourism brand is heritage-city
  strolling. — https://content.astroawani.com/berita-malaysia/pempengaruh-berpakaian-wanita-ditahan-jaim-ketika-buat-live-tiktok-528288

### Perak (`15666254B15173108486297`)
Direction: mixed — a killing of a gender-nonconforming person, with an actual murder charge brought.
- **27 August 2025, Ipoh:** a person reported as a man "believed to be transgender because of feminine
  characteristics" was found dead and bloodied in an alley near Jalan Horley after allegedly being chased
  down and **run over by a lorry**; on **4 September 2025 a 32-year-old lorry driver, M I Saravanan, was
  charged with murder** before Ipoh Sessions Court. Motive (anti-trans animus versus traffic rage) was not
  established, so this is recorded as a **prosecution that happened**, not as a hate-crime finding. — https://newswav.com/article/man-believed-to-be-trans-found-dead-after-being-chased-run-over-by-lorry-in-A2508_Ox4oyS · https://berita.rtm.gov.my/kes/senarai-berita-kes/senarai-artikel/pemandu-lori-didakwa-bunuh-lelaki-berpenampilan-wanita-di-ipoh/

### Pahang (`15666254B34720353032971`)
Direction: worse than national — the canonical bad-response case.
- **23 February 2017, Kuantan:** **Sameera Krishnan, a 27-year-old transgender florist worker, was stabbed
  and shot up to three times**; Human Rights Watch demanded prosecution of the perpetrators. The Pahang CID
  chief then said police had **"found no element of hate crime"** and were instead **investigating the
  victim's own past activities** and a possible link to a kidnapping in Klang two years earlier — the
  victim-as-suspect framing the brief treats as damning. — https://www.hrw.org/news/2017/02/24/transgender-woman-murdered-malaysia · https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2017/02/26/transgender-murder-not-hate-crime-cops-say/1323421

### Federal-level posture (bounds all 16 units)
- The **Religious Affairs Minister Datuk Mohd Na'im Mokhtar** said in May 2025 that **even closed-door
  LGBTQ-friendly events are "against societal norms"**, that "any attempt to normalise such deviant
  lifestyles is contrary to the Federal Constitution, existing laws and official government policy", and
  urged police to investigate and act — the doctrine that makes private assembly itself a police matter
  nationwide. — https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2025/05/29/religious-affairs-minister-says-even-closed-door-lgbtq-friendly-events-against-societal-norms-urges-cops-to-probe-and-act/178539
- Every state has its own religious department with enforcement powers (Jaheaik in Kelantan, JAIM in
  Melaka, JAIS in Selangor, the Kedah Islamic Department), so the enforcement variable is genuinely
  state-by-state even where the offence texts look similar.

## Regions with no sub-national signal
Negeri Sembilan, Pahang beyond the 2017 killing, Penang, Perlis, Sabah, Sarawak — and the Federal
Territories of Kuala Lumpur, Labuan and Putrajaya as distinct units — produced no unit-specific
trans-related source in this pass. Penang and the two Borneo states are conventionally treated as
less restrictive, but that is inference, not documentation: **do not score them as "better" on
absence of evidence.** Perlis has no finding but is the most conservative peninsula state after
Kelantan/Terengganu in the political record, and no source in this pass confirms or denies it.

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **The primary variable in Malaysia is not state-vs-state hostility but state-vs-state *enforcement
  capacity*, plus one state (Kelantan) with a distinct criminal law.** Sharia jurisdiction reaches only
  Muslims and only within Malaysia, so a **foreign non-Muslim visitor is generally outside the Sharia
  system** — which materially changes how these units should be scored for an international traveller
  compared with a Malaysian one. The civil-law risks (public-order, indecency, immigration detention,
  and violence) apply to everyone; the Sharia risks apply to Muslim travellers and to any visitor
  travelling with, or mistaken for, a local Muslim companion.
- Because of that, the **Kelantan 2026 gender-transition offence and the Kedah/Kelantan religious-department
  raids are still worth flagging**: they set the national policy frontier (other states copy
  provisions — Kelantan's 2019 code was itself the template others compared against), and they are what
  federal "Malaysia Madani" rhetoric gets measured against.
- **No Malaysian state has a bathroom/facility law**, and no Pride event ban was documented in this pass;
  Malaysia's LGBTQ+ gatherings are held privately and discreetly, which is precisely what the minister's
  statement about closed-door events targets.
- Violence against trans people in Malaysia is under-tracked because **"hate crime" is not a category in
  Malaysian law or police practice** — Pahang's refusal to see a hate motive in 2017 is structural, not
  local. Use the enforcement/official-statement record as the primary regional signal.
- Malaysia's national anchor is 0.156 (close to Indonesia's 0.155). Kelantan should sit distinctly below
  it; Sabah/Sarawak plausibly above it but undocumented here.
