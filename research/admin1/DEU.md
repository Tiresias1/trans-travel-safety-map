# DEU — Germany · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 16 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
RICH — the divergence is real and two-sided: **four eastern Landtage have legislated or moved
against self-determination/gender policy** (Sachsen, Sachsen-Anhalt, Thüringen, plus Bayern in the
south), while an **amadeu-antonio-stiftung security report documents record far-right attacks on
Pride events in 2025 by location**, and police now publish queer-hostile offence counts that differ
sharply by Land. Evidence is concentrated in ~6 units (Sachsen, Brandenburg, Thüringen,
Sachsen-Anhalt, Bayern, Berlin); the remaining ten have framework-level differences only.

## Different-legal-system regions
**None.** Criminal law, the general Equal Treatment Act (AGG), passport/registration law and the
2024 Self-Determination Act (SBGG) are federal and uniform across all 16 Länder. What *does* differ
is which Land controls the implementation layer: Standesämter (registry offices), schools, police
and public administration are Land competences, so a federal law can be administered hospitably or
hostilely depending on the Land — which is precisely what the findings below record. No religious or
customary courts have any jurisdiction.

## Regions with findings

### Sachsen (`10402087B89016843004490`)
Direction: worse than national — the epicentre of both the legislative offensive and the street attacks.
- **Bautzen, summer 2024: a mob of more than 700 neo-Nazis assembled at the pride parade** and
  disrupted its progress — the single largest organised counter-mobilisation of the season, in a
  mid-sized Saxon town; the 2024 season also produced nine other pride-event incidents. The 2025
  event returned under a heavy police presence, with organisers describing Saxony's countryside as
  "queer hinterland" facing "aggressive right-wing extremists". — https://rega-net.org/en/research/report/germany-no-longer-safe-attacks-on-pride-events-as-physical-manifestations-of-anti-queer-backlash · https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/csd-und-gegendemo-in-bautzen-pride-im-queeren-hinterland-mit-aggressiven-rechtsextremen-14157878.html
- **Döbeln (central Saxony), 20 September 2025:** ~720 people at CSD, a simultaneous counter-march
  through the town, **seven criminal offences recorded** by police and a formal investigation opened
  after a Hitler salute. — https://www.diesachsen.de/en/social/csd-in-doebeln-investigation-after-hitler-salute-3056653
- **Dresden 2025:** thousands marched through Alt- and Neustadt while **neo-Nazis attempted to
  disrupt the parade** with a counter-protest. — https://www.tag24.de/thema/demonstration/bunt-und-laut-csd-teilnehmer-ziehen-durch-dresden-neonazis-mit-gegenprotest-3390855
- **Legislative initiative:** Saxony joined Sachsen-Anhalt and Thüringen in a joint Länder
  Bundesrat move to **tighten the federal Self-Determination Act (SBGG)** in the wake of the
  "Fall Liebich", aiming to restrict how trans and intersex people use the right to change legal
  gender — an organised, multi-state attempt to roll back Germany's self-ID law. — https://www.antenne.de/nachrichten/welt/laender-wollen-gesetz-zur-selbstbestimmung-nachschaerfen

### Sachsen-Anhalt (`10402087B23625801106445`)
Direction: worse than national — co-author of the SBGG-tightening initiative.
- Partner in the Sachsen/Thüringen-led Länder initiative to make the Self-Determination Act harder
  to use, on the stated ground that self-determination rights are being "abused". — https://www.antenne.de/nachrichten/welt/laender-wollen-gesetz-zur-selbstbestimmung-nachschaerfen

### Thüringen (`10402087B67217417145666`)
Direction: worse than national — a parliament formally hostile to gender policy.
- AfD fraction motion **"Keine Gendersprache an Thüringer Schulen"** demanding the Landtag's ban on
  gender-inclusive language in schools be consistently implemented, asserting schools must not be
  "a socio-political experimental field". — https://parldok.thueringer-landtag.de/ParlDok/dokument/100119/keine_gendersprache_an_thueringer_schulen_landtagsbeschluss_konsequent_umsetzen.pdf
- Draft **"Thüringer Gesetz zur Beendigung gleichstellungspolitischer Maßnahmen"** — a bill to
  *end* state equality-policy machinery, arguing that the existing Thuringian equality law
  "regularly violates the principle of equal opportunity" by enforcing equality of results. — https://parldok.thltcloud.de/parldok/dokument/106316/8_3111_thueringer_gesetz_zur_beendigung_gleichstellungspolitischer_massnahmen
- Also a partner in the SBGG-tightening initiative. — https://www.antenne.de/nachrichten/welt/laender-wollen-gesetz-zur-selbstbestimmung-nachschaerfen

### Bayern (`10402087B60477050509260`)
Direction: worse than national on official posture; no evidence of higher street danger.
- **Gender-language ban in force since 1 April 2024:** schools, universities and all public
  administration bodies are **prohibited** from using gender-inclusive German; the rule came from a
  Söder government declaration in December 2023 ("we will even prohibit gendering in schools and
  administration") and a cabinet decision amending the language regulations of public institutions.
  Review bodies found no court consequences, and commentators describe it as "pure symbol politics"
  — but the practical effect for a visitor is that **every public counter, registry office and
  school in Bavaria operates under an instruction that official gender-inclusive framing is
  forbidden**. — https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/gesellschaft/gender-verbot-bayern-100.html · https://apnews.com/article/germany-gender-language-bavaria-change-school-grammar-8a0ae45ebf0134627221382d0bbb0ef0 · https://www.sueddeutsche.de/bayern/bayern-gender-verbot-1-april-li.3226657
- Bavarian police publish their own hate-crime situation report: **2,021 recorded hate crimes in
  2024, up 8.2% from 1,867 in 2023**, roughly two-thirds politically motivated; the state LKA's
  LSBTIQ-specific breakdown was contested in a parliamentary query (Drucksache 19/5811, Feb 2025)
  about how many were motivated by hostility to sexual orientation versus gender-related animus. — https://www.stmi.bayern.de/presse-und-medien/pressemitteilungen/detail/herrmann-stellt-das-lagebild-zur-hasskriminalitaet-2024-vor/ · https://www.lsvd.de/media/doc/2445/260107_schriftliche_anfrage_florian_siekmann_hasskriminalit__t.pdf

### Brandenburg (`10402087B40185768535592`)
Direction: worse than national — arson targeting a queer venue before a Pride event.
- **Cottbus, 20 October 2025:** a paper bin was set alight against the wall of the
  "Regenbogenkombinat" queer cultural centre ("Bunte Welt") in the afternoon; staff tried to
  extinguish it, the façade was damaged, and **state security (Staatsschutz) opened an arson
  investigation**. The report links it to a run of earlier suspected far-right attacks in the city,
  including incendiary devices aimed at an alternative housing project. — https://www.lr-online.de/lausitz/cottbus/csd-in-cottbus-staatsschutz-ermittelt-nach-brand-bei-regenbogenkombinat-78394024.html · https://www.maz-online.de/brandenburg/cottbus-mutmasslicher-brandanschlag-auf-kulturzentrum-bunte-welt-vor-csd-umzug-CYWKC5SUWRFVTHAQE2RID2AYIA.html
- The subsequent CSD Cottbus & Niederlausitz (17th edition) ran under a **large police presence with
  two counter-demonstrations**, one organised by the far-right group "Deutsche Jugend vor…"; the
  previous year's parade had also drawn far-right counter-protests, and the queer community
  described itself as "particularly watchful" because of the preceding incidents. — https://www.niederlausitz-aktuell.de/niederlausitz-aktuell/orte/cottbus/303318/cottbuser-csd-parade-mit-grossaufgebot-der-polizei-zwei-gegendemos.html · https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/pride-parade-held-germanys-cottbus-164820809.html · https://www.welt.de/regionales/berlin/article6a50cbee43b7fd5185005019/nach-angriffen-cottbus-setzt-beim-csd-zeichen-gegen-rechts.html
- Pattern, not single incident: two venue attacks plus a year-on-year counter-protest history plus
  Staatsschutz involvement. Mitigating positive response: police protection and a security posture
  that let the parade proceed without major disruption.

### Berlin (`10402087B20892132820961`)
Direction: mixed — the most institutionalised protection in Germany, and now an explicitly warned-about event environment.
- The organisers of the **Berlin Christopher Street Day urged participants to be vigilant** ahead of
  the 2025 event "amid a rise in attacks on LGBTQ+ events across Germany", with hundreds of
  thousands expected — the Guardian's reporting frames Berlin as the place where Pride is largest
  but where organisers now issue security advice. — https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/jul/26/its-time-for-us-to-be-louder-germanys-pride-parades-face-up-to-rise-in-attacks
- Berlin remains the jurisdiction with the deepest anti-discrimination apparatus and the largest
  visible community; the warning is about the event not the everyday.

### Hessen (`10402087B85226028911536`)
Direction: worse than its reputation would suggest, or simply better reporting.
- Landtag records that **queer-hostile offences in Hessen rose from 33 in 2022 to 83 in 2023**
  (parliamentary inquiry, Drucksache 20/14942-era question), with the questioners noting queer
  people remain disproportionately affected by hate crime. A 150% jump in two years in one Land is
  either a real deterioration or a reporting change — an assessor should weight it accordingly. — https://starweb.hessen.de/cache/DRS/21/4/02154.pdf

### National frame that bounds all 16 units
- The **Federal Criminal Police Office (BKA), with the Interior Ministry, published a dedicated
  situation report on the crime-related security of LSBTIQ* people** ("Sicherheit von LSBTIQ*
  Menschen in Deutschland"), showing a worrying multi-year rise in police-registered queer-hostile
  offences and introducing a new reporting category — this is the instrument that makes
  Land-by-Land comparison possible. — https://www.bka.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Publikationen/JahresberichteUndLagebilder/SicherheitLSBTIQ/SicherheitLSBTIQ.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=6
- The **Amadeu Antonio Stiftung's 2025 security report "Queerfeindlichkeit sichtbar machen"** (its
  first) documents that 2025 hosted **a record 245 CSD events and, simultaneously, a record high in
  right-wing attacks on them**, analysing the *organised* far-right mobilisation against specific
  events. This is the single best per-location risk map for a visitor in Germany. — https://www.amadeu-antonio-stiftung.de/angriffe-auf-csds-erreichen-2025-rekordniveau-151491/

## Regions with no sub-national signal
Baden-Württemberg, Bremen, Hamburg, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Niedersachsen, Nordrhein-Westfalen,
Rheinland-Pfalz, Saarland, Schleswig-Holstein — no Land-specific trans statute, ordinance,
enforcement series or event-security finding was located in this pass. Sachsen and Bayern also have
documented *statutory* divergences not repeated elsewhere; the ten units above should default to the
national score.

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **Germany's clearest gradient is east-vs-west and city-vs-rural, not Land-vs-Land.** Every
  documented attack site in this pass is in the east (Bautzen, Döbeln, Dresden, Cottbus) but each is
  a *small or mid-sized town* — rega-net's framing is explicitly that Pride in the eastern provinces
  is no longer safe, and the Bautzen 2025 coverage describes marching "for life in the countryside".
  Leipzig, Berlin, Dresden's inner city and the western metros remain comparatively safe; Saxony's
  villages do not. Do not read "Sachsen is worse" as "Leipzig is worse".
- **The legislative threat is to a federal law, not a Land law.** The SBGG (self-ID) applies
  nationwide; Sachsen/Sachsen-Anhalt/Thüringen are trying to change it through the Bundesrat. If they
  succeed the divergence disappears (nationally worse); if they fail, the eastern Länder keep a
  documented hostility record with no legal instrument. Either way, the attempt itself is
  evidence of official climate and should be scored.
- **The Bavarian language ban is climate, not exposure.** A visitor is not prosecuted for gendern;
  what changes is the disposition of the public-sector staff a visitor must deal with (registry,
  school, police station, hospital admission). Weight it as a service-quality/hostility signal.
- **Registration friction is administered locally.** Gender-marker and name changes under the SBGG
  run through Land-level registry offices and Standesämter, and three Länder are publicly
  demanding the law be tightened — expect uneven implementation within the same federal statute,
  though no per-Land refusal data was found.
- **Hate-crime statistics are a Land-by-Land production, not a Land-by-Land reality.** Bavaria and
  Hessen publish their own Lagebilder; the BKA now runs a federal LSBTIQ category. A high count may
  mean better police classification (as in Hessen's jump), so use these numbers for trend and
  official attention, not for ranking danger between Länder.
