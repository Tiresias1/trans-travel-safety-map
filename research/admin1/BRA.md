# BRA — Brazil · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 27 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
RICH — ANTRA's annual *Dossiê* publishes **killings per state for every unit** for 2024 and 2025,
which gives genuine per-region violence data with counts; plus one state (Santa Catarina) has now
passed an actual statutory bathroom restriction and was the venue of a nationally significant STF
ruling upholding a bathroom refusal; plus a documented extortion pattern involving a public
employee in Niterói (Rio de Janeiro state). Caveat carried throughout: Brazil's world-leading
lethality sits on top of a *nationally protective* STF framework, so the state numbers measure
exposure, not legal hostility.

## Different-legal-system regions
**None.** All 27 units are under the same federal constitution and penal code; there is no
religious or customary criminal jurisdiction. Two qualifications worth recording:
- **Federally administered indigenous lands and quilombola territories** sit inside several units
  (Amazonas, Roraima, Mato Grosso do Sul, Bahia) with parallel community authority; no sourced
  trans-specific evidence was found for them.
- **Municipal autonomy** is real in Brazil: city and state ordinances on facilities are adopted
  locally (Santa Catarina's new law expressly binds state *and municipal* administration), so some
  of the sharpest variation in this file is below ADM1 level.

## Regions with findings

### Santa Catarina (`14911670B90188072957077`)
Direction: worse than national — the only Brazilian state to have enacted a statutory bathroom restriction, and the venue where the STF let a refusal stand.
- **Lei nº 19.686 de 21/01/2026** (signed by Governor Jorginho Mello on 26 January 2026): educational
  institutions of every level in the state — basic, fundamental, secondary, technical and higher
  education — must provide bathrooms **separately by sex**, prohibiting shared/unisex installation
  and use; non-compliance carries fines of up to R$10,000. A second bill (PL 491/2021, approved
  22 December 2025) bans neutral bathrooms in state and municipal public administration bodies. — https://www.legisweb.com.br/legislacao/?id=489796 · https://www.michelteixeira.com.br/sc-aprova-projeto-proibindo-banheiro-neutro-em-reparticoes-publicas-do-estado-e-municipios/ · https://www.equaldex.com/region/santa-catarina-brazil
- A further bill (PL 771/2025, tabled 22 Oct 2025, read in plenary 23 Oct) would restrict school and
  locker-room use to the "biological sex recorded on the birth certificate" — indicating continued
  legislative escalation, not a one-off. — https://floripa.lgbt/politica/pl-que-restringe-banheiros-por-sexo-biologico-acende-alerta-sobre-direitos-trans-em-sc/
- **The decisive national case came out of this state:** a trans woman was stopped from using the
  women's bathroom by staff at a Florianópolis shopping centre, urinated on herself in front of
  shoppers, and had to take public transport home; on 6 June 2024 the **Supreme Tribunal Federal
  voted to reject her appeal**, leaving the refusal lawful under ordinary private-premises rules. — https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/justica/noticia/2024-06/stf-rejeita-recurso-de-mulher-trans-barrada-em-banheiro-de-shopping
- ANTRA, ABGLT and IBRAT responded by **denouncing Brazil to the Inter-American Commission on Human
  Rights** over the STF's inaction in RE 845.779/SC (August 2025) — i.e. the region-specific
  incident generated an international complaint against the federal state. — https://antrabrasil.org/2025/08/20/brasil-denunciado-a-cidh-acao-banheiros-trans/
- Note: the STF framework remains *protective* nationally, which makes this state's divergence the
  clearest legal-exposure signal in Brazil.

### São Paulo (`14911670B46470234103867`)
Direction: mixed — highest absolute count in 2024 and the economic core, but the count then fell.
- **Led the national ranking in 2024 with 16 killings** of travestis and trans people. — https://g1.globo.com/df/distrito-federal/noticia/2025/01/27/dossie-revela-que-122-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-foram-assassinadas-no-brasil-em-2024-maioria-jovem-negra-e-pobre.ghtml · https://sergipeemdebate.com.br/numero-de-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-assassinadas-no-brasil-cai-em-2024/
- Fell to 4 recorded killings in 2025 (regional ranking: out of the top group). — https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/brasil-lidera-ranking-mundial-de-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans
- The 2024 dossier's key structural finding applies here too: **at least 68% of the killings (83 of 122) happened outside state capitals, in interior cities** — so São Paulo's interior, not the capital, is where the risk sits. — https://sergipeemdebate.com.br/numero-de-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-assassinadas-no-brasil-cai-em-2024/

### Ceará (`14911670B7632012573600`)
Direction: worse than national — the most consistent lethal hotspot in the Northeast.
- 11 killings in 2024 (3rd nationally, **1st in the Northeast**); **8 killings in 2025 — tied for the highest state total in the country.** — https://g1.globo.com/ce/ceara/noticia/2025/01/28/ceara-foi-o-estado-do-nordeste-com-mais-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans-em-2024-diz-dossie.ghtml · https://igbaagencia.com.br/2026/01/26/ceara-lidera-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans-em-2025-dossie-aponta-maioria-de-vitimas-negras-no-pais/
- Racial pattern in the 2025 data: of the 57 cases where race could be established, the majority of victims were Black — Ceará is where that concentration is largest. — https://igbaagencia.com.br/2026/01/26/ceara-lidera-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans-em-2025-dossie-aponta-maioria-de-vitimas-negras-no-pais/

### Minas Gerais (`14911670B44044837234259`)
Direction: worse than national — tied top in 2025, 2nd in 2024.

### Goiás (`14911670B6484683145874`), Maranhão (`14911670B41041569544971`), Pará (`14911670B48776179681147`)
Direction: worse than national — 5 killings each in 2025.
- Three states in the same band, spanning the Cerrado agricultural frontier, the poor Northeast interior and the Amazon; Maranhão in particular is one of the poorest states in the federation. — https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/brasil-lidera-ranking-mundial-de-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans

### Rio Granda do Norte (`14911670B91095571793858`)
Direction: better than national on record, but volatile —
- Zero recorded killings in 2024, then 4 in 2025 — the sharpest deterioration in the dataset. A state that appears safe in one year's dossier can disappear from the top of the list the next; treat single-year zeros as noise. — https://sergipeemdebate.com.br/numero-de-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-assassinadas-no-brasil-cai-em-2024/ · https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/brasil-lidera-ranking-mundial-de-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans

### Rio de Jeneiro (`14911670B85657526756793`) — including Niterói
Direction: mixed — moderate killing count, but a documented extortion regime with a public employee inside it.
- 3 killings recorded in 2025 (low band). — https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/brasil-lidera-ranking-mundial-de-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans
- **Pattern, state-implicated (Jan–Feb 2026):** trans sex workers in central Niterói reported being
  forced to pay R$120 per week each to a man who claimed connections with **militias and public
  agents**; he had reportedly "almost strangled three of the girls". The alleged pimp was an
  **ex-employee of Niterói city hall**, dismissed in January 2026 after the complaints, arrested in
  "Operação Ronda Sumbria" by the 76ª DP, and remanded in pre-trial custody by the TJRJ. Council
  member Benny Briolly (herself a trans elected official) took the case to the Public Ministry and
  human-rights commissions. Recorded as a pattern (multiple complainants, ~2 years running) with a
  mixed institutional response: arrests and a dismissal happened, but the protection racket relied
  on the appearance of state/militia backing. — https://oglobo.globo.com/rio/bairros/niteroi/noticia/2026/02/03/ele-ja-chegou-a-quase-enforcar-tres-meninas-mulheres-trans-que-vivem-da-prostituicao-em-niteroi-dizem-que-homem-exige-r-120-por-semana-de-cada-uma.ghtml · https://oglobo.globo.com/rio/bairros/niteroi/noticia/2026/01/29/mulheres-trans-denunciam-acao-de-cafetao-em-niteroi-e-vereadora-vai-ao-mp.ghtml · https://extra.globo.com/rio/casos-de-policia/noticia/2026/02/justica-decreta-prisao-preventiva-de-acusado-de-explorar-mulheres-trans-em-niteroi-homem-era-funcionario-da-prefeitura-ate-janeiro.ghtml · https://cidadedeniteroi.com/policia/operacao-em-niteroi-prende-ex-servidor-por-cafetinagem/
- **Visitor-facing reverse direction:** in Copacabana, the tourist police delegation (Deat) arrested
  two trans women in flagrente for extorting a **Norwegian tourist** with threats to circulate
  intimate videos — a documented scam vector aimed specifically at visitors. — https://www.tupi.fm/sentinelas/mulheres-trans-presas-extorsao-turista-copacabana/

- The **Northeast recorded 38 of the 80 national killings in 2025** — nearly half the country total — against the Southeast's 17; ANTRA's victim profile is "young, Black, poor and **from the Northeast**". Paraíba and Rio Grande do Norte had 4 each, Alagoas 2, and the region's states dominate the mid-table. — https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/brasil-lidera-ranking-mundial-de-assassinatos-de-pessoas-trans · https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/nacional/brasil-e-o-pais-que-mais-mata-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-aponta-dossie/

### Acre (`14911670B96632893955983`), Roraima (`14911670B45290909693812`), Amapá (`14911670B26412867168762`), Amazonas (`14911670B46798344041190`)

## Regions with no sub-national signal
No unit lacks data entirely (ANTRA reports counts for all 27). No incident-, ordinance- or
enforcement-level evidence beyond the annual count was found for: Amapá, Amazonas, Espírito Santo
(`14911670B28216513632935`, 2 killings in 2025), Mato Grosso (`14911670B2082355341666`, 3), Mato
Grosso do Sul (`14911670B40722526455680`, 2), Paraná (`14911670B30315977547221`, 4), Piauí, Roraima,
Sergipe, Tocantins (`14911670B72497658138655`) and Acre.

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **The dominant geographic fact is interior-vs-capital, not state-vs-state:** at least **68% of the
  2024 killings (83 of 122) occurred outside state capitals**, in interior towns. A state average
  therefore hides the actual risk geography, which is small-town Brazil. This is the single most
  important thing an assessor should know about the Brazilian map. — https://sergipeemdebate.com.br/numero-de-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-assassinadas-no-brasil-cai-em-2024/
- **Legal ceiling vs lethal floor.** The STF has criminalised LGBT+ phobia (2019,equating it with racism) and
  recognises gender identity without surgery, yet Brazil remained the world's deadliest country for
  trans people for the **18th consecutive year** (2025 dossier). Deaths fell: 122 (2024) → 80 (2025),
  −34%, but attempted homicides rose and public-space killings with "requintes de crueldade"
  (extreme cruelty) continue; youngest 2024 victim was 15. — https://valorinternational.globo.com/politics/news/2026/01/27/murders-of-transgender-people-fall-in-the-last-two-years-in-brazil.ghtml · https://antrabrasil.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/dossie-antra-2026.pdf · https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/nacional/brasil-e-o-pais-que-mais-mata-pessoas-trans-e-travestis-aponta-dossie/
- **Under-counting is systematic**, so state counts are floors, not totals; ANTRA itself says the
  decline partly reflects monitoring capacity. Do not read a small state's zero as safety. — https://antrabrasil.org/assassinatos/
- **Santa Catarina is the only unit where a visitor's *legal* exposure differs from the national
  rule**, and it did so twice in three months (Dec 2025 and Jan 2026). Everything else in Brazil is
  a violence/policing gradient.
- Victim profile to keep in view when scoring: mostly trans women, young, Black, poor, Northeastern,
  frequently in sex work — so the risk is concentrated on people who are *visibly* trans in public
  space, which is exactly the exposure profile of a traveller who presents conspicuously.
- Tourism-specific note: the Copacabana extortion case shows the transaction can run the other way
  (trans sex workers → foreign tourist). An assessor should not read "trans people are victims in
  Rio" as "a trans visitor is not also exposed to being used as a victim-perpetrator reversal in
  scams".
