# PER — Peru · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 26 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
MEDIUM — Peru has one genuinely useful **official** regional dataset: the Public Ministry's own
criminological study of intentional deaths of LGTB people 2012–2021, which found **58% of those deaths
occurred in just six "distritos fiscales" (Lima 12 cases, La Libertad 10, Lima Norte, and three others)** —
a real per-region lethality ranking produced by the state. To that add a regional protective ordinance
(**La Libertad**), a provincial hate-crime investigation (**Tacna**), two 2025 killings in the Lima region,
and the national baseline of 54 killings 2020–2023 from the Cayetano Heredia University observatory.
Four to five of 26 units have distinct evidence; the rest is national.

## Different-legal-system regions
**None.** Peru has one criminal code and one judicial system; no religious or customary criminal
jurisdiction. Two qualifications:
- **Rondero / campesino community justice** ("rondas campesinas") exercises recognised
  dispute-resolution authority in parts of **Cajamarca, Lambayeque, La Libertad, Amazonas, Huánuco and
  Ayacucho**, and the constitution permits community justice within human-rights limits. No source in this
  pass documents a trans-specific rondas case, so this is recorded as a *structural* caveat (in these
  departments a dispute may be settled by an assembly rather than a court) and nothing more.
- **Regional governments do legislate on trans inclusion.** The **Direcciones Regionales de Educación** are
  organs of the regional government (art. 76 of the General Education Law 28044), so education policy —
  where Peru's anti-"ideología de género" conflict actually bites — is regionally administered. See the
  Lambayeque regional management resolution cited below.

## Regions with findings

### Lima (`1875124B65618634887963`) — and the paired unit `Municipalidad Metropolitana de Lima` (`1875124B43998217738279`)
Direction: worse than national — the top homicide venue in the state's own data, and the site of 2025 killings.
- The Public Ministry's study *Características criminológicas de las muertes dolosas de personas LGTB en el
  Perú 2012–2021* identified **84 criminal complaints, 88 victims and 72 identified perpetrators**, and
  found **58% of the deaths occurred in six distritos fiscales, led by Lima (12) and La Libertad (10), with
  Lima Norte among them**. Lima Centro also concentrates the highest national frequency of reported crimes
  overall (10.0%; eleven fiscal districts hold 62%). — https://www.gob.pe/institucion/mpfn/noticias/594143-ministerio-publico-identifico-88-homicidios-de-personas-lgtb-entre-los-anos-2012-y-2021 · https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/9663523/7909946-boletin-de-delitos-denunciados-2025-ofobcri.pdf
- **24–28 August 2025, Independencia (northern Lima):** **Rubi Viena**, a trans woman, was reported missing
  on 24 August and found dead **in her own home** four days later; colleagues at the Fevacel market and
  relatives publicly demanded justice. — https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2025/08/29/independencia-mujer-transgenero-reportada-desaparecida-es-hallada-sin-vida-y-allegados-exigen-justicia-1833960
- **Boundary-data note for the scorer:** this ADM1 layer carries both `Lima`
  (`1875124B65618634887963`) and `Municipalidad Metropolitana de Lima`
  (`1875124B43998217738279`), plus `El Callao` (`1875124B73300566086976`). Greater Lima's fiscal districts
  (Lima, Lima Norte, Lima Centro) span those overlapping polygons, so the Lima findings here should be
  applied to both Lima units rather than split arbitrarily.

### La Libertad (`1875124B91024374055933`)
Direction: **mixed — second-deadliest jurisdiction in the official data, and the one region with a protective regional ordinance.**
- **10 of the 88 recorded intentional deaths of LGTB people (2012–2021) fell in the La Libertad distrito
  fiscal**, second only to Lima — a Trujillo-centred concentration that is not explained by population
  alone. — https://www.gob.pe/institucion/mpfn/noticias/594143-ministerio-publico-identifico-88-homicidios-de-personas-lgtb-entre-los-anos-2012-y-2021
- **Ordenanza Regional N.° 009-2024-GRLL-CR** (regional council session 3 September 2024, approved 6
  September 2024, published in El Peruano 7 November 2024) **"promotes the labour inclusion of travesti,
  transsexual and transgender people in the La Libertad Region"**, affirming their right to formal, dign
  work in equitable conditions. — https://www.gob.pe/institucion/regionlalibertad/normas-legales/6015324-009-2024-grll-cr · https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2340675-1
- Caveat per the brief: a labour-inclusion ordinance is a **resident-facing** instrument a visitor never
  uses; it is recorded here only as evidence that this particular regional council was willing to legislate
  visibly for trans people — the official-climate reading, and it sits oddly beside the homicide ranking.

### Tacna (`1875124B58976419670313`)
Direction: worse than national.
- **19 April 2025: Marcia Paucara Cora, 48, a trans woman, was found dead in a room of the hospedaje "Los 5
  Titos"** in Tacna; the necropsy found **death by mechanical asphyxia through compression of the airways**,
  she had been seen entering with a man who then fled and remained unidentified, and **the case is being
  investigated as a possible hate crime** with relatives demanding justice — reported by the independent
  human-rights outlet Wayka. A border city's first documented such case, in a small unit. — https://wayka.pe/tacna-investigan-el-asesinato-de-una-mujer-trans-como-posible-crimen-de-odio/ · https://trome.com/actualidad/nacional/tacna-mujer-trans-fue-hallada-muerta-tras-ingresar-a-hostal-con-sospechoso-que-luego-huyo-video-noticia/

### Lambayeque (`1875124B61751373376494`)
Direction: mixed — administrative-capacity note only.
- A **Regional Management Resolution (R.G.R. N°000757-2024-GR-LAMB)** shows the regional government
  regulating education-administration competences via the Dirección Regional de Educación — included as
  evidence that the education levers where "gender ideology" fights are conducted sit with regional
  governments. No trans-specific Lambayeque incident was found. — https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/6498737/5670813-resolucion-gerencial-regional-n-000757-2024-gr-lamb.pdf

### National frame (bounds all 26 units) — this is where Peru's real movement is
- **Observatorio de Derechos TLGBI of Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia: 54 killings of TLGBI people and
  170 rights-violation cases between 2020 and 2023**, with **trans women the most affected group**, and hate
  crimes rising. — https://agenciapresentes.org/2024/07/22/peru-aumentaron-los-crimenes-de-odio-y-las-mujeres-trans-son-las-mas-afectadas/
- **Seven transfeminicides recorded by 15 February 2025** (Red Trans Perú reported **four trans women killed
  by pimping mafias**; the portal Conexión Vida counted seven), triggering a **national march against hate
  crimes**. — https://www.nodal.am/2025/02/peru-organizaciones-denuncian-que-hasta-el-15-de-febrero-se-registraron-siete-transfeminicidios-y-exigen-justicia/
- **There is still no hate-crime statute** covering sexual orientation or gender identity: the Public
  Ministry's own analysis notes the violence "does not receive condemnation because there is no law
  punishing hate crimes against this community" — so a killing is charged as homicide with no aggravation,
  everywhere equally. — https://www.infobae.com/lgbt/2022/09/28/88-miembros-de-la-comunidad-lgbt-murieron-por-delitos-dolosos-y-justicia-de-peru-no-castiga-crimenes-por-falta-de-normativa/
- **The Inter-American system is active against Peru**: the Corte IDH judgments in **Azul Rojas Marín** and
  **Olivera Fuentes** established Peruvian state responsibility for violence against LGBTI people, including
  torture/rape by police in Rojas Marín's case — the legal backdrop that makes police abuse a known,
  adjudicated pattern rather than an allegation. — https://idehpucp.pucp.edu.pe/boletin-eventos/la-situacion-de-violencia-y-discriminacion-contra-la-poblacion-lgbti-en-el-peru-apuntes-a-proposito-de-los-casos-azul-rojas-marin-y-olivera-fuentes/
- **Current regression is national, not regional:** the **Constitutional Court's Plenary Judgment 228/2024**
  addressed the absence of an administrative procedure for changing given names and sex markers for trans
  and intersex people (invoking IACHR Advisory Opinion OC-24/17); on **19 November 2025 Congress approved
  bill 8731/2024** which UN human-rights experts warned (Geneva, 22 December 2025) would **delete the concept
  of "gender" from key public policies** and constitute a serious rollback; and **bill 12285/2025-CR**
  (MP María Jáuregui, Renovación Popular) proposes to **criminalise with imprisonment those who accompany
  the gender-identity affirmation of minors**. — https://www.tc.gob.pe/jurisprudencia/2024/02563-2021-AA.pdf · https://www.ohchr.org/es/press-releases/2025/12/peru-bill-eliminating-concept-gender-threatens-progress-human-rights-warn-un · https://wayka.pe/proyecto-de-ley-criminaliza-identidad-de-genero-en-infancias-y-adolescencias-trans/
- PromSex/ODC **"Adelante con la Diversidad II"** statistical bulletins track violence against LGBTI people
  and against LGBTI rights defenders by year — the main source if a finer regional cut is later needed. — https://promsex.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/BOLETIN-INFORMATIVO-SOBRE-VIOLENCIA-HACIA-LAS-PERSONAS-LGBTI-Y-HACIA-LAS-PERSONAS-DEFENSORAS-DE-LOS-DERECHOS-LGBTI-2021-5.pdf

## Regions with no sub-national signal
Amazonas, Ancash, Apurímac, Arequipa, Ayacucho, Cajamarca, Cusco, Huancavelica, Huánuco, Ica, Junín,
Moquegua, Pasco, Piura, Puno, San Martín, Ucayali, El Callao, Madre de Dios, and the remaining Amazonian departments —
no region-specific trans law, ordinance, incident series or enforcement finding was located. Note that the
Public Ministry study names six *distritos fiscales* but only three were retrievable from the summary
(Lima, La Libertad, Lima Norte), so additional departments are implicated by that dataset without being
identifiable from the source text available here.

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **Peru's regional signal is city-concentrated: greater Lima and La Libertad (Trujillo) carry the killings
  in the state's own data**, and the 2025 cases reviewed here (Tacna, Independencia) are also urban. Coastal
  cities plus the northern coast are where a visitor's exposure sits; the Amazonian and highland departments
  have neither community visibility nor complaint machinery, so their absence from every dataset is an
  artefact.
- **Pimping mafias are the specific killer identified by the movement itself** (four of the early-2025
  transfeminicides attributed to "mafias proxenetas"), which locates risk in the sex-work economy rather
  than in random street violence — the same structure seen in Brazil and Colombia.
- **Treat "no hate-crime law" as the key national finding**: it means Peru cannot produce regional
  comparisons from official statistics, and simultaneously means a trans person assaulted anywhere gets the
  same thin legal protection. Combined with two adverse Inter-American judgments against the Peruvian state
  (one for police torture/rape), the practical reading is: weak law, precedent for police abuse, and
  functioning civil-society monitoring.
- **Watch the 2025–26 legislative shockwaves** (the 8731/2024 "gender" deletion and the criminalisation of
  accompanying minors' transition). If they become law, Peru's national score moves for all units at once;
  the regional layer documented here is secondary to that.
