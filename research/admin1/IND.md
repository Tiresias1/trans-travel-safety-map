# IND — India · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 36 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
MEDIUM — real sub-national evidence exists for roughly 10 of 36 units, and it is of three kinds:
(a) state **policy machinery** (only 12 states/UTs have constituted a legally mandated transgender
welfare board; Tamil Nadu and Kerala have dedicated state policies), (b) **policing practice at
specific check posts and in specific states** (Tripura, West Bengal, Delhi — a repeated script of
treating gender-nonconforming appearance as evidence of Bangladeshi infiltration), and (c) **event
suppression in one state** (Punjab). What does **not** exist is usable state-level violence data:
official NCRB counts are so broken that they cannot support regional comparison. No state in India
criminalises trans presence.

## Different-legal-system regions
**None in the mandatory-clause sense** — criminal law (IPC/BNS, the Transgender Persons
(Protection of Rights) Act 2019) is on the Concurrent/Union list and applies to all 36 units;
personal-status law is religious, not territorial. Legally distinct configurations that an
assessor should nonetheless know about:
- **Jammu and Kashmīr** (`1811400B42025667129192`) and **Ladākh** (`1811400B23769854471902`) were
  reconstituted from a state into two Union Territories in 2019, losing their own legislature; J&K
  is administered with a Governor and sits under a heavy security regime, with armed-forces
  jurisdiction under AFSPA in parts. No trans-specific J&K evidence was found — the difference is
  the security/checkpoint environment itself, which raises document-and-appearance scrutiny for any
  visitor. — (framework fact; no region-specific trans source located)
- **Puducherry** (`1811400B81659894240990`), **Delhi** (`1811400B23625213884851`) and the other UTs
  have partial or no legislatures, so state-level protective policy depends on the Union
  government — noted by RTI research as a driver of the welfare-board gap. — https://www.thequint.com/gender/the-reality-of-transgender-welfare-boards-in-india-an-rti-investigation
- **Goa** (`1811400B6111924962807`) retains a Portuguese-derived civil-code tradition and is India's
  main foreign-tourism destination, but no divergent trans-related statute or enforcement pattern
  was found in this pass.

## Regions with findings

### Tripura (`1811400B57209440839078`)
Direction: worse than national — the clearest border-post pattern in the country, in which presenting as trans is itself treated as a suspicion trigger.
- 2 Aug 2024: Tripura police apprehended **15 Bangladeshi nationals including seven transgender
  individuals** at the Panisagar check post (North district) on the Agartala–Guwahati highway, in
  separate incidents within 24 hours; state shares an 856 km border. — https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/tripura-news/tripura-police-nabs-15-bangladeshi-nationals-including-7-transgenders
- 2 Aug 2024 (same operation set): seven arrested "including two transgender individuals and four
  **disguised as third-gender**" — the framing that trans presentation is a *disguise* for
  infiltration is explicit in police reporting. — https://northeasttoday.in/northeast/tripura-police-arrest-seven-bangladeshi-nationals-including-two-transgender-individuals-four-disguised-as-third-gender/ · https://neindia.com/seven-bangladeshis-arrested-while-trying-to-enter-india-as-a-transgender/
- 9 Nov 2024: twelve Bangladeshi nationals, "including minors and transgender people", detained in
  coordinated operations across the state in 24 hours. — https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/tripura-news/tripura-12-bangladesh-nationals-including-minors-transgender-people-arrested
- Recorded as a **pattern** (three separate mass sweeps in five months, published counts, named
  check post, state actors) rather than an incident. Tripura also appears in the 12-state list that
  has constituted a transgender welfare board. — https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1779350

### West Bengal (`1811400B32673734874058`)
Direction: worse than national — the same checkpoint script, plus the only state where official hate-crime counts register trans cases at all.
- South Dinajpur police arrested a Bangladeshi transgender person, "Ali Mondal aka Bijli Mondal" of
  Sirajganj district, for allegedly **infiltrating India**, along with the e-rickshaw driver who
  carried her from the border. — https://www.telegraphindia.com/west-bengal/police-in-south-dinajpur-arrest-bangladeshi-transgender-for-allegedly-infiltrating-india/cid/2077943
- West Bengal was one of only three states recording *any* cases under the Transgender Persons Act
  in 2021 (3 of the 7 national cases). — https://www.queerbeat.org/stories/indias-crime-data-would-have-you-believe-that-trans-people-barely-face-any-violence-heres-why-that-doesnt-add-up/
- Has a constituted welfare board. — https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1779350

### Delhi (`1811400B23625213884851`)
Direction: worse than national on profiling; best-served city for a visitor.
- Delhi police apprehended **four allegedly illegal Bangladeshi nationals "disguised as transgender
  people"** from the Azadpur Sabzi Mandi area in northwest Delhi, handed to the FRRO for
  deportation; the official framing linked them to begging at traffic signals. — https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/law-order/3376517-delhi-police-busts-bangladeshi-nationals-posing-as-transgender-individuals
- Same "transgender = disguise/illegal migrant/begging" police logic as Tripura and West Bengal,
  three states apart → the pattern is replicating in the capital. For a foreign visitor this means
  visible gender-nonconformity in public is, in Delhi, an independently sufficient reason for a
  police interaction.
- Delhi is also India's most institutionally visible trans city (hosted the longest-running pride
  marches and the capital's welfare machinery); the two facts coexist.

### Punjab (`1811400B91946361694107`)
Direction: worse than national — the only documented forced cancellation of a Pride parade in this pass.
- **Pride Amritsar cancelled its 27 April 2025 parade at the Rose Garden** after "mounting
  resistance" from Sikh religious organisations including the **Nihang Singh faction and the Akal
  Takht**, the faith's highest temporal authority; the organisers cited opposition from religious
  groups as the reason for cancelling the annual event. Amritsar is the spiritual centre of
  Sikhism, so this is a specific city-state with unusual religious-authority power over public
  assembly. — https://www.washingtonblade.com/2025/04/23/opposition-from-religious-groups-prompts-indian-pride-group-to-cancel-annual-parade/

### Tamil Nādu (`1811400B21167269026319`)
Direction: better than national — the most protective sub-national regime in India, with courts actively confirming it.
- **First Indian state to introduce a transgender welfare policy** (1995) and the home of the
  Thirunangai/Thirunambi administrative categories; a dedicated **Transgender Welfare Board** sits
  inside the Social Welfare & Women Empowerment Department. — https://tg.tnsw.in/webapp/index.aspx
- The Madras High Court on 4 August 2025 (WP 7284 of 2021) noted with approval that the state had
  brought into effect the **Tamil Nadu State Policy for Transgender Persons 2025**, calling it "a
  victory for transgender and intersex persons after their long struggle" and expressly
  commending the government. — https://s3.courtbook.in/2025/08/0Amadras-high-court-commends-tamil-nadus-policy-for-transgender-and-intersex-persons.pdf
- Also the only state where a case under the Transgender Persons Act was recorded in 2020 (1 of 1
  nationally) — i.e. its courts are at least being used. — https://www.queerbeat.org/stories/indias-crime-data-would-have-you-believe-that-trans-people-barely-face-any-violence-heres-why-that-doesnt-add-up/
- Complicating signal (Dec 2025): Hindu transgender devotees in Madurai announced a fast unto death
  over the DMK government's decision on lamp-lighting at the Thirupparankundram hill temple — trans
  people are political actors in Tamil Nadu's temple-administration debates, and the state
  government's temple decisions can generate conflict with them. — https://tfipost.com/2025/12/madurai-transgenders-announce-fast-unto-death-against-dmk-govts-decision-to-not-allow-hindus-to-light-lamp-at-thirupparankundram-hill/

### Kerala (`1811400B45534780755480`)
Direction: better than national.
- **First state in India to adopt a Policy for Transgenders**, with named welfare measures including
  an overseas scholarship scheme for transgender persons, run through the Social Justice Department. — https://www.sjd.kerala.gov.in/beneficiary-info.php?benef_sl=Nw%3D%3D
- One of only three states recording cases under the Transgender Persons Act in 2021 (2 of 7), and a
  welfare board exists. — https://www.queerbeat.org/stories/indias-crime-data-would-have-you-believe-that-trans-people-barely-face-any-violence-heres-why-that-doesnt-add-up/ · https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1779350

### Assam (`1811400B78322693766699`)
Direction: better than national — on paper.
- One of only three states with recorded cases under the Transgender Persons Act in 2021 (2 of 7). — https://www.queerbeat.org/stories/indias-crime-data-would-have-you-believe-that-trans-people-barely-face-any-violence-heres-why-that-doesnt-add-up/
- Part of the Northeast border belt where the infiltration/Bangladeshi-detention script also operates
  (the Panisagar raids run along the Assam–Agartala highway). No Assam-specific case was found.

### Uttar Pradesh (`1811400B11231190780494`)
Direction: mixed — a lethal public-religion counterweight.
- **Kumbh Mela, Prayagraj (Jan–Feb 2025):** the Kinnar Akhara — the trans/hijra monastic order —
  fielded a **Mahamandaleshwar (one of its highest-ranking monks)** leading ceremonial bathing at
  the world's largest religious gathering, i.e. the most trans-visible public space in India is in
  Uttar Pradesh. A trans visitor at the Kumbh is inside an institutionally recognised hierarchy,
  not an underground. — https://www.ipsnews.net/2025/02/worlds-largest-religious-gathering-becomes-trans-inclusive-despite-controversies/
- Counterweight: Kanpur, 10 Aug 2025 — a transgender woman, Kajal, 25, and her 12-year-old adopted
  brother were strangled by intruders in Yogendra Vihar during a robbery; the suspect died by
  suicide. **Recorded as one case only** — no UP series was found, so it does not support a regional
  finding on its own. — https://www.tdor2.translivesmatter.info/reports/2025/08/10/kajal_kanpur-uttar-pradesh-india_0340e725
- UP is among the 12 states/UTs with a welfare board. — https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1779350

### Maharashtra (`1811400B15614733245507`) and Chhattīsgarh (`1811400B70654311394581`)
Direction: worse than national on public-festival access screening (religion-based, which doubles as an outing mechanism).
- Maharashtra: after the VHP publicly urged garba organisers **not to allow non-Hindus** and to check
  **Aadhaar cards** for entry, a political row erupted; in Nagpur organisers imposed Aadhaar
  verification, security checks with "male and female" boundaries and a ritual bow before a Varaha
  avatar as entry conditions. — https://scroll.in/latest/1086810/maharashtra-row-erupts-after-vhp-urges-garba-organisers-to-not-allow-entry-of-non-hindus · https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/aadhaar-tilak-bow-before-varaha-avatar-tighter-rules-for-garba-in-nagpur-9340790
- Chhattisgarh: in Bastar district, VHP and Bajrang Dal members objected to non-Hindu sponsors and
  participants at garba events, claiming damage to "cultural purity". — https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/raipur/garba-nights-in-bastar-face-objection-vhp-bajrang-dal-oppose-non-hindu-sponsors-their-participation/articleshow/124076442.cms
- Why this counts here: an ID-checked, religion-screened entry gate at a public festival is precisely
  the mechanism by which a gender-nonconforming visitor is examined and outed by authorities who are
  not supposed to be involved — and it is happening in named states, not nationwide.
- Chhattisgarh single-case note: in November 2024 a transgender woman known as Kajal/"Coca Cola" was
  shot and killed in an abandoned quarry in a **Rs 12 lakh contract killing** motivated by rival
  claims to a community leadership seat ("Guru ki Gaddi"). Reported as one incident with no second
  sourced instance found in this pass — recorded only because contract killings over guru-succession
  recur in central India and a scorer may meet the pattern elsewhere. — https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/raipur/transgender-rivalry-leads-to-gruesome-murder-for-rs-12-lakh-in-chhattisgarh/articleshow/115532210.cms

### The welfare-board gradient (all 36 units)
Direction: mixed — the closest thing India has to a per-state official-will index.
- **Only 12 of the states/UTs have constituted the transgender welfare board that section 10(1) of
  the Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Rules, 2020 requires**: Tamil Nadu, Kerala,
  Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Chandigarh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Tripura
  and Mizoram. — https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1779350
- **19 states and UTs still have no welfare body**, of which four are Union Territories controlled by
  the Centre and **eight are states where the BJP is in power or in the ruling alliance**; an RTI
  investigation further found that most of the boards that do exist are "rudderless", without a
  policy framework, compliance mechanism or real power. — https://www.thequint.com/gender/the-reality-of-transgender-welfare-boards-in-india-an-rti-investigation
- So the divergence for a visitor is thin: even the "good" states mostly have non-functioning bodies.
  But the presence of a board does mark which governments were willing to be seen legislating.

## Regions with no sub-national signal

Goa, Himāchal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmīr, Ladākh, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha,

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **The single most important finding is a measurement failure, and it should not be converted into
  reassurance.** Under the Transgender Persons Act, the NCRB recorded just **20 complaints
  nationwide across 2019–2023** (2020: 1 case, Tamil Nadu; 2021: 7 cases in Assam, Kerala and West
  Bengal only). In the 2022 Crimes in India report, **9 of 29,356 murder victims were trans**, **1 of
  110,140 adult kidnap victims**, and **no** trans rape, sexual-assault or mob-lynching cases at all;
  2021 recorded 236 trans victims of any crime. Trans Murder Monitoring logged 102 Indian trans
  killings 2008–2021. India therefore cannot be regionalised from official violence data, and the
  official figure is evidence about police registration, not about safety. — https://www.queerbeat.org/stories/indias-crime-data-would-have-you-believe-that-trans-people-barely-face-any-violence-heres-why-that-doesnt-add-up/ · https://www.washingtonblade.com/2024/09/24/anti-transgender-discrimination-violence-in-india-persists/ · https://behanbox.com/2023/11/20/why-lives-and-deaths-of-trans-persons-remain-underreported-undocumented/ ·
- **Legal uniformity is the honest headline:** no Indian state criminalises transgender people or
  gender expression; section 377 was read down in 2018 and the 2019 Act (whatever its faults) is a
  national protection statute. The state-level variation that exists is in *implementation
  machinery*, *policing culture at borders/checkpoints*, and *whether religious authorities can veto
  public assembly* (Punjab, and to a lesser degree temple-administration states).
- **The border/checkpoint pattern is the strongest visitor-specific signal in this file** and it runs
  across Tripura, West Bengal and Delhi. It operates on foreigners and on Indians alike, and it
  treats gender-nonconforming appearance as a suspicion category. A trans visitor crossing into
  north-east India by land, or being rounded up while begging/visible in Delhi, is the realistic
  harm scenario.
- **City-vs-region matters far more than state-vs-state in India**: the historically protective,
  community-institutionalised centres (Chennai/Bangalore/Mumbai/Kolkata/Delhi, and the hijra
  chela-guru networks around them) behave very differently from small towns, and Tamil Nadu/Kerala's
  policies land differently in Kochi or Chennai versus rural Bihar.
- Festival calendars are a real exposure variable for a visitor: Kumbh (Prayagraj, UP) is
  trans-affirming at institutional level; Navratri/garba (Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat)
  increasingly has religion-screened entry; Amritsar (Punjab) has a religious authority that killed
  its Pride. An assessor with month-of-travel data could score these as seasonal rather than purely
  regional.
