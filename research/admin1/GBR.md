# GBR — United Kingdom · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 4 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
RICH — all four units have real, distinct, sourced sub-national evidence, and the UK is unusual in
that the divergence is *legislative and prosecutorial*, not merely attitudinal: Scotland has a
blocked self-ID act, a unique statutory transgender hate-crime aggravation, and a custody policy
just voided by its own court; Wales runs a protection-first education and action-plan approach;
Northern Ireland's minister pulled trans school guidance and its hate-crime bill excludes trans
people; England (with Wales and Scotland) now lives under a statutory single-sex-services code.
Only 4 units exist, so this file covers 100% of them.

## Different-legal-system regions
**Scotland** (`14339913B23556801435424`) is the one unit with a genuinely separate legal system —
Scots law, its own courts (Court of Session / High Court), its own statute book and its own
prosecution service (COPFS), plus a devolved parliament. Two consequences visible to a visitor:
- Scotland's Parliament passed the **Gender Recognition Reform (Scotland) Bill** in December 2022 by a two-thirds majority, and it was **blocked on 17 January 2023 by the first-ever Section 35 Order** under the Scotland Act 1998; the Court of Session held the block lawful (8 Dec 2023). Scotland therefore has a *different, unenacted* gender-recognition law than the rest of the UK. — https://www.parliament.scot/bills-and-laws/bills/s6/gender-recognition-reform-scotland-bill · https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-9705/ · https://www.scotcourts.gov.uk/media/lkkd0t1r/court-of-session-petition-by-the-scottish-ministers-for-judicial-review-of-the-gender-recognition-reform-scotland-bill-prohibition-on-submission-for-royal-assent-order-2023-made-and-laid-before-the-uk-parl.pdf
- **The Hate Crime and Public Order (Scotland) Act 2021** (in force 1 April 2024) created a specific **"transgender identity" hate-crime aggravation that has no equivalent in England or Wales**, so Scottish prosecutors charge and report anti-trans offences as a distinct strand while English/Welsh law does not recognise gender identity as a hate-crime strand at all. — https://www.copfs.gov.uk/publications/hate-crime-in-scotland-2025-26/html/ · https://www.gov.scot/publications/hate-crimes-recorded-police-scotland-2024-25/ · https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/hate-crime-england-and-wales-year-ending-march-2025/hate-crime-england-and-wales-year-ending-march-2025
- Northern Ireland is also a separate legal jurisdiction (own courts, own assembly, PSNI, and its own recorded "transgender identity" hate strand) but does **not** have its own criminal-law divergence on trans status — see its entry.

## Regions with findings

### Scotland (`14339913B23556801435424`)
Direction: mixed — strongest formal charging framework for anti-trans hate in the UK, but its custody policy was just ruled unlawful and its self-ID law was vetoed by Westminster.
- **Unique enforcement machinery:** COPFS reported **104 charges with a transgender-identity aggravation in 2025-26, up 21% on 86 the year before — the highest number since the aggravation was created in 2010.** Police Scotland recorded 8,538 hate crimes in 2024-25, the first full year of the 2021 Act. Rising *recorded* anti-trans charging is double-edged: it evidences both more hostility surfacing and an institution that takes it seriously. — https://www.copfs.gov.uk/publications/hate-crime-in-scotland-2025-26/html/ · https://www.copfs.gov.uk/publications/hate-crime-in-scotland-2024-25/html/ · https://www.gov.scot/publications/hate-crimes-recorded-police-scotland-2024-25/
- **Custody divergence, reversed:** the Scottish Prison Service's Policy for the Management of Transgender People in Custody (Feb 2024) allowed gender-identity-based placement; on 19 June 2026 Lady Ross in the Court of Session ruled that guidance **unlawful**, holding sex segregation in Scottish prisons must follow biological sex; First Minister John Swinney confirmed the government would not appeal, and trans women were **moved out of women's prisons**. — https://judiciary.scot/home/sentences-judgments/judgments/2026/06/19/petition-of-for-women-scotland-for-judicial-review · https://scotcourts.gov.uk/media/pnbpzgex/2026csoh59-petition-of-for-women-scotland-for-judicial-review.pdf · https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cx2jndnjwexo · https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cz7502eq5lro ·
- **Vetoed expansion of rights:** the Gender Recognition Reform Bill passed by two-thirds of the Parliament in Dec 2022 and was blocked in Jan 2023; it remains in force-as-blocked and "paused" after the 2025 Supreme Court judgment rather than being revived. A visitor in Scotland therefore has no self-ID route that England lacks — but also a political narrative in which Scotland's own institution was overridden. — https://www.parliament.scot/bills-and-laws/bills/s6/gender-recognition-reform-scotland-bill · https://murrayblackburnmackenzie.org/2025/04/14/uk-supreme-court-case-where-could-the-decision-leave-the-gender-recognition-reform-scotland-bill/

### England (`14339913B95766344400054`)
Direction: worse than the pre-2025 position, and worse than Wales on official posture.
- **Statutory single-sex code in force:** the EHRC's updated Code of Practice for Services, Public Functions and Associations was laid on 21 May 2026 and **came into force on 5 August 2026** as statutory guidance, replacing the 2011 code. It states that single-sex spaces open to the public — toilets, gym changing rooms, hospital wards, rape-crises centres — should be organised **by biological sex**, and that trans people should use gender-neutral facilities or those matching their birth sex. It is legally binding guidance for service providers across England, Wales and Scotland, but it is administered in England and it is the clearest "you may be refused" signal on the map. — https://www.dacbeachcroft.com/it/What-we-think/Guidance-on-single-sex-spaces-updated-EHRC-Code-of-Practice · https://www.brabners.com/insights/sport/ehrc-code-of-practice-significant-guidance-on-single-sex-services-following-for-women-scotland · https://www.gov.uk/government/news/organisations-receive-clear-accessible-guidance-on-how-to-implement-equality-law-in-updated-draft-code-of-practice · https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c9983lv4dlno
- **The legal predicate was itself a Scotland-originated case:** For Women Scotland Ltd v The Scottish Ministers (UKSC, 16 April 2025) held that "sex" and "woman" in the Equality Act 2010 mean biological/registered sex, and that a Gender Recognition Certificate does not change legal sex for Equality Act purposes — the reasoning behind the code and applied UK-wide. — https://www.equalityhumanrights.com/our-work/uk-supreme-court-ruling-meaning-sex-equality-act-our-work · https://cdn.skai.gr/sites/default/files/attachments/2025-04/uksc_2024_0042_judgment_aea6c48cee.pdf
- **No trans strand in hate-crime law:** England and Wales recorded 115,990 hate crimes in the year to March 2025 across five monitored strands — race, religion, sexual orientation, disability, transgender identity is **not** one of the strands recorded separately in the England & Wales collection (unlike Scotland and Northern Ireland), so anti-trans offending in England is statistically invisible in official data. — https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/hate-crime-england-and-wales-year-ending-march-2025/hate-crime-england-and-wales-year-ending-march-2025

### Wales (`14339913B89763821047858`)
Direction: better than England on official posture, same binding facility rules.
- Runs an **LGBTQ+ Action Plan** as official programme, with a standing update page responding to the For Women Scotland judgment (19 December 2025) and specific guidance for young people — i.e. a devolved administration publicly maintaining LGBTQ+ inclusion policy after the ruling. — https://www.gov.wales/sites/default/files/publications/2026-01/lgbtq-action-plan-for-wales-young-people.pdf
- **Statutory school guidance covering gender reassignment:** the Welsh Government's "Rights, Respect, Equality" statutory guidance to schools (consultation May 2025) obliges schools to challenge bullying and harassment including on sex, gender reassignment and sexual orientation grounds, with **recording and monitoring** of incidents and a parental escalation route; a dedicated **national guidance for schools on supporting trans children** was in development through 2025-26, evidenced by a Wales Centre for Public Policy rapid evidence review and by FOI releases (28 May 2026) on the consultation work. — https://www.gov.wales/sites/default/files/consultations/2025-05/rights-respect-equality-statutory-guidance.pdf · https://wcpp.org.uk/report/rapid-evidence-review-to-inform-guidance-for-schools-to-support-trans-young-people/ · https://www.gov.wales/atisn26923
- Caveat: the EHRC code applies in Wales too, so single-sex service exclusion is equally lawful there — the divergence is in *state posture* (guidance that protects inclusion) rather than in what a facility manager may do.

### Northern Ireland (`14339913B6930312138089`)
Direction: worse than national — active removal of protections, an excluded hate-crime strand, and a documented paramilitary-flavoured attack history.
- **School guidance withdrawn:** Education Minister Paul Givan ordered the Education Authority's 2019 transgender-pupils guidance **removed from the website** and reviewed after the FWS ruling, calling it "unlawful and flawed" and a product of "activist campaigns"; other parties publicly pressed for reinstatement and for disclosure of the legal advice. The withdrawn guidance had expressly covered single-sex spaces such as changing rooms and toilets. — https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c749l179g12o · https://app.newsletter.co.uk/story/5306488/content.html · https://www.standard.co.uk/news/uk/peter-martin-department-of-education-supreme-court-belfast-transgender-b1247030.html
- **Trans people left out of new hate-crime law:** the Justice Minister (Naomi Long) sought to include transgender people as a "protected" group in sentencing/hate-crime reform; the DUP was reported to be **stalling** the measure. — https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c23ee11v8nlo
- Official statistics nevertheless track the strand: PSNI/NISRA report a separate "transgender identity" category quarterly, with **both transgender-identity incidents and crimes increasing** (from 61 to a higher figure) in the year to September 2025, alongside a series running back to 2004/05. — https://www.nisra.gov.uk/news/over-2000-race-hate-incidents-recorded-police-12-months · https://www.psni.police.uk/about-us/our-publications-and-reports/official-statistics/hate-motivation-statistics · https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/hate-incidents-and-crimes-in-northern-ireland-period-ending-31-december-2025
- **Attack pattern with a paramilitary character (Derry/Londonderry and Belfast):** a pipe bomb was thrown through the window of the home of a 27-year-old trans woman, Rachael Keys, in the Lincoln Court area of Derry while she watched television, described by police as a "sectarian hate crime", forcing her to move out; PSNI's own long-run series and the Rainbow Project report an average of ~290 anti-LGB&T hate incidents/crimes per year in NI (2013–2017) with stagnating reporting; a gay man was hospitalised after a Belfast city-centre assault in December 2019. Counted as a series rather than as one event, and with the police "sectarian" framing implicating organised actors. — https://www.mirror.co.uk/news/uk-news/exploding-pipe-bomb-thrown-through-6574568 · https://www.rainbow-project.org/hatecrimeni/ · https://www.thepinknews.com/2019/12/10/belfast-homophobic-attack-head-injuries-kissing-hospitalised/
- Counter-signal: health funding for gender identity services was defended publicly by the Health Minister (Mike Nesbitt, £800k+ uplift) against DUP/TUV criticism, so the executive is split rather than uniformly hostile. — https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cgqn9k28z3go

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **Much of the harshest current change is UK-wide, not English.** The For Women Scotland judgment (April 2025) binds all four nations, and the EHRC statutory code covers **England, Scotland and Wales**. Only two things are genuinely nation-specific: (a) Scotland's separate hate-crime aggravation and its voided prison guidance; (b) Northern Ireland's withdrawn school guidance and stalled hate-crime inclusion; (c) Wales's action plan and school guidance. Assessors should not double-count the EHRC code as an "England" regional penalty — it is the national floor.
- **London vs the rest of England** is the strongest practical gradient inside England (visible communities, dedicated services, policing hate-crime units) but no sourced sub-England region-level trans evidence was found in this pass, and the boundary data stops at England as a whole.
- **Statutory guidance is not a criminal ban.** Nobody is arrested in England for presenting in a toilet; the risk mechanism is *service refusal and ejection by private/third-party staff*, legitimised by the code. That is a different risk shape from the US state criminal bans and should be scored as such.
- **Recording asymmetry is itself a finding**: Scotland and Northern Ireland count anti-trans offences as a distinct strand; England and Wales do not. Absence of English statistics is therefore evidence about measurement, not about safety — do not read it as low incidence.
- Cross-reference: the national anchor for GBR in research/anchors.md sits at 0.74 (Reduced Risk) with the statutory biological-sex facilities code noted; the Feb/July 2026 code commencement (5 Aug 2026) postdates that anchor and should be treated as a deterioration signal for all four units.
