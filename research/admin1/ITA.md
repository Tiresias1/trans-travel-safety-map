# ITA — Italy · ADM1 research
Researched: 2026-09-17 · Units in boundary data: 5 usable, 0 excluded

## Richness classification
MEDIUM — all five units carry at least one sourced, region-specific finding, and there is an actual
**region-by-region** Italian study of anti-LGBT violence naming the six highest-victim regions. But
the boundary units are **NUTS-1 macro-regions** (Nord-Ovest, Nord-Est, Centro, Sud, Isole), far
coarser than the Italian regions the evidence is about: everything found is at city/province level
inside these five boxes, so the map resolution is too low to carry Italy's real variation. Recorded
as MEDIUM with a resolution warning rather than inflated.

## Different-legal-system regions
**None.** No Italian region has criminal or moral jurisdiction; the penal code, the 1982 rectification
law (L. 164/82) and public-security law are national. Two real asymmetries worth recording:
- **Regions with special statute** (Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol, Valle d'Aosta, Friuli-Venezia
  Giulia, Sicilia, Sardegna) have expanded legislative competence — the first two in autonomous
  provinces. None used it on gender-related matters in any source found. Sicilia and Sardegna sit
  inside the **Isole** unit here.
- **Municipal and regional law-making on equality policy is real and divergent**: Milano and Torino
  have adopted official alias-name registers, and Veneto/Lombardy passed "natural family" motions.
  These are sub-ADM1 and not a different legal system, but they are the layer where Italy's
  variation actually lives.

## Regions with findings

### Nord-Ovest (`72843720B99597932318450`)
Direction: mixed — Italy's highest-victim regions and its most protective municipal measures, in the same box.
- **Lombardy and Piedmont are two of the six Italian regions with the highest number of anti-LGBT
  victims** in the Progetto Gionata region-by-region study covering 2012–2025 (the others being
  Lazio, Toscana, Campania, Puglia). — https://www.gionata.org/omotransfobia-in-italia_2012_2025/
- **Piedmont, Alessandria, via De Amicis:** a 28-year-old Peruvian transgender woman was found dead in
  her flat on 9 October 2025; on 22 December 2025 the Carabinieri charged and jailed two homeless men
  aged 22 and 25 already imprisoned for a **series of violent robberies against vulnerable people**,
  linking them to the killing via wiretaps and a confession. Recorded as a **positive institutional
  response** (scientific section of the Carabinieri, cooperating prosecutor, prompt charges) inside a
  documented robbery pattern targeting fragile people. — https://www.torinocronaca.it/news/cronaca/587654/omicidio-in-via-de-amicis-ad-alessandria-intercettazioni-e-confessioni-portano-a-due-indagati.html · https://radiogold.it/news-alessandria/424620-alessandria-omicidio-via-de-amicis-arrestao-giovani-22-25-anni-carabinieri/ · https://www.farodiroma.it/alessandria-rapine-e-violenze-contro-i-piu-fragili-omicidio-di-via-de-amicis-in-carcere-due-ventenni-accusati-di-aver-ucciso-una-donna-trans-r-bobbio/
- **Lombardy, Milano:** the city council approved the **first "registro di genere" in Italy** (motion
  by Monica Romano, the first transgender woman elected to the council, 2022) — chosen name on civic
  and municipal documents, transport subscriptions and staff badges. Torino introduced **"Identità
  Alias"** for the comune and the public libraries. These are the practical tools that decide whether a
  gender-nonconforming visitor is humiliated at a ticket window or a library desk. — https://www.lifegate.it/milano-registro-di-genere-italia-transgender · https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2022/07/31/a-milano-il-registro-di-genere-per-transgender-che-sia-dispirazione-per-una-legge-nazionale/6666109/ · https://www.comune.torino.it/schede-informative/identita-alias · https://www.comune.torino.it/novita/comunicati/torino-approvate-linee-guida-per-lidentita-alias-comune-nelle-biblioteche
- **Lombardy, Brianza Pride (Arcore, 27 September 2025):** right-wing militants were seen giving
  **Roman salutes** along the parade route; the Digos moved on the organisers pre-emptively and
  reported the spark "struck out at birth". — https://www.mbnews.it/2025/09/saluti-romani-al-brianza-pride-e-la-digos-stronca-sul-nascere-la-scintilla/
- **Lombardy, official posture:** the regional council approved (1 July 2015, majority, opposition
  walked out) a Lega-sponsored motion "in support of the natural family" and against "gender
  ideology", and the region's earlier resolutions rejected the WHO Regional Office's *Standards for
  Sexuality Education in Europe*. Dated, but the durable regional political signature. — https://www.giuristiperlavita.org/joomla/scrittideisoci/331-lombardia-il-consiglio-regionale-approva-mozione-a-sostegno-della-famiglia-naturale · https://bur.regione.veneto.it/BurvServices/pubblica/DettaglioDgr.aspx?id=287656

### Centro (`72843720B863019116732`)
Direction: mixed — the most protective court ruling in Italy, and an assault on a Pride march.
- **Emilia-Romagna, Bologna, January 2025:** a first-conviction judgment recognised the
  **"transphobic motive"** behind what the judge called an "extreme and unheard-of" violence — a
  punitive rape of a trans sex worker — sentencing and awarding damages, and **for the first time
  admitting the Movimento Identità Trans (Mit) as civil party**. This is the strongest good-response
  evidence found anywhere in this country file. — https://www.dire.it/24-01-2025/1118174-fu-stupro-punitivo-donna-trans-risarcito-anche-movimento-che-aiuta-sex-worker/
- **Marche, Ascoli Piceno, June 2025:** activist Raniero Bertone was **assaulted during the Piceno
  Pride** march between the public gardens and Piazza Arringo; his rainbow flag bearing a six-pointed
  star was seized and thrown away. — https://www.corriereadriatico.it/ascoli_piceno/ascoli_piceno_pride_aggressione_raniero_bertone_bandiera_sottratta_gettata_via_simbolo_di_israele_motivi-8892138.html
- **Lazio and Toscana** are two of the six highest-victim regions in the Gionata regional study; Rome
  and Naples Prides were separately attacked politically over the Middle East conflict in June 2025. — https://www.gionata.org/omotransfobia-in-italia_2012_2025/ · https://www.arcigay.it/en/articoli/arcigay-e-antagonisti-attaccano-il-roma-pride-e-il-napoli-pride-uso-strumentale-della-crisi-in-medio-oriente/

### Nord-Est (`72843720B59566147937015`)
Direction: worse than national on official climate.
- **Veneto:** the regional council approved motion no. 340 (3 July, Venice, 178th public session)
  backing "those who fight in defence of the natural family", preceded by motion no. 270 (July 2014)
  proposing a "Feast of the Family"; the regional government institutionalised a **"Festa della
  Famiglia Naturale"** on the last school day before the Christmas holidays and formally **rejected
  the aims and content of the WHO Regional Office's *Standards for Sexuality Education in Europe*** —
  an official regional act of hostility to sex education, published in the region's official bulletin. — https://www.pianetamilk.it/regione-veneto-approvata-mozione-pro-famiglia-naturale/ · https://www.circolopink.it/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/veneto_risoluzione_famiglia.pdf · https://bur.regione.veneto.it/BurvServices/pubblica/DettaglioDgr.aspx?id=287656
- **Veneto, Venezia (2025):** a gay student was **stalked by three fascists**, reported in the same
  press coverage that carried the Catania beating — i.e. an ideological-mob pattern reaching the
  north-east too. — https://www.biccy.it/catania-aggressione-omofoba-in-un-fast-food/
- Note that Veneto and Trentino-Alto Adige are also the regions where tourism crosses the Brenner;
  no region-specific trans-visitor incident data exists.

### Sud (`72843720B88210905209841`)
Direction: worse than national.
- **Campania and Puglia** are two of the six highest-victim regions in the Gionata regional study —
  the southern pairing. — https://www.gionata.org/omotransfobia-in-italia_2012_2025/
- **Campania, Scampia (Naples), March 2025:** two young men on a scooter stopped, insulted and
  assaulted a young man, shouting homophobic slurs, per ILGA-Europe's country review — part of what
  that review calls "a troubling increase in the number **and geographic spread** of bias-motivated
  violence against LGBTI people in Italy in 2024, with incidents reported almost monthly". — https://www.ilga-europe.org/files/uploads/2025/02/Annual-Review-2025-Italy.pdf
- **Campania, Torre Annunziata, 13 October 2025:** Francesco Caraviello, an Arcigay Napoli activist and
  president of Discovery Queer, was subjected to a verbal attack **culminating in threats of stabbing**
  by two young men at the EAV railway station. — https://www.ilgazzettinovesuviano.com/2025/10/13/torre-annunziata-grave-episodio-di-omofobia-minacce-di-accoltellamento-contro-giovane-attivista-lgbtqia/
- **Calabria, Reggio Calabria, 28 May 2024:** a person was kidnapped, subjected to violence and robbed
  in the city centre; Arcigay publicly **commended the squadra mobile** for the swift intervention
  while demanding an anti-homophobia law — a good-response data point in the region with the weakest
  institutional reputation. — https://www.arcigay.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ARCIGAY-Report-omolesbobitransfobia-2025.-pdf.pdf
- **Puglia, Taranto** and Basilicata's Termoli featured in the 2025 Onda Pride circuit, i.e. public
  LGBTQ presence continues in the south; Arcigay's own framing on the same day was that "in Italy,
  vigilantes beat LGBTQIA+ people". — https://www.arcigay.it/en/comunicati/onda-pride-sette-cortei-oggi-ad-alba-catania-favignana-lodi-napoli-taranto-e-terni-piazzoni-arcigay-solidali-alla-mamma-aggredita-a-catania-il-governo-meloni-ci-espone-alla-violenz/ · https://www.arcigay.it/en/comunicati/onda-pride-oggi-tre-cortei-a-rimini-termoli-e-trapani-piazzoni-arcigay-sicurezza-in-italia-i-vigilantes-picchiano-le-persone-lgbtqia/

### Isole (`72843720B81376294924159`)
Direction: worse than national.
- **Sicily, Catania, 26 April 2025:** three young men were **beaten in a fast-food restaurant at ~5 a.m.
  because they were gay**; the prosecutor's office reconstructed the sequence (insults and threats, then
  the physical assault) and video circulated publicly in August 2025; the victims were hospitalised. — https://tg.la7.it/cronaca/aggressione-omofoba-catania-fast-food-giovani-gay-video-06-08-2025-242285 · https://www.biccy.it/catania-aggressione-omofoba-in-un-fast-food/
- **Sicily, Catania (July 2025):** a mother was attacked in Catania in an incident Arcigay named in a
  national statement, claiming "the Meloni government is exposing us to violence". — https://www.arcigay.it/en/comunicati/onda-pride-sette-cortei-oggi-ad-alba-catania-favignana-lodi-napoli-taranto-e-terni-piazzoni-arcigay-solidali-alla-mamma-aggredita-a-catania-il-governo-meloni-ci-espone-alla-violenz/
- **Sicily, sustained pattern:** a June 2026 survey of Sicilian coverage describes **rising homophobic
  bullying and violence against the LGBTQIA+ community alongside suicides**, listing multiple cases;

## Regions with no sub-national signal
None of the five units is empty, but within-unit coverage is uneven: **Sardegna** (inside Isole),
**Friuli-Venezia Giulia and Trentino-Alto Adige** (inside Nord-Est), **Umbria and Marche outside

## Urban / rural and general notes
- **The national dataset is the better evidence base than anything regional.** Arcigay's May 2025
  report catalogued **110 hate-crime episodes between May 2024 and May 2025**, "distributed
  capillarily across the whole national territory, with **greater concentration in large cities**";
  Progetto Gionata's parallel monitoring counted **105 episodes and 154 victims** in May 2024–April
  2025, above the twelve-year average; the 2024 edition counted 149 reported cases, **three homicides
  and one suicide**, i.e. one violence every two days. Arcigay's headline is that the **"branco"**
  (mob/group attack) is spreading and is "often of ideological inspiration". — https://www.imgpress.it/attualita/omolesbobitransfobia-110-storie-nel-report-2025-di-arcigay/ · https://www.gionata.org/rapporto-2025-sullomotransfobia-in-italia/ · https://www.arcigay.it/comunicati/omolesbobitransfobia-lallarme-di-arcigay-ogni-due-giorni-una-violenza-4-morti-negli-ultimi-dodici-mesi/
- **The visitor-specific mechanism is named in the Italian data: dating-app luring for beating or
  extortion** ("adescamenti sulle dating app a scopo pestaggio o estorsione") was flagged by Arcigay as
  a distinct rising category. This is the single most transferable warning for a trans visitor to
  Italy, and it is national, not regional. — https://www.arcigay.it/comunicati/omolesbobitransfobia-lallarme-di-arcigay-ogni-due-giorni-una-violenza-4-morti-negli-ultimi-dodici-mesi/
- **Transfobia is the most severe slice of Italian hate crime**: over twelve years of monitoring, the
  transfobic share of recorded incidents never fell below 10%, peaking at 19.5% (2017) and 13.6% in the
  most recent year analysed. Sex workers are the recurring victim profile (Bologna, Alessandria,
  Reggio Calabria). — https://www.gionata.org/la-transfobia-in-italia-la-piu-atroce-e-diffusa-discriminazione-contro-le-persone-transgender/ · https://www.gionata.org/omotransfobia-in-italia_2012_2025/
- **Resolution problem, stated plainly for the scorer:** Italy's real sub-national differences are at
  *regione* (20) and *comune* level — Milano/Torino registers vs Veneto's family motions vs Catania's
  street violence. Collapsing to 5 macro-regions means, e.g., that Milano's protective register and
  the Brianza Roman salutes land in the same unit, and that Lazio and Toscana absorb everything for
  central Italy. If a finer boundary layer is available, this file's findings should be re-cut to
  regione.
- **Institutional response is the discriminator here, not law.** Italy has no anti-hate-crime statute
  covering sexual orientation/gender identity in force (Rainbow Map rank 35 of 49), yet courts have
  produced a "transphobic motive" conviction with damages (Bologna) and police have produced fast
  charges (Alessandria, Reggio Calabria). Weight the good-response evidence; do not read the legal
  gap as police inaction everywhere.
